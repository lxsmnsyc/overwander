# The fifth generation's moves

**92 new moves** from the fifth generation, taught to every pokemon already in
the game, and the **three starters** that came with them.

The rest of that generation arrives family by family.

## 92 new moves

From Hone Claws to Fusion Bolt, with the four **Drives** that set what type a
Techno Blast comes out as: Douse, Shock, Burn and Chill.

- **Shell Smash, Quiver Dance, Coil, Shift Gear and Work Up** join the setup
  moves, and **Cotton Guard** and **Autotomize** answer them.
- **Scald, Hurricane, Hex, Foul Play, Acrobatics, Wild Charge, Drill Run and
  Horn Leech** fill out what a pokemon can throw.
- **Sacred Sword, Secret Sword, Psystrike, Bolt Strike, Blue Flare, Glaciate,
  V-create, Relic Song and Techno Blast** are here for the pokemon they belong
  to, ahead of the pokemon themselves.

### Moves that read a real-time fight

- **Round and Retaliate** double when a teammate sang a Round or fell in the
  last 2 seconds, and **Fusion Flare and Fusion Bolt** double for each other.
- **Echoed Voice** lands 40 harder for each one inside 2 seconds of the last,
  up to 200.
- **Two different Pledges** from one team inside 2 seconds combine at 150 power
  and leave a rainbow, a sea of fire or a swamp for 8 seconds. A sea of fire
  burns an eighth of a pokemon's HP each time it acts.
- **After You** finishes a teammate's wind-up at once, and **Quash** sends a
  target's wind-up back to the start.
- **Quick Guard** turns away moves with a shortened wind-up.
- **Wonder Room and Magic Room** end when they are cast a second time.

### A raid boss keeps its footing

- **A boss is never staged** with Final Gambit, Quash, Guard Split, Power Split,
  After You, Ally Switch or Bestow.
- **It shrugs off** Quash, Sky Drop, Guard Split and Power Split aimed at it.
- **Me First** no longer cuts a boss's move off.

## Three more starters

Nine species, each with its learnset, its sprites and a candy of its own. **A
new trainer now picks a starter from fifteen.**

- **Snivy** walks the shrublands and steppes, **Tepig** the badlands and
  deserts, and **Oshawott** the rocky coasts and kelp forests, in the water and
  on the shore.
- **Each has a signature built on its first landed move.** Leaf Opening takes a
  stage of Speed off whatever Snivy hits first. Ember Opening makes Tepig's
  first landed move hit 1.5x. Shell Opening raises Oshawott's Defense two
  stages when its first move lands.
- Serperior also reaches Infiltrator and Unnerve, Emboar reaches Iron Fist, and
  Samurott reaches Swift Swim and Sniper. None of those is in the mainline.

## Trainers play them better

- **A move that raises several stats**, such as Dragon Dance or Calm Mind, is
  still worth using once one of those stats is full.
- **Follow Me, Role Play and Skill Swap** are left alone when there is nobody to
  pull for and nothing to copy.
- **A built expert team reads its own abilities.** It counts a rampage at full
  power, skips a drawback its ability prevents, drops a move its ability ruins,
  and carries a move that suits a teammate, such as Swagger for Own Tempo.
- **An expert weighs recoil** against the HP the pokemon has, for Flare Blitz,
  Brave Bird, Wood Hammer and Head Smash.

## Moves look like themselves

**57 of the 92** carry a picture chosen for them, either their own or the one an
older move that does the same thing already uses. The rest arrive as their type
does.

- **Techno Blast** fires a line of plasma that rings out in hexagons, and
  **Psyshock** hangs shards of psychic force round the target and drives them
  in.
- **Shell Smash** cracks the shell off the pokemon, and **Quiver Dance**
  flutters glittering wings up round it.
- **Blue Flare** raises a column of blue fire, **Bolt Strike** brings bolts down
  from every side, and **Freeze Shock and Ice Burn** break ice spikes up round
  the target.
- **Sacred Sword and Secret Sword** drop a great sword point first through the
  target, and **V-create** drives a V of fire down onto it.
- **The Pledges** raise a column of fire, water or leaves out of the ground.
- **Heavy Slam and Heat Crash** spread a shadow under the target before the
  ground gives way.
- **A move that does what an older one does is drawn like it.** Hurricane blows
  like Aeroblast, Dragon Tail swings like Iron Tail, and Wide Guard and Quick
  Guard put up Protect's shell.

## Everything already here learns them

**Kanto, Johto and Hoenn** learn the new moves by level, machine, tutor and egg,
the way Black, White, Black 2 and White 2 teach them.

## Fixes along the way

- **A fight's top bar fits a phone.** The health bars take a row of their own,
  so the speed buttons and Leave stay reachable.
- **A catch sheet and a dex entry** draw a pokemon at its real height, standing
  on the portrait's floor, and a move's category badge stands as tall as its
  name.
- **Sprite sheets are kept by the browser** instead of being checked on every
  visit, and a repacked sheet is still picked up.
- **A raid host who lost can host the lair again**, rather than being sent back
  to the fight they lost.
