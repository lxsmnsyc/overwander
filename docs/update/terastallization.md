# Terastallization

**Terastallization** arrives, with every catch's **Tera type**, **Tera Shards**
and **Tera raids** at a new Tera Crystal landmark.

## How it works

Like a Mega, a Z-Move and Dynamax, there is nothing to press. A pokemon holding
a **Tera Orb** Terastallizes as it takes the field. Only the first one on a side
does, once a fight. A pokemon holding a Mega Stone, a Z-Crystal or a Dynamax
Band never Terastallizes.

Terastallized, a pokemon:

- is hit as its Tera type alone;
- keeps 1.5x on its own types' moves and gains 1.5x on its Tera type's, or 2x
  where the Tera type is one of its own (2.25x with Adaptability);
- lifts Tera-type moves under 60 power to 60, except multi-hit and priority
  moves;
- stays Terastallized for the rest of the fight, through switching out.

**Tera Blast** takes the Tera type once its user has Terastallized, and hits as
a physical move when the user's Attack is higher than its Special Attack.

A Terastallized pokemon glows in its Tera type's colour, with that type's jewel
floating over it.

## Tera types and Tera Shards

- **Every new catch has a Tera type**, usually one of its own types and now and
  then any type. Catches from before keep their first type as their Tera type.
- **The catch sheet shows it** as a type badge.
- **Fifty Tera Shards** of a type change a catch's Tera type to that type.
  Shards are found in the overworld and won from Tera raids.

## Tera raids

A **Tera Crystal** stands in place of some caches, above ground and in caves.
Its raid is a Terastallized pokemon of the biome. The lobby shows its Tera type
and reads its weaknesses off that type alone.

Clearing one pays a raid's gold and Tera Shards of the boss's Tera type. A boss
caught after the raid keeps its Tera type.
