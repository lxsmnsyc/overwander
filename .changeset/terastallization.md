- Terastallization: a pokemon holding the new Tera Orb Terastallizes as it takes the field, the first on its side, once a fight.
- Terastallized, it is hit as its Tera type alone, keeps the power of its own types and gains power on the Tera type, and its weak Tera-type moves rise to 60 power. It lasts the whole fight.
- Tera Blast takes the user's Tera type once it has Terastallized.
- Every new catch has a Tera type, usually one of its own types. Fifty Tera Shards of a type change it. The catch sheet shows it.
- A Terastallized pokemon glows in its Tera type's colour, with its jewel floating over it.
- Tera raids at a new Tera Crystal landmark: a Terastallized boss of the biome that pays Tera Shards of its Tera type, and keeps that type once caught.
