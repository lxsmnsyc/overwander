# Paldea's Zeniths

**60 new Zeniths**: one for every Paldea pokemon that can have one, from
Meowscarada to Pecharunt, and one for the Paldean Tauros, shared by its three
breeds. Each is reached by holding its own **Zenith Shard**, which is dug up
where its line lives. A Zenith fights 100 above its pokemon's base stat total,
none of it in HP, and wears an ability its line can never be born with. No two
of Paldea's share one. With these, every region has its Zeniths.

## What to look at first

- **Shape-changers keep their Zenith.** Zenith Terapagos shifts into a Terastal
  Zenith as it arrives, and Zenith Palafin comes back from a switch as a Hero
  Zenith.
- **One shard, several shapes.** Each of Ogerpon's masks, the three Paldean
  Tauros breeds, female Oinkologne, Maushold's family of three, Three-Segment
  Dudunsparce and Masterpiece Sinistcha has a Zenith shape under its pokemon's
  shard. Ogerpon's Terastal shapes have one too, though a shard holder never
  Terastallizes.
- **Some take a new type.** Zenith Orthworm is Steel/Ground.
- **Some are still waiting.** Bramblin, Toedscool, Rellor and Flittle wait for
  their final stages, and the Paldea pokemon without art yet get their Zeniths
  with it.
