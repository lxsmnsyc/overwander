Signature abilities for the next four Johto families:

- Fermenter (Shuckle): each time it acts with a free hand, a Berry Juice appears in it.
- Heave (Heracross): the first contact move it lands on each enemy casts Whirlwind at them.
- Sharp Claw (Sneasel): each contact move it lands drops the target's Defense by a stage.
- Sweet Paw (Teddiursa): its contact moves heal it 1/8 of the damage they deal.
