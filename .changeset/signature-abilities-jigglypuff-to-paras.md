Signature abilities for four more Kanto families:

- Lullaby (Jigglypuff): sleep it inflicts lasts 1.5x as long, and its moves hit 1.5x against a sleeping target.
- Bloodthirst (Zubat): draining moves and Leech Seed restore 1.5x for it, and every other heal on it is halved.
- Deep Roots (Oddish): while casting or channelling it takes 0.6x and cannot be made to flinch.
- Fungal Bloom (Paras): heals 1/8 of its HP each time it lands a status condition on an enemy.
