A trainer, a grunt or a stall no longer stands on the water in a flooded field. A chunk whose dry ground runs out used to put the next landmark on the nearest wet cell whatever it was, so a country that is almost all lake could field a Bird Keeper out on the water. Only what can be afloat takes a wet cell now, and a duel is afloat on the open sea alone, where the country's trainers are narrowed to its swimmers and sailors.
