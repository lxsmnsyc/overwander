# Unova's second road and first cave

**Six more families** from the fifth generation: three that live along the
grass of Unova's second road, and three that live under it, in the first cave
that road runs past.

Everything from before is still here.

## The road

**Munna and Musharna** drift over grassland and woodland in the evening and at
night. A Munna becomes a Musharna when it is given a **Moon Stone**, so it
never needs a level.

**Pidove, Tranquill and Unfezant** are the pigeons of the same two countries,
out in the morning and by day. Pidove becomes Tranquill at 21 and Unfezant at
32. A female Unfezant is drawn as her own bird rather than as a recoloured
male.

**Blitzle and Zebstrika** run the grassland and the steppe by day. Blitzle
becomes Zebstrika at 27.

**Two of those three lines are in the dex before they are in the world.**
Nobody has drawn Tranquill, Blitzle or Zebstrika yet, and a line waits for its
middle stage, so the Pidove and Blitzle families can be read about and looked
up but not met. Munna and Musharna are out there now.

## The cave

A cave has no sky, so **the cave pool is the same at every hour**. What is down
there is down there whether you go in at dawn or at midnight.

**Roggenrola, Boldore and Gigalith** are the ore of the place. Roggenrola is
the commonest thing in any cave in the world. Boldore turns up deeper in, and
Gigalith is elusive. Roggenrola becomes Boldore at 25, and **Boldore becomes
Gigalith when it is traded**, so a Gigalith is somebody's gift or somebody's
bargain. The line is also out on mountains and in the badlands in the morning
and by day.

**Woobat and Swoobat** hang in the same caves, and come out on mountains and
in the montane forest in the evening and at night. **A Woobat evolves on
friendship**, which is the first of the fifth generation's families to ask for
it.

**Drilbur and Excadrill** dig the caves, the badlands and the desert, the last
two in the morning and by day. Drilbur becomes Excadrill at 31 and picks up
**Steel** on the way, so a digger that was only Ground before comes out of the
ground armoured.

## Six signature abilities

Each of the six families brings one of its own.

- **Doze**, for the Munna family. It heals 1/16 of its HP for each second it
  has stood idle, up to 8, as it next acts.
- **Homing**, for the Pidove family. Its moves cannot miss a target it has
  already landed a move on this fight.
- **Storm Dash**, for the Blitzle family. Its moves hit 10% harder for each
  stage of Speed it holds, up to 1.5x.
- **Aftershock**, for the Roggenrola family. A blow that leaves it standing on
  1 HP costs whoever threw it 1/4 of their HP.
- **Heart Mark**, for the Woobat family. It casts Attract at an enemy as it
  arrives on the field.
- **Torque**, for the Drilbur family. Its moves hit 1.25x and wind up 25%
  slower.

Torque leaves cooldowns alone, so a Drilbur's Speed still says what Speed
always says. Doze stops banking the moment a Munna starts casting or
channelling, and pays out only if there is damage to heal.

## What else they can be born with

The ordinary pools are the mainline's: Forewarn and Synchronize on the Munna
line, Big Pecks and Super Luck on the pigeons, Lightning Rod and Motor Drive on
the zebras, Sturdy and Weak Armor on the ore, Unaware and Klutz on the bats,
and Sand Rush and Sand Force on the diggers. Telepathy, Rivalry, Sap Sipper,
Sand Force, Simple and Mold Breaker are the hidden ones.

**Each final stage reaches a fourth.** A Musharna can be born with Comatose, an
Unfezant with Defiant, a Zebstrika with Reckless, a Gigalith with Sand Stream,
a Swoobat with Infiltrator and an Excadrill with Tough Claws.

## Where to look first

- **Any cave.** Roggenrola is the base of the pool everywhere underground, and
  Woobat and Drilbur are a step behind it.
- **Grassland and woodland after dark**, for Munna.
- **Badlands and desert in the morning**, for Drilbur out in the open, and
  mountains by day for Roggenrola.
