Signature abilities for four more Kanto families:

- Overload (Voltorb): its Speed doubles below 1/2 HP.
- Psyseed (Exeggcute): an enemy its Psychic moves damage has Leech Seed cast on it.
- Mourning Bone (Cubone): its moves hit 1.4x while it is the only one left standing on its side.
- Second Wind (Tyrogue): the first time it falls below 1/4 HP it heals 1/3 of its HP, once per battle.
