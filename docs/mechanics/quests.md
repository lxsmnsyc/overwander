# Quests

A **quest** is something the game asks of you and pays you for. The quest
board sits behind **Quests** in the menu: each card says what to do, how far
along you are, and what it pays. When every line on a card is met, Claim
hands the reward over on the spot.

## Requirements

Most requirements are lifetime totals: pokemon caught (overall, of a type,
of a species, or from one line), eggs hatched, levels raised, items used,
steps walked with a buddy, services taken from the wandering people, Team
Rocket grunts beaten, landmarks claimed, raids fought or won, trades
settled, friends made, auctions settled. Everything you have already done
counts; nothing resets.

A few quests instead ask you to **hand something over**: those items stay in
your bag until the moment you claim, and are taken as the reward is paid. A
bag short of the ask claims nothing and loses nothing.

## Rewards

A quest can pay items, a finished pokemon, a meeting (the pokemon stands in
front of you and you throw your own ball; it cannot run), or an egg. What is
in an egg stays unsaid until it hatches. Everything a quest pays also shows
on your gift history, since it is paid through the same machinery.

## The rotating board

Beside the lifetime quests are two boards that turn over on their own, the same
for every player in the world:

| Board      | What it holds                                                                                | Turns over |
| ---------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------- |
| **Daily**  | Three: the day's featured family (or plain catches), a walk, and one drawn from a small pool | Every day  |
| **Weekly** | One hunt: five catches from a single family line                                             | Every week |

Both are read in **UTC**, so they turn over at the same moment everywhere rather
than sweeping around the world with local midnight. The weekly hunt never names
a legendary: those belong to raids.

Progress on a rotating quest is counted **from the moment the window first sees
you**, not from your lifetime total, so a board is always something to go and do
rather than something already finished. An unclaimed daily is gone when the day
turns.

## Chains

Some quests are links in a chain: the next one only appears once the one
before it is claimed. The opening chain walks a new trainer through the
whole game, from a first catch to a first raid.
