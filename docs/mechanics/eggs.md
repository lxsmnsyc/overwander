# Eggs

An **egg** is a pokemon that has not been seen yet. Everything inside it is
settled at the moment the egg is obtained, so hatching **reveals** the pokemon
rather than rolling it: walking further or waiting longer cannot improve what
comes out.

An egg starts at level 1, holds nothing, and cannot do anything a pokemon does:
it cannot be given items, levelled, evolved, entered into a raid or groomed.

Eggs come from **nests** in the world and from the **breeder**, one of the
travelling specialists. A **Hidden Grotto** holds one about one time in 64, laid
the way a nest's is, and the quest **Nest for a Nibble** pays a Chansey egg for
three Oran Berries. A grotto's egg and the quest's are nest eggs in every way
described below.

## Nests

A **Nest** landmark holds one egg every **twelve hours**, refilling at local
midnight and noon, so each player may take one per half day.

What lies in it is drawn from the commoner pokemon of that biome at that time of
day, always the first stage of its line. A nest holds what hatches, not what it
grows into: a Marill in the biome is an Azurill in the nest. A biome where
several early stages of one line live is a biome where that egg is
correspondingly more likely. Only the base, uncommon and rare bands are drawn
from, so a line met there only as its final stage, or a species that never
evolves, is never in a nest.

No nest ever holds a legendary, and none holds an unown: unowns are met rather
than hatched.

A nest egg is better than a wild meeting of the same species:

- It knows **two egg moves**, where its line has any. The first is one no other
  line in its egg groups learns by levelling, where there is one, which is a
  move only a chain of breeding would otherwise teach. Both are placed first in
  the move list, so the four-move limit cannot displace them.
- **Two of its six individual values are perfect**, which two decided at random.
- Its hidden ability turns up about one time in five rather than one in eight.
- It hatches with room for **two abilities** and **two held items**.
- It needs **half the walk** its species would otherwise ask for.

It comes in a Nest Ball. A nest is claimed under the sky the way a wild pokemon is
met under it, so the weather's gifts reach the egg: see [Weather](weather.md).
A dark day can close its heart, too.

## Breeding

A **Breeder** takes two of the player's pokemon and **5,000 gold**. Neither
parent is consumed, held or locked; both are returned the moment the egg exists.

A pair can produce an egg when all of the following hold:

| Requirement                          | Reason                                     |
| ------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------ |
| They share an egg group              | The usual breeding rule                    |
| Opposite genders, or one is a Ditto  | Two Dittos produce nothing                 |
| Neither is in the undiscovered group | Where legendaries and the unbreedable live |
| Neither is an egg                    | An egg is not a parent                     |
| They are two different pokemon       | One pokemon cannot be both parents         |
| Neither is in a live battle          | A pokemon in a fight is busy               |

The egg is the first stage of the **mother's** line, or of the non-Ditto parent's
line when a Ditto stands in. A few mothers lay something else: a Manaphy's egg is
a Phione. The breeder helps each player once per three-hour window at a given
cell.

### Inheritance

**Three of the six individual stats** are copied from one parent or the other and
the rest are rolled fresh, or **five** when either parent holds a **Destiny
Knot**. Which ones, and which parent each comes from, are random, so leaving the
same pair with the breeder twice produces two different pokemon. A parent
holding a power item passes the stat it names for certain, and that counts
toward the three or five. Two power items still force only one stat.

Any move the line can **only** inherit, and that one of the parents actually
knows, is passed down. That is what makes breeding a way to _teach_ a move rather
than to gamble for one. A move **both** parents know that the line learns by
levelling is passed down too, years before it would have been due. Inherited
moves come first in the list.

The rest comes mostly from the mother:

- **Ability:** the mother's, four times in five, or three in five when it is her
  hidden one, as long as the egg's line can have it. Otherwise the egg rolls its
  own.
- **Nature:** its own, unless a parent holds an **Everstone**, which passes that
  parent's nature down whole.
- **Ball:** the mother's. A Master Ball is not passed on, and the egg comes in a
  Poke Ball instead.

The hatchling's level and gender are its own.

A **shadow parent may pass the shadow on**, but only half the time, so breeding
two shadows is no more certain than breeding one. An egg that inherits it hatches
with the Shadow ability permanently, costs double candy to raise, and takes twice
the walk to hatch.

## Hatching

Only the **buddy** walks, so an egg must be the buddy for its steps to count.

**How far depends on the species.** An egg is counted in hatch cycles, and one
cycle is **128 steps**. Most of the dex takes 20 cycles, or 2,560 steps, but a
Magikarp is out after 5 (640 steps) and a Dratini takes 40 (5,120).

| Egg                          | Cycles | Steps |
| ---------------------------- | ------ | ----- |
| Magikarp                     | 5      | 640   |
| Pichu, Cleffa, Igglybuff     | 10     | 1,280 |
| The early-route ones         | 15     | 1,920 |
| Most species                 | 20     | 2,560 |
| Elekid, Magby, Scyther, Onix | 25     | 3,200 |
| Omanyte, Kabuto              | 30     | 3,840 |
| Eevee, Aerodactyl            | 35     | 4,480 |
| Happiny, Munchlax, Dratini   | 40     | 5,120 |

A **nest egg halves** whatever its species asks for, a **shadow egg doubles**
it, and a portal crossing counts nothing at all: a crossing is not a walk.

**Flame Body** or **Magma Armor** **halves the walk**, but only if that pokemon
was the buddy **when the egg was picked up**. It stacks with the nest's half. This makes it a real choice: walk to the nest with the
Ponyta, then swap to the egg once it is in hand. Picking up a Ponyta afterwards
does nothing for an egg already being carried, because once the egg is the buddy
there is nothing beside the player keeping it warm.

On the **species day** of whatever is inside it, an egg counts every pace as 1.2.
That is credit for the walking done on the day, so an egg carried past midnight
keeps what it banked.

Steps are credited against real elapsed time, so nothing is gained by reporting
them in a rush, and an egg's progress always matches the ground actually walked.

Hatching pays candy for the family exactly as catching one would, and the pokemon
emerges at **120 friendship** rather than the 70 a caught pokemon starts at. A
shadow hatches at nothing: its heart is closed whichever way it arrived.

## The daycare lady

A **Daycare Lady** takes an egg and **2,500 gold** and advances it by **half of
its whole walk**. An egg a quarter of the way along comes out three
quarters of the way; one past halfway is finished outright; any egg at all is
finished by two visits. The fee is what paces it.

She refuses only an egg already ready to hatch, and, like most travellers, helps
each player once per three-hour window at a given cell. See
[People you meet](npcs.md).
