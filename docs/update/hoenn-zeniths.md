# Hoenn's Zeniths

**50 new Zeniths**, one for every Hoenn pokemon that can have one, from
Mightyena to Deoxys. Each is reached by holding its own **Zenith Shard**, which
is dug up where its line lives. A Zenith fights 100 above its pokemon's base
stat total and wears an ability its line can never be born with; no two of
Hoenn's share one.

## What to look at first

- **Zenith Castform** brings its own hail and turns into its Snowy shape. Its
  weather shapes are Zeniths too, so a sun or a rain from the other side turns
  it into the Sunny or Rainy Zenith rather than taking the Zenith away.
- **Zenith Deoxys** has a shape for each of its four: whichever shape holds
  the one Deoxys Zenith Shard fights as that shape's Zenith, and each wears
  Beast Boost.
- **Zenith Shedinja** keeps its 1 HP and Wonder Guard and wears Sturdy, so it
  lives through the first hit that gets past its guard.
- **Zenith Slaking** still loafs every other move, but Guts turns any status
  into more Attack.
- **Zenith Whiscash** wears Sap Sipper, so its one weakness, Grass, feeds it
  instead.
