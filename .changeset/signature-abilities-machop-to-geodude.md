Signature abilities for four more Kanto families:

- Overhead Throw (Machop): its contact moves hit 1.4x against a heavier target and 1.1x against a lighter one.
- Digest (Bellsprout): landing a move on a target at or below 1/4 HP heals it 1/4 of its own HP.
- Tentacle Grasp (Tentacool): no enemy it has landed a move on may flee while it is still standing.
- Solid Core (Geodude): physical moves hit it at 0.7x and special moves at 1.3x.
