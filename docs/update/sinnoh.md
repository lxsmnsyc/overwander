# Sinnoh

Sinnoh is the fourth region. It adds 107 pokemon, 113 moves, a fourth league, a
second Battle Frontier and Team Galactic. And **every one of the 246 families**
now has a signature ability of its own.

Everything from before is still here.

## 107 more pokemon

The dex runs to **493**. Sinnoh is its own region in it, with its own quest
chain and its own medal.

- **Three new starters.** A new trainer now picks a first partner from twelve.
- **Nine legendaries**: Uxie, Mesprit, Azelf, Dialga, Palkia, Giratina,
  Heatran, Regigigas and Cresselia.
- **Four mythicals**: Manaphy, Darkrai, Shaymin and Arceus. **Phione** hatches
  from a Manaphy's egg.
- **Rotom**, which moves between all six of its shapes.
- **Two fossils**, the Skull and the Armor, holding Cranidos and Shieldon.
- **New stages for 28 older lines**, among them Magnezone, Rhyperior, Togekiss,
  Weavile, Mamoswine, Gallade, Froslass, Leafeon and Glaceon.
- **Seven babies**: Mime Jr., Happiny, Munchlax, Bonsly, Mantyke, Budew and
  Chingling. Riolu is a baby too, and turns up in the prized band.
- **The Sinnoh Pokedex** asks for 21 caught, then 54, then 103, and pays a
  Master Ball, the **Sinnoh Dex Medal** and **Professor Rowan** to wear.

### New ways to evolve

- **A move the pokemon knows.** A Yanma needs Ancient Power to become a
  Yanmega, and a Lickitung needs Rollout.
- **A trade while holding gear.** A Rhydon handed over holding a Protector
  becomes a Rhyperior. The Electirizer, Magmarizer, Dubious Disc and Reaper
  Cloth work the same way.
- **An egg that is never its mother.** A Manaphy's egg hatches into a Phione,
  which is the only way to meet one.
- **Held gear at night.** A Sneasel holding a Razor Claw becomes a Weavile in
  the evening or at night, and a Gligar does the same with a Razor Fang.

### Shapes you choose

- **Rotom** takes a machine from a **Rotom Catalog**, which offers every shape
  but the one it is in.
- **Dialga, Palkia and Giratina** fight in their Origin Formes holding their own
  orb.
- **Shaymin** fights in its Sky Forme holding a **Gracidea**.
- **Arceus** takes the type of the **Plate** it holds, across eighteen shapes.
- **Burmy** wears the cloak of the biome it was met in, and **Shellos** wears
  the shell of its side of the map.
- **Cherrim** opens in the sun and closes when it goes.
- **A form brings its own ability** into battle. It takes no slot and cannot be
  copied, traded or taken.

## A signature ability for every family

**246 signature abilities**, one for every family from Bulbasaur to Arceus. Each
belongs to its family and nobody else.

- **A group shares one idea.** Each legendary beast survives one finishing blow
  on 1 HP, then grows a stage faster, stronger or harder to hurt.
- **The starters share one too.** Bulbasaur, Charmander and Squirtle each tilt
  the field toward their own type. Treecko, Torchic and Mudkip each grow a stat
  as they fight.
- **Counterparts answer each other.** Lunatone makes its party take less and
  Solrock makes enemies take more, and neither works while the other stands.
- **Some examples:** Chansey never loses more than 1/4 of its HP to one hit.
  Abra blinks away from the first attack. Magikarp hits harder the longer it
  lasts. Relicanth has no weaknesses and no resistances.
- **The raid demo** grants every pokemon its signature, so you can watch them.

## 113 new moves

Every Diamond, Pearl and Platinum move, merged into every learnset from Kanto to
Sinnoh.

- **Moves that read the fight.** Payback and Assurance double against a target
  that acted or was hurt in the last 2 seconds. Sucker Punch waits for a target
  mid-cast. Trump Card grows with every one played.
- **Trick Room** reverses cast times. Cooldowns stay with Speed.
- **Stealth Rock and Toxic Spikes** are laid on the ground, and **Defog** clears
  both sides.
- **Healing Wish and Lunar Dance** are spent on a teammate already on the
  field. Lunar Dance also makes every move ready at once.

## Moves look like themselves

Sinnoh's moves, and dozens of older ones, land as a picture of their own.

- **Roar of Time** throws out rings that freeze and shatter, and **Spacial
  Rend** tears a pink gash in the air.
- **Judgment** brings shafts of light down round the target, and **Draco
  Meteor** brings meteors down on it.
- **Crush Grip** closes two great hands on the target, and **Magma Storm** winds
  a vortex of lava round it.
- **Swords Dance** crosses swords overhead, **Protect** throws up a shell of
  hexagons, and **Splash** flops about while nothing happens.
- **Pin Missile, Bullet Seed and Rock Blast** fly in one piece at a time.
- **Aqua Ring** puts hoops of water round the pokemon, **Trick Room** builds a
  room over the field, and **Gravity** weighs it down.
- **A substitute stands in front** of the pokemon behind it, which dims until it
  breaks. The doll drops in from above.
- **U-turn hits and then leaves**, drawn as a throw out and back.
- **X-Scissor and Cross Chop cross into an X**, and every slash is a straight
  blade.
- **Sweet Scent blows petals** and **Aeroblast is a storm**.
- **The mark each blow leaves** is drawn in the field, in front of the pokemon
  it hit.

## A fourth league

**Thirty-two gyms**, eight badges in each region, and four champions.

- **Sinnoh's leaders**, Roark to Volkner, keep gyms in the countries their type
  belongs to.
- **Aaron, Bertha, Flint and Lucian** take the Elite Four seats.
- **Cynthia** takes the champion's seat with Spiritomb, Roserade, Togekiss,
  Lucario, Milotic and Garchomp, and pays the **Sinnoh Champion** title.
- Every leader is drawn in their **Diamond and Pearl coat**, unlocked by their
  badge.

## Sinnoh's Battle Frontier

Five more houses, and a **print** for each.

| House          | Keeper            | The house rule                               |
| -------------- | ----------------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| Battle Tower   | Palmer            | Nothing                                      |
| Battle Factory | Thorton           | The house rents both sides                   |
| Battle Arcade  | Dahlia            | A spun panel sets the sky, items or statuses |
| Battle Castle  | Darach or Caitlin | Nothing heals your side for the whole fight  |
| Battle Hall    | Argenta           | One pokemon a side, hers drawn against yours |

- A win hangs the **silver print**. Beating the second three hangs the
  **gold** one.

## Team Galactic

- **Cyrus** leads, with **Mars, Jupiter and Saturn** under him.
- They keep the crime landmark in the **cold country**: glaciers, tundra, taiga,
  montane forest and the Beyond.
- Each of them pays their own mark, and the uniform pays one for clearing a
  cell.

## Honey trees and better nests

- **Honey Trees grow in forests.** Lather one with **Honey** once a window and a
  pokemon comes down to meet you on the spot.
- **Twelve species come only from Honey Trees**: Weedle, Kakuna, Wurmple,
  Silcoon, Cascoon, Combee, Burmy, Aipom, Teddiursa, Heracross, Pinsir and
  Munchlax. Honey is sold at the medicine stall and turns up in forest caches.
- **A nest egg is worth the walk.** Two stats hatch perfect, a hidden ability
  comes one time in five, it knows two egg moves, and it hatches in half the
  steps with room for a second ability and a second held item.

## Water and ice have their own spawns

- **Ponds, rivers and seas** spawn what lives in water, and a sea's islands
  spawn what lives on land.
- **Frozen water** in cold biomes has spawns of its own, and it can still be
  walked on.
- **Nothing that only swims turns up on land**, and nothing that only walks
  turns up in water.
- **Uxie, Mesprit and Azelf** can also be met in the waters of their lakes.

## Legends in more places

- **Seventeen new lairs**, from Spear Pillar and Turnback Cave to the three
  lakes, the Hall of Origin and Flower Paradise.
- **A legendary can live in more than one lair**, and its dex entry lists them
  all. The Regis can also be raided at Rock Peak, Iceberg and Iron Ruins.
- **The Embedded Tower** stands on beaches with Kyogre, Groudon and Rayquaza,
  and **Navel Rock** rises in the deep ocean with Lugia and Ho-Oh.
- **Every legendary spawns wild** in each biome that hosts its lair.
- **Rayquaza and the Sky Pillar** moved from the mountains to the ocean.

## More trainers on the road

- **Sinnoh brings 42 classes**, covering every type the region grows. 16 are
  trades nobody had before, among them the Worker, the Rancher and the Cyclist.
- **Seven Hoenn classes** that shipped without anyone wearing them stand at the
  stops now, from the Pokémon Breeder to the Young Couple.
- Every class is drawn in its own coat, which its own wins unlock.

## Raids play fair

- **Stat drops land on a raid boss.** Growl, Screech and Intimidate were refused
  before.
- **A boss no longer faints to Destiny Bond.** It faints only at zero HP.
- **Nothing takes a boss apart.** Role Play, Skill Swap, Gastro Acid, Worry
  Seed and the three stat swaps no longer work on one.
- **Taunt, Torment, Imprison and Encore** no longer hold a boss to part of its
  move set.

## Fixes along the way

- **A volcano's lava can no longer be walked on**, and nothing spawns or stands
  on it.
- **A confused pokemon hitting itself** no longer breaks the fight.
- **Follow Me outranks** the abilities that pull a move elsewhere.
- **A rental support** is no longer given a nature that raises its attack.
- **Big raids run lighter.** Asking whether a pokemon has an ability no longer
  sweeps the whole field.
- **The sprite processor** is its own page at /sprite-processor.
