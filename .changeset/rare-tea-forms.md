- One Sinistea and Poltchageist in a hundred is the real thing: an antique Sinistea grows into an antique Polteageist with the new Chipped Pot, and an artisan Poltchageist into a masterpiece Sinistcha with the new Masterpiece Teacup.
- One Dunsparce in a hundred grows into Three-Segment Dudunsparce.
- A wild Polteageist, Sinistcha or Dudunsparce is its rare form just as often. Each is drawn as its usual form until it has art of its own.
