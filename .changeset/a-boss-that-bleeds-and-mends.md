---
'overwander': patch
---

- A raid boss takes indirect damage again: poison, burns, seeds, weather and a crash off a missed move all count, for at most 100 each.
- Damage measured as a share of its health, such as Super Fang, is still refused, and a cost it pays itself is still paid in full.
- A boss heals at most an eighth of its pool at a time, so the healing moves are back in its learnset. Rest stays barred, since the sleep lands in full while the healing does not.
- Leech Seed drains what it actually took rather than what it aimed for.
