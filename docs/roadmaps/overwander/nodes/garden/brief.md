# Garden

## Goal

A private patch of plots (for example 4) in a `garden_plots` table. Berries grow in real time, read off the planting time; a ripe plot yields 2 to 5 and wilts after a day. Watering per stage raises the yield and Mulch speeds growth or delays wilting, both optional.
