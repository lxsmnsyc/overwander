# Unova's first three families

The walk out of Unova's first town brings **seven more pokemon**: the scouts,
the dogs and the thieves that keep the grass beside the road. Each family
carries **a signature ability of its own**.

The rest of the region arrives family by family.

## Seven pokemon

- **Patrat and Watchog**, the scouts. A Patrat posts itself in the grassland and
  the steppe by day, and a Watchog keeps watch there in the evening and at
  night.
- **Lillipup, Herdier and Stoutland**, the dogs of the grassland and the
  woodland. A Stoutland leaves the woods for the steppe and the tundra.
- **Purrloin and Liepard**, the thieves of the woodland and the grassland after
  dark.
- **They grow as they go.** A Patrat becomes a Watchog at 20 and a Purrloin a
  Liepard at 20. A Lillipup becomes a Herdier at 16 and a Stoutland at 32.

## A signature for each of them

- **Spotter.** A Watchog on the field calls the shot: its whole team throws 1.2x
  as accurately at anything caught mid-cast.
- **Loyal Guard.** The dog plants itself while a teammate is under half health,
  and takes 1.5x Defense and Special Defense for as long as that lasts.
- **Cat Burglar.** The first move a Liepard lands each fight takes the target's
  held item, so long as it has a hand free.
