# Hisui's Zeniths

**16 new Zeniths**: one for every Hisui pokemon that can have one, from
Wyrdeer to Overqwil, and one for each Hisuian final of an older line, from
Hisuian Arcanine to Hisuian Decidueye. Each is reached by holding its own
**Zenith Shard**, which is dug up where its line lives. A Zenith fights 100
above its pokemon's base stat total, none of it in HP, and wears an ability its
line can never be born with. No two of Hisui's share one.

## What to look at first

- **Bloodmoon Ursaluna and female Basculegion share their shard.** Each has a
  Zenith shape of its own, wearing Thick Fat and Supreme Overlord.
- **The Hisuian finals have shards of their own.** A Hisuian Arcanine holding
  an Arcanine Zenith Shard stays as it is; it needs the Hisuian Arcanine Zenith
  Shard.
- **Zenith Hisuian Braviary turns its battle cry Flying.** Aerilate makes Hyper
  Voice and its other Normal moves Flying, with STAB.
