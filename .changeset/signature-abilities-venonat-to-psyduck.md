Signature abilities for four more Kanto families:

- Dust Storm (Venonat): its Special Attack rises 12% for every enemy carrying a status, up to 4 of them.
- Undermine (Diglett): each move it lands leaves that enemy taking 5% more from everybody, up to 25%, for the rest of the fight.
- Cutpurse (Meowth): the first move it lands on each enemy knocks their held item away.
- Headache Burst (Psyduck): at or below 1/2 HP its Special Attack is 1.5x and its Psychic moves cannot miss.
