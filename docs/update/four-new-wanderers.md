# Four new wanderers

**Four new people wander the world**: a **Geologist** who sells stones, a
**Dojo Master** who teaches a fifth move and more, a **Trader** who brings
pokemon from other biomes, and a **Hyper Trainer** who takes a value to the
top. **Every dialog is redrawn** as a white sheet, and **gym leaders, the Elite
Four and champions now change every 3 hours**.

Everything from before is still here.

## The new wanderers

| Who           | What they do                | Price                   | How often    |
| ------------- | --------------------------- | ----------------------- | ------------ |
| Geologist     | Sells 12 kinds of stone     | Gold                    | Any time     |
| Dojo Master   | One more move slot, up to 8 | A Heart Scale           | Any time     |
| Trader        | Swaps one of his 6 pokemon  | One of yours, same band | Once a visit |
| Hyper Trainer | Takes one IV to 31          | 10,000 gold a point     | Once a visit |

- **The Geologist's crate** is drawn from the evolution stones, the gems, the
  held stones, the weather rocks, the Everstone and Light Clay.
- **The weather rocks, the Everstone and Light Clay can now be bought**, and
  still turn up on the ground as before.
- **The Trader's pokemon come from other biomes.** One you take from him
  arrives traded, so it can take a trade evolution.
- **The Breeder** can now turn up in the breeder outfits from Diamond and
  Pearl, Omega Ruby and Alpha Sapphire, and Black 2 and White 2.

## The Skill Book

- **A new item that gives a pokemon one more move slot**, the same as a visit
  to the Dojo Master.
- **It is a prized find in item caches**, as rare as the Utility Belt, and is
  never sold.

## The league changes seats

- **Gym leaders, Elite Four members and champions change every 3 hours.** Each
  is drawn from the biome's own list, so one town cycles through them.

## The Move Reminder

- **It also offers what a pokemon's pre-evolutions learned** by levelling, up to
  its current level.

## Every dialog redrawn

- **Dialogs are white sheets with round corners.** The title sits on a blue name
  box across the top edge.
- **A dialog ends on a tinted dock**, with the way out on the left and the
  actions on the right. It has one way out, its own Close or Walk on button,
  rather than a second × beside it.
- **Costs, what you carry and what is at stake are shown as chips.**
- **Every NPC counter shares one layout**: the person and their words, then what
  it costs and what you have, then what you pick.
  - Every counter that takes gold or a Heart Scale shows how much you carry.
  - Picking a pokemon or a rock no longer spends anything. The button at the
    foot pays, with the price on it.
  - Once a counter has done its one thing, the person says so in place of the
    box.
- **The Portal, Honey Tree, Nest, Stop, Gym Seat and Raid dialogs** share the
  same layout. Crossing, lathering, taking an egg and seating a team report in a
  toast.
- **A trainer's dialog says in one line what beating them pays.** A Frontier
  Brain's house rule is still said in full.
- **The catch sheet is a summary screen.** The pokemon stands on a field with
  its level in the corner, its facts as chips under it, and its stats,
  abilities and items, moves and evolutions each in a card of their own.
- **The safari is a battle screen**, with the pokemon on a field, a textbox
  that says how each throw went, and Bag, Throw and Run tiles.

## Tooltips and hover cards

- **A tooltip is a small white tag** that follows the mouse: the name in bold,
  a chip saying what it is, and the description under it.
- **A hover card is a white card** with facts as rows rather than boxes. A
  move's card shows its power, accuracy, PP and cooldown as a row of four
  numbers.
- **Both flip and shift to stay on screen**, and keep their place as the page
  scrolls.

## Signing in

- **The sign-in screen is drawn on the same white sheet**, with Sign in and
  Create account as two tabs.
- **Google, GitHub and the passkey sit under an "or".** The passkey is on the
  Sign in tab only, since it opens an account that already has one.
- **A malformed email address is now named as the problem**, rather than "Could
  not sign you in just now."

## For staff

- **The staff dashboard has a Switches page.** It closes a part of the game, or
  all of it for maintenance, while the game runs, with the message players are
  told.
