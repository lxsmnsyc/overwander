Several read the fight rather than the clock, since there are no turns here. Payback and Assurance double their power against a target that has cast or been hurt in the last 2 seconds. Trump Card gets stronger with every one played this fight rather than as its PP runs down. Sucker Punch and Me First both wait for a target that is mid-cast on a damaging move, and Me First cuts that cast off. Last Resort waits until every other move the user knows has been cast this fight. Avalanche answers for a wound the user took in the last 2 seconds, which its long wind-up gives time to be dealt. Trick Room bends the wind-up rather than the turn order: while it stands, every cast on the field is timed as if its move's priority were the opposite sign, and cooldowns are left to Speed. Defog follows the modern rule and sweeps the spikes from both sides. Stealth Rock costs whatever walks in an eighth of its health, doubled or halved by how it takes a Rock move. Healing Wish and Lunar Dance are spent on a teammate already on the field, since a team is all out from the start of a fight and nothing is sent in to replace a fallen pokemon; the dance also clears what its target is waiting on, so every move is ready at once, which is what a full PP restore comes to where PP is a wait rather than a pool.
