Signature abilities for four more Kanto families:

- Static Field (Electabuzz): its Speed rises 15% for each contact hit it has taken, up to 4 of them.
- Forge Heat (Magmar): a burn it inflicted bites 1.5x as hard, and its moves hit 1.3x against a burning target.
- Snapjaw (Pinsir): its moves hit 1.5x against a target that is casting or channelling.
- Bullheaded (Tauros): its contact moves hit 1.3x, and everything hits it 1.15x in return.
