Signature abilities for five more Hoenn families:

- Cloud Step (Swablu): the first move aimed at it each battle deals no damage to it.
- Feud Claws (Zangoose): its moves hit 1.4x against a poisoned target.
- Venom Fang (Seviper): every contact move it lands leaves the target badly poisoned.
- Moon Pull (Lunatone): its side takes 0.85x damage while it stands, unless a Sun Glare holder stands too.
- Sun Glare (Solrock): enemies take 1.15x damage while it stands, unless a Moon Pull holder stands too.
