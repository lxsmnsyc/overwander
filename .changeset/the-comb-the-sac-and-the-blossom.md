Three more Sinnoh families: Combee, Buizel and Cherubi. Only a female Combee has a comb to build, so a Vespiquen is always one that was born to be one, and a Cherrim's open blossom is a worn shape the way a Castform's sky is. Their signatures are Pollen Dole, which sends 1/16 of a teammate's HP back to whoever is worst hurt each time it lands an attack, Float Sac, which holds a Buizel over Ground moves and hazards until a blow takes it under half, and Second Bloom, which opens a Cherubi once a fight for 1/4 of its HP and a stage of Special Attack.
