Signature abilities for four more Hoenn families:

- Cold Snap (Snorunt): whoever lands a contact move on it is frozen 20% of the time.
- Applause (Spheal): it heals 1/16 of its HP each time a teammate lands a move.
- Pearl Guard (Clamperl): its Special Attack and Special Defense count 1.5x while it holds an item.
- Unchanged (Relicanth): every move hits it for neutral damage, so it has no weaknesses and no resistances. Relicanth's fourth ability is now Multiscale rather than Solid Rock.
