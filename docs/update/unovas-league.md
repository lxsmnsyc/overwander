# Unova's league

**Unova's trainers take the road.** Thirteen gym leaders and ten new badges, an
Elite Four, **Iris** in the champion's seat, **N and Alder** above her, **Team
Plasma** in the woods, and 48 trainer classes. **Every town now stands the whole
ladder**, from a gym leader up to a champion.

Everything from before is still here.

## Thirteen gym leaders

Cilan, Chili, Cress, Lenora, Burgh, Elesa, Clay, Skyla, Brycen and Drayden from
the first league, and Cheren, Roxie and Marlon from the sequels.

- **Ten new badges**: Trio, Basic, Insect, Bolt, Quake, Jet, Freeze, Legend,
  Toxic and Wave. They are drawn from the Unova badge sheet on the profile
  shelf.
- **Striaton is one gym kept by three people.** Cilan, Chili and Cress each
  fight a different type, and they pay one Trio Badge between them.
- **Cheren and Lenora pay the same Basic Badge**, Cheren in Aspertia and Lenora
  in Nacrene.
- **Opelucid's gym is Drayden's**, so Iris is only ever the champion.
- **Each leader is seated in the countries their type lives in**, and fields
  the same world-wide pool of their type that every other leader does.

## The Elite Four

**Shauntal** on Ghost, **Marshal** on Fighting, **Grimsley** on Dark and
**Caitlin** on Psychic.

- **They keep the same seats in both of Unova's leagues**, so each is one fight
  with one mark.
- **Caitlin also keeps Sinnoh's Battle Castle.** Each seat pays its own coat:
  her league coat comes with her mark, and her castle coat with the castle's
  print.
- **Each asks to see Unova's ten badges** before they will fight.

## The champion and the two legends

- **Iris** holds Unova's crown. She asks to see all four of the Elite Four
  beaten first, and pays the **Unova Champion** title and both of her coats.
  Her six are the ones she defends the sequels with, Archeops among them and
  Haxorus last.
- **N** turns up where a champion would have been, at full level, and asks for
  no badges. He brings **both tao dragons** rather than the one his version
  hands him, behind Zoroark, Carracosta, Archeops and Klinklang. Beating him
  pays his mark and his coat.
- **Alder**, Unova's champion before Iris, turns up the same way with the six
  he held the league with: Accelgor, Bouffalant, Druddigon, Vanilluxe,
  Escavalier and Volcarona. Beating him pays the **Alder Defeated** mark, which
  unlocks his coat.

## Every town stands the whole ladder

- **Certain in every town**: a Pokemon Center, a gym seat, an auction board, a
  gym leader, an Elite Four member and a champion.
- **About 1 town in 3** stands a second gym leader, which is two badges in one
  walk.
- **Fewer lots are left for trade.** A town now averages 2.5 market stalls and
  2.6 wandering NPCs, down from about 3.5 of each.

## Team Plasma

- **Team Plasma keeps the crime landmark in the woods**: the temperate forest,
  the two rainforests, the tropical seasonal forest and the woodland. Team
  Rocket keeps the open country it held there before.
- **Grunts come in both uniforms**, the robes and the fatigues.
- **Colress and Zinzolin** stand above them, and **Ghetsis** bars the cell in
  either of his coats.
- **Four new marks**: Team Plasma Repelled for clearing a cell, and one each for
  Colress, Zinzolin and Ghetsis.

## 48 trainer classes

- **Twenty-two trades nobody had before**: the Backpacker, Baker, Clerk, Dancer,
  Depot Agent, Doctor, Harlequin, Hoopster, Infielder, Janitor, Lady,
  Linebacker, Maid, Musician, Nursery Aide, Pilot, Smasher, Socialite, Striker,
  Suit Actor, Veteran and the Backers.
- **Twenty-six of Unova's own takes on trades already on the road**, from its
  Ace Trainer down, each fielding Unova's answer to that type.
- **Each stands in the countries its type belongs to**, and Unova's Ace Trainer
  travels everywhere like the other regions' four.
