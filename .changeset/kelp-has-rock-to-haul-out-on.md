A kelp forest's rocks have life on them: Krabby, Seel, Shellos, Buizel and Tangela by day, with Kingler, Dewgong, Gastrodon, Floatzel, Tangrowth and Shuckle behind them, Wingull and Pelipper overhead until dusk, and the crabs, seals, slugs and otters alone after dark. The Unowns were the only thing standing above the waterline before, which made an alphabet easier to meet than it should be.
