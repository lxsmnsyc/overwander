Boxes, the Bag, the Pokedex and the dex entry take the Field Kit look.

- Boxes opens as wide as the catch sheet. Your boxes run down the left, each with a meter showing how full it is. The box's name, its fill and its menu sit on one line over it, and This box or All boxes is a switch beside the search.
- While picking, a green bar under the title holds everything you can do to the picks. A Picked tray under the box shows what you have picked in every box.
- The let-go list pages twenty at a time.
- The Bag's pockets are a rail of colours with their counts. Pressing an item now shows it beside the tray first, with the button that uses it. The panel gives its count, pocket, sell price, what it does and which of your pokemon it would help.
- The Pokedex counts seen and caught at the top of a rail of regions. A region you have caught in full wears a gold star, and a square whose species comes in forms carries a mark. A key under the grid says what each square means.
- A dex entry has a header washed in the species' colour over three columns. The left holds the portrait, its facts and its evolution line. The middle holds base stats, abilities and habitat. The right holds its moves.
- The Bottle Cap and Ability Patch choices are cards, and the moves to pick from look like the catch sheet's move cards.
