# Paldea's routes

**32 of Paldea's families**, from the starters to Frigibax, each with a
signature ability. They are met across Paldea's provinces, its coast and the
north, and every Gen 9 ability they bring works in battle.

## The starters

**Sprigatito, Fuecoco and Quaxly** are performers. One of each one's blows
reaches past the pokemon it was thrown at.

- **Petal Scatter:** a Grass critical hit Sprigatito's line lands also deals
  1/4 of its damage to every other enemy.
- **Spreading Flame:** when a Fire move from Fuecoco's line burns a target,
  the other enemy lowest on HP is burned too.
- **Wake:** a Water move from Quaxly's line slows its target and every other
  enemy by one stage, at most once every 8 seconds.

Each final stage reaches four abilities:

- **Meowscarada:** Overgrow, Protean, Prankster and Magician.
- **Skeledirge:** Blaze, Unaware, Punk Rock and Flash Fire.
- **Quaquaval:** Torrent, Moxie, Dancer and Quick Feet.

They are met as rarely as the other starters: Sprigatito in the grassland in the
morning and day, Fuecoco on the savanna by day and evening, and Quaxly in the
swamp from morning to evening.

## The South Province

- **Lechonk** casts Stockpile on itself each time it eats a Berry.
- **Tarountula** makes an enemy that takes the field mid-fight wind up its first
  move 50% slower.
- **Nymble** pushes a target's wind-up back half a second with each contact
  move.
- **Pawmi** paralyses with its punches 20% of the time. Pawmo evolves once it
  has walked 1,000 steps as your buddy.
- **Tandemaus** sends each strike of a multi-hit move at a different enemy.
  One in a hundred grows into the **family of three** rather than four, and a
  wild Maushold is the family of three just as often.
- **Fidough** hands a Sitrus Berry to the teammate lowest on HP with free hands,
  every 20 seconds.
- **Smoliv** cures a teammate's major status whenever it heals them.
- **Nacli** answers a Water move with Salt Cure, at most once every 10 seconds.
- **Charcadet** winds up its next move 25% faster after landing a Fire move. It
  grows into Armarouge with the **Auspicious Armor** or Ceruledge with the
  **Malicious Armor**, both new.

## The West and East Provinces

- **Tadbulb** hits 1.5x with Electric moves a target a Water move landed on in
  the last 4 seconds.
- **Wattrel** hits 1.3x with Electric moves while Tailwind blows on its side.
- **Bramblin** winds up 50% faster after any wind move lands.
- **Toedscool** cures a teammate's major status when Grassy Terrain heals them.
- **Capsakid** is healed by a burn rather than hurt, and keeps its Attack.
- **Rellor** hands its held item to a teammate below 1/3 HP with empty hands,
  once a fight.
- **Flittle** taunts the target of each Psychic move it lands, at most once
  every 10 seconds per target.
- **Tinkatink** remakes each teammate's held item the first time it is used up.
- **Wiglett** makes Ground moves aimed at it miss, and gains Speed from each.

Bramblin, Toedscool, Rellor and Flittle are their line's end until their
evolutions are drawn.

## The coast and the roads

- **Finizen** takes the first blow each fight that would knock out a teammate,
  while it is above half HP.
- **Varoom** winds up faster the lower its HP, up to 30% faster.
- **Cyclizar** puts up a free Substitute the first time it falls below half HP.
- **Orthworm** lays Spikes with its Steel moves, at most once every 10 seconds.
- **Glimmet** makes every poisoned enemy wind up 20% slower.

## The north

- **Greavard** picks up a fallen teammate's item when its own hands are empty.
- **Cetoddle** gains Attack every 4 seconds while snow falls, up to +3.
- **Veluza** pays half for moves that cost HP.
- **Dondozo** swallows a teammate below 1/3 HP: it cannot be targeted for 4
  seconds and heals 1/4 of its HP.
- **Tatsugiri** makes its teammates' moves hit 1.2x while it is behind a
  Substitute or its own Protect. It is met in all three of its shapes.
- **Frigibax** gains +2 Defense and Special Defense after a move that leaves it
  open, until it acts again.
