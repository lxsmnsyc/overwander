# Galar's Zeniths

**54 new Zeniths**: one for every Galar pokemon that can have one, from
Rillaboom to Calyrex, and one for each Galarian final of an older line, from
Galarian Rapidash to Galarian Darmanitan. Each is reached by holding its own
**Zenith Shard**, which is dug up where its line lives. A Zenith fights 100
above its pokemon's base stat total, none of it in HP, and wears an ability its
line can never be born with. No two of Galar's share one.

## What to look at first

- **Calyrex keeps its Zenith on either steed.** Ice Rider and Shadow Rider
  Calyrex each have a Zenith of their own, all wearing Supreme Overlord.
- **Born and changed shapes share one shard.** Low Key Toxtricity, Antique
  Polteageist, Female Indeedee, Rapid Strike Urshifu and each of Alcremie's
  Sweets have a Zenith shape. So do Cramorant's catches, Noice Eiscue, Hangry
  Morpeko and Galarian Zen Darmanitan, which a Zenith changes into the way its
  pokemon does.
- **Some take a new type.** Zenith Grapploct is Fighting/Water, Zenith
  Polteageist Ghost/Grass, Zenith Cursola Ghost/Rock, Zenith Sirfetch'd
  Fighting/Flying and Zenith Pincurchin Electric/Water.
- **Galarian Mr. Mime has none yet.** It waits for Mr. Rime.
