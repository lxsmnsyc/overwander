- Sinnoh's three starters are in: the Turtwig, Chimchar and Piplup lines, nine species with their Diamond, Pearl, Platinum and HeartGold/SoulSilver learnsets, their sprites and a candy each. A new trainer may start from any of the twelve now.
- Each of the three carries a signature braced for one kind of blow. Bark Brace takes the first physical blow each fight at 1/2 and roots, Cinder Brace takes the first special one at 1/2 and puts the flare into its next move to land, and Crest Brace refuses the first status move aimed at it and takes a stage of Special Attack from the insult.
- Torterra also reaches Sturdy and Harvest, Infernape reaches Vital Spirit and Flash Fire, and Empoleon reaches Defiant and Filter, which are this registry's rather than the mainline's.
- Turtwig walks the temperate rainforests, montane forests and woodlands, Chimchar the montane forests and mountains, and Piplup the polar oceans, rocky coasts and glaciers.
