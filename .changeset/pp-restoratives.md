Added the Ether, Max Ether, Elixir and Max Elixir as held items. An Ether halves the first cooldown its holder's moves start and a Max Ether clears it, once a battle. An Elixir halves every cooldown, and a Max Elixir clears them, the moment all of its holder's moves are cooling down at once. They are not berries, so Unnerve does not stop them, and they turn up in the ground's item pool.
