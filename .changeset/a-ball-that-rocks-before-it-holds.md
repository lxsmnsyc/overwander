A thrown ball now rocks up to three times before it holds, and how far it got is what the meeting says about how close the throw came. The odds are unchanged: each shake is an independent check, and three of them multiply back to the old catch chance.

Some throws come out critical and hold on a single shake. How often depends on how many species the player has caught, and a Super Luck or Sniper buddy doubles it.
