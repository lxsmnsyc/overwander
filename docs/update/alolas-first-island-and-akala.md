# Alola's first island and Akala's roads

**Alola opens.** Its three starters, the families of Melemele's first roads,
meadow and hill, and of Akala's first roads and Brooklet Hill. **Alola's items**
arrive with them: the Z-Crystals and Memories turn up on walks, along with the
Beast Ball, the nectars and the terrain seeds. Each family carries **a signature
ability of its own**.

Everything from before is still here.

## Alola's three starters

| Family                       | Where                                                    | When              | Evolves        |
| ---------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------- | -------------- |
| Rowlet, Dartrix, Decidueye   | The tropical rainforest and the tropical seasonal forest | Evening and night | At 17, then 34 |
| Litten, Torracat, Incineroar | The grassland and the steppe                             | Evening and night | At 17, then 34 |
| Popplio, Brionne, Primarina  | The beach and the rocky coast, on land or in the water   | Morning and day   | At 17, then 34 |

**Each starter plays to a crowd**, counting up to 4:

- **Quill Audience**, Decidueye's. Its Speed is 1.1x for each enemy standing.
- **Heel Audience**, Incineroar's. Its Attack is 1.1x for each enemy standing.
- **Aria Audience**, Primarina's. Its Special Attack is 1.1x for each teammate
  standing.

## Melemele's first roads

| Family                       | Where                                                    | When            | Evolves                      |
| ---------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- | --------------- | ---------------------------- |
| Grubbin, Charjabug, Vikavolt | The tropical rainforest and the tropical seasonal forest | Morning and day | At 20, then by Thunder Stone |
| Pikipek, Trumbeak, Toucannon | Not in the wild yet                                      |                 | At 14, then 28               |
| Yungoos, Gumshoos            | Not in the wild yet                                      |                 | At 20, by morning or day     |

- **Pikipek and Yungoos wait on art** for Trumbeak, Toucannon and Gumshoos.
- **Drumroll**, Toucannon's. Its multi-hit moves strike 1 more time.
- **Score to Settle**, Gumshoos's. It hits 1.3x against whichever enemy last
  hit it.
- **Trickle Charge**, Vikavolt's. Its Special Attack rises 10% for each Electric
  move anybody lands, up to 1.5x.

## Melemele's meadow and hill

| Family             | Where                                                                | When            | Evolves            |
| ------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------- | ------------------ |
| Crabrawler         | The beach and the rocky coast                                        | Any hour        | By Ice Stone       |
| Crabominable       | The glacier and the alpine tundra                                    | Any hour        |                    |
| Oricorio           | One style per biome, see below                                       | Morning and day | Never              |
| Cutiefly, Ribombee | The grassland, the temperate forest and the tropical seasonal forest | Morning and day | At 25              |
| Rockruff, Lycanroc | The steppe, the grassland and the badlands                           | Any hour        | At 25, by the hour |

- **Oricorio dances in the style of where it was met**: Baile in the savanna,
  Pom-Pom in the grassland, Pa'u on the beach and Sensu in the tropical
  rainforest.
- **A nectar dances an Oricorio into another style.** It is spent on each
  change.
- **Rockruff grows into the Lycanroc of the hour it evolves at**: Midday by
  morning or day, Midnight by night, and Dusk in the evening. Each of the three
  is met in the wild at its own hour.

Their signatures:

- **Rebound Punch**, Crabominable's. The punch after one that missed or was
  blocked hits 1.5x.
- **Dizzy Twirl**, Oricorio's. It never hits itself while confused, and its
  moves hit 1.3x while it is.
- **Honey Share**, Ribombee's. The berries its teammates eat heal 1.5x as much
  and raise a stat 1 more stage.
- **Provoke**, Lycanroc's. The next single-target move of an enemy it hits is
  turned onto itself.

| ---------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------- | ------------------------------- |
| Wishiwashi                   | The coral reef, the ocean and the mangrove                              | Any hour        | Never                           |
| Mudbray, Mudsdale            | The savanna, the steppe and the grassland                               | Any hour        | At 30                           |

| Bounsweet, Steenee, Tsareena | The tropical rainforest and the tropical seasonal forest                | Morning and day | At 18, then at 29 knowing Stomp |

- **Wishiwashi fights as a school** from level 20, while it holds above 1/4 of
  its HP.

Their signatures:

- **Regroup**, Wishiwashi's. A lone Wishiwashi heals 1/8 of its HP each time it
  acts.
- **Heavy Hooves**, Mudsdale's. Its physical moves hit 1% harder for every 20 kg
  it weighs, up to 1.4x.
- **Bubble Ward**, Araquanid's. Its teammates take Fire moves at 0.75x and
  cannot be burned.
- **Orchid Guise**, Lurantis's. Enemies cannot aim at it until its first attack
  lands, and that attack hits 1.3x.
- **Trop Stride**, Tsareena's. Its kicking moves hit 1.3x.

## Alola's items

- **Z-Crystals and Memories turn up on walks**, one thin slot each. The
  Z-Crystals also come out of dust clouds.
- **A Silvally holding a Memory takes that type's shape.**
- **The Beast Ball** catches 5x on an Ultra Beast and 0.1x on anything else. It
  turns up on walks and is never sold.
- **The Terrain Extender** makes the terrain its holder lays last 1.6x as long.
- **The Electric, Grassy, Misty and Psychic Seeds** give their holder 1 stage of
  Defense or Special Defense once their terrain covers it, then are gone.
- **The four nectars, the N-Solarizer and the N-Lunarizer** are in the bag and
  turn up on walks.

## Abilities now built

- **Long Reach**: none of its moves make contact.
- **Liquid Voice**: its sound moves are Water moves.
- **Stakeout**: it hits 2x against an enemy that has not acted since it came
  onto the field.
- **Water Bubble**: Fire moves hit it at 0.5x, it cannot be burned, and its
  Water moves hit 2x.
- **Wimp Out**: below 1/2 HP it leaves the field for its strongest teammate.
- **Dancer** copies Revelation Dance.
- **Iron Fist** now powers up every punch, Mach Punch and Drain Punch among
  them, rather than only the first two generations' six.

## Other abilities

- **Decidueye** takes Sniper or Keen Eye, **Incineroar** Tough Claws or Iron
  Fist, and **Primarina** Cute Charm or Hydration.
- **Gumshoos** takes Scrappy, and **Vikavolt** Compound Eyes.
- **Oricorio** takes Tangled Feet, Keen Eye or Gale Wings, **Ribombee** Friend
  Guard, **Crabominable** Shell Armor, and **Rockruff** Own Tempo.
- **Wishiwashi** takes Swift Swim, Wimp Out or Anticipation, **Mudsdale**
  Steadfast, **Araquanid** Overcoat or Mirror Armor, and **Lurantis** Sharpness
  or Chlorophyll.
- **All of them learn their moves** by level, machine, tutor and egg, as they
  do in Sun, Moon, Ultra Sun and Ultra Moon.
