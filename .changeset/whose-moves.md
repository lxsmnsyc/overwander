Item and ability descriptions say whose moves they boost. Wise Glasses now reads "Its holder’s special moves hit 1.1x." rather than "1.1x damage from special moves.", which read as damage taken. The same fix covers Muscle Band, Expert Belt, Metronome, Soul Dew, Life Orb, the gems, incenses, plates and type boosters, and abilities such as Blaze, Iron Fist and Technician.
