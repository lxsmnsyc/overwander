Signature abilities for four more Kanto families:

- Brood Fury (Nidoran female): her moves hit 1.35x while an ally is hurt, and 1.5x once an ally has fallen.
- Warlord (Nidoran male): each move it lands that is not the one it landed last hits 10% harder, up to 1.4x.
- Wishing Well (Clefairy): each time it acts, the ally lowest on HP heals 1/12 of theirs.
- Nine Tails (Vulpix): its Special Attack rises 8% for each hit it has taken, up to 9 hits.
