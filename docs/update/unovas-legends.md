# Unova's legends

**Nine legendaries and mythicals** from the fifth generation: the three dragons
of the Tao trio, the three forces of nature, and Unova's three mythicals. Kyurem
can be **fused** with either dragon, and each mythical has a **relic** that
calls it out.

Everything from before is still here.

## The Tao trio

- **Dragonspiral Tower** stands in the taiga and the tundra, and holds Reshiram
  or Zekrom, one of the two each time.
- **Giant Chasm** stands in the tundra and the glacier, and holds Kyurem.
- **Each of the three is also a wild spawn** where its lair stands.
- **Turboblaze and Teravolt** are new. Both do what Mold Breaker does: the
  target's abilities cannot hinder their moves.

### Fusing Kyurem

**The DNA Splicers** are new. Used on a Kyurem, they ask which dragon to fold
in.

- **The dragon picked goes inside the fusion rather than being spent.** It
  leaves the boxes until the two are taken apart again, which the same Splicers
  do.
- **The fused pokemon names what is inside it**, so nothing is lost.
- **Black Kyurem** takes Zekrom's Attack and Teravolt, and **White Kyurem**
  takes Reshiram's Special Attack and Turboblaze.
- **Neither can be folded** out of a fight, an egg or the buddy slot.

## The forces of nature

**Tornadus, Thundurus and Landorus** all stand in the grassland at any hour, out
of the **Abundant Shrine**. One lair holds all three, the way the burned tower
holds the beasts.

**Each one's second shape is written but cannot be reached yet.** The mirror
that turns one into the other works the way the Meteorite does for a Deoxys, and
it waits until the other two shapes are drawn.

## The mythicals

- **Victini** stands on the rocky coast, **Meloetta** in the woodland and
  **Genesect** in the desert, at any hour.
- **Three new lairs**: Liberty Garden for Victini, the Abyssal Ruins for
  Meloetta, and the P2 Laboratory for Genesect. No biome hosts one, so the world
  never puts a mythical on the map.
- **Three new relics**, found in the rarest band of the overworld item pool and
  nowhere else. The **Liberty Pass** calls Victini, the **Music Box** calls
  Meloetta and the **Colress Machine** calls Genesect. Each is spent when its
  raid starts.
- **Relic Song** turns a Meloetta from its singing shape into its dancing one,
  and back again.
- **A Genesect holding a Drive fights in that Drive's shape.** The Drive already
  set the type of its Techno Blast.

## Nine signature abilities

**The Tao trio share one idea, told three ways.** Each hits 1.3x against a
certain kind of enemy.

| Dragon   | Signature    | Hits 1.3x against an enemy carrying |
| -------- | ------------ | ----------------------------------- |
| Reshiram | Truth Creed  | A status condition                  |
| Zekrom   | Ideal Creed  | A raised stat stage                 |
| Kyurem   | Hollow Creed | Neither                             |

**The forces of nature each lift one type** for their whole team, so three
standing together lift three.

| Force     | Signature | Its team throws at 1.3x |
| --------- | --------- | ----------------------- |
| Tornadus  | Windfall  | Flying moves            |
| Thundurus | Stormfall | Electric moves          |
| Landorus  | Landfall  | Ground moves            |

**The mythicals each have one of their own.**

- **Winner's Share**, for Victini. Each enemy that faints gives its whole team 1
  stage of Attack and 1 of Special Attack.
- **Countertune**, for Meloetta. Changing shape swaps its Attack and Special
  Attack stages, and swaps its two defences.
- **Overclock**, for Genesect. It casts 25% faster above 1/2 HP, and at or below
  that it loses 1/16 of its HP each time it acts.

## Two abilities that now work

- **Victory Star.** The holder's whole team, itself included, throws moves at
  1.1x accuracy.
- **Dancer.** Whenever anybody uses a dance move, the holder casts the same one
  straight after, for free. Rain Dance does not count as a dance.

## What else they can be born with

The mainline gives most of them one ability, so each carries three of this
registry's own.

| Legend    | Can also be born with                     |
| --------- | ----------------------------------------- |
| Reshiram  | Flash Fire, Pressure or Serene Grace      |
| Zekrom    | Motor Drive, Pressure or Sheer Force      |
| Kyurem    | Snow Warning, Ice Body or Intimidate      |
| Tornadus  | Wind Rider or Snow Warning                |
| Thundurus | Lightning Rod or Drizzle                  |
| Landorus  | Harvest or Sand Stream                    |
| Victini   | Flash Fire, Inner Focus or Magic Guard    |
| Meloetta  | Soundproof, Healer or Dancer              |
| Genesect  | Analytic, Synchronize or Adaptability     |

## Where to look first

- **Taiga and tundra**, for Dragonspiral Tower, and **tundra and glacier** for
  Giant Chasm.
- **Grassland**, for the Abundant Shrine.
- **The item pool's rarest band**, for a relic to call a mythical out.
