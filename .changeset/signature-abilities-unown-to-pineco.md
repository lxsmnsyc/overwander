Signature abilities for the next four Johto families:

- Ruinous Script (Unown): held items do nothing on the enemy side while it stands.
- Backlash (Wobbuffet): it banks 1/4 of every hit it takes, and pays the bank back to whoever last struck it when it next acts.
- Ambidextrous (Girafarig): its moves are worked out from whichever of its Attack and Special Attack is higher.
- Shrapnel (Pineco): when it faints, it casts Spikes and Toxic Spikes onto the enemy side.
