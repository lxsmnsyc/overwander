Signature abilities for the next four Johto families:

- Good Omen (Togepi): no move from its side can miss while it stands.
- Prophecy (Natu): it casts Future Sight at an enemy as it arrives on the field.
- Live Wire (Mareep): it casts Thunder Wave at an enemy as it arrives on the field.
- Spillover (Marill): healing past its full HP is thrown at an enemy as damage rather than wasted.
