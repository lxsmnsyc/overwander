# Dynamax and the new raids

**Dynamax and Gigantamax** arrive. Like Mega Evolution and Z-Moves, they
happen on their own, once a side a fight, with nothing to press, so a player
and a trainer get them the same way. Three new kinds of raid come with them:
**Max Raids**, **Alpha raids** and **Noble raids**, and Totems get a landmark of
their own.

## Dynamax

- **A pokemon holding a Dynamax Band** Dynamaxes the first time it drops below
  1/2 HP. Only one pokemon a side does it each fight.
- **It lasts 3 moves.** Its next 3 moves are thrown at full size, then it
  shrinks back, keeping its share of HP.
- **Its HP doubles,** current and max.
- **It stands in the middle of its team**, since it cannot be sent away.
- **It looks the part.** It is drawn larger, its body washed red and pulsing
  (a deeper magenta for a Gigantamax), with a red glow round it on any ground
  and three small red storm clouds swirling over its head for as long as it
  stays grown. A raid boss looks the same.
- **A pokemon holding a Mega Stone or a Z-Crystal never Dynamaxes.** A side's
  Mega, Z-Move and Dynamax are each once a fight.

### What it shrugs off

- It cannot flinch, and nothing sends it away: Roar, Whirlwind, Dragon Tail,
  Circle Throw, a Red Card or an Eject Button.
- Encore, Disable, Torment and Instruct do not take, and a Choice item does
  not lock it.
- OHKO moves and the weight moves (Low Kick, Grass Knot, Heat Crash, Heavy Slam)
  fail against it.
- **Behemoth Blade, Behemoth Bash and Dynamax Cannon** hit it 2x.

## Max Moves

Each damaging move goes off as the Max Move of its type, and each status move as
**Max Guard**, a guard that nothing gets through.

| Leaves behind                      | Max Moves                                                     |
| ---------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Weather                            | Flare (sun), Geyser (rain), Hailstorm (hail), Rockfall (sand) |
| Terrain                            | Lightning, Overgrowth, Mindstorm, Starfall                    |
| A stage off every foe              | Strike, Flutterby, Phantasm, Wyrmwind, Darkness               |
| A stage onto the user's whole side | Knuckle, Steelspike, Ooze, Quake, Airstream                   |

A Max Move hits harder the stronger the move it replaced, and goes through any
guard but Max Guard, breaking it.

## Gigantamax

- **A pokemon with the Gigantamax Factor** throws its species' **G-Max Move**
  in place of that type's Max Move when it Dynamaxes. There are 33 of them, each
  with its own effect: G-Max Wildfire leaves the field burning, G-Max Gold Rush
  scatters coins, G-Max Finale heals the whole side, and so on.
- **Max Mushrooms** give the factor to a pokemon whose species can Gigantamax.
  They are dug up in the prized band.
- **The factor stays** through trades and evolution, and shows on the catch
  sheet.

The Dynamax Band is dug up beside the Mega Stones, and turns up in dust clouds.

## Max Raids

- **A new landmark, the Max Raid**, stands out in the country: a ring of rock
  round a hole with a pillar of red light rising out of it, purple and brighter
  over a Gigantamax. The light carries at night and goes dark once you have
  cleared it for the window.
- **The boss is a final stage of a line the biome spawns**, a new one every raid
  window, **Dynamaxed for the whole fight**. When its species can Gigantamax it
  always does, so its G-Max Move goes off.
- **The lobby works like a lair's.** Up to 20 players, the host starts it.
- **Clearing one pays 50,000 gold** and the boss at level 40, plus 1 or 2 Max
  Mushrooms and, one clear in 10, a Dynamax Band. A Gigantamax boss caught from
  the raid keeps the Gigantamax Factor.
- **Totems have a landmark of their own**, a trial site with the Totem's gold
  aura over it, holding a Totem every window. Lairs always hold their own
  legendary or shadow again.

## Alpha raids

- **A new landmark, the Alpha**, stands in open country. It holds an oversized
  pokemon the tile's biome spawns, at any stage of its line.
- **An Alpha calls its kind back.** At 3/4, 1/2 and 1/4 of its HP, it brings
  its side back up to 6 of its own species.
- **Beating one pays 50,000 gold**, and the Alpha waits to be caught at level
  40 with 3 random stats at 31.

## Noble raids

- **A new landmark, the Noble Arena**, stands in open country. Half the time it
  holds one of Hisui's five Nobles in its own biomes: Kleavor, Hisuian Lilligant,
  Hisuian Arcanine, Hisuian Electrode or Hisuian Avalugg. Otherwise it holds a
  final stage of the tile's biome.
- **A Noble cannot faint.** Its HP is its Frenzy. It staggers at 3/4, 1/2 and
  1/4, and every 10 seconds it bursts on the whole party after a 1.8 second
  wind-up, which a guard can answer.
- **It is calmed, not caught,** once its Frenzy is drained.
- **Balms soothe it.** Each player packs up to 3 into a Noble raid, and their
  party throws one every 10 seconds to soothe 1/50 of the Frenzy. Balms are
  sold at the medicine counter.
- **A calmed Noble pays 55,000 gold** and 2 or 3 Balms. One of the five canon
  Nobles also leaves its treasure: Kleavor a Black Augurite, Lilligant a Sun
  Stone, Arcanine a Fire Stone, Electrode a Leaf Stone and Avalugg a
  Never-Melt Ice.

## World generation

Every new landmark takes a share of rolls that were already landmarks, at most
one of each to a chunk, so nothing else on any map moves:

- **About one item cache in five** on dry ground above ground is now a Max Raid,
  and 2 more of the cache's 15 shares on the surface are Alpha sites.
- **About one trainer in five** on dry ground is now a Totem, and the next 1 of
  the trainer's 10 shares on the surface is a Noble Arena.

## Not here yet

- **Gigantamax shapes** wait for the collection to draw them. A Gigantamax
  pokemon is drawn as its own shape, larger.
- **Coalossal's G-Max Volcalith and Copperajah's G-Max Steelsurge** are ready
  for the day their lines are drawn.
