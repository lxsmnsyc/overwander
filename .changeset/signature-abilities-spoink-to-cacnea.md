Signature abilities for four more Hoenn families:

- Stored Bounce (Spoink): half of each hit it takes is stored, up to 1/2 its HP, and the next move it lands deals the lot on top.
- Unique Spots (Spinda): it arrives with 2 stages in one random stat and 1 stage off another, rolled fresh each time.
- Antlion Pit (Trapinch): any enemy move that misses it costs that enemy 1/8 of their HP.
- Patient Stalk (Cacnea): its moves hit 10% harder for each second it has stood idle, up to 50%, spent on the next one it lands.
