Signature abilities for the next four Johto families:

- Momentum (Phanpy): its moves hit 1.1x for each move it has landed since taking the field, up to 1.5x.
- Mind Fog (Stantler): enemy Special Attack counts 0.85x while it stands.
- Palette (Smeargle): its moves take the type of the last move that hit it.
- Cowbell (Miltank): it casts Heal Bell over its side as it arrives on the field.
