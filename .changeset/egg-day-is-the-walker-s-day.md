The featured family's 1.2x egg paces now turn over at the player's own midnight, the way the day's spawns, shiny odds, catch odds and candy already do, rather than at UTC midnight.

The rotating quest board is the player's day too: the three dailies and the weekly hunt turn over at their local midnight, and the Featured slot names the family their own world is crowding.
