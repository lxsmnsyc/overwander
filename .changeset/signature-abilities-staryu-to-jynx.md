Signature abilities for four more Kanto families:

- Core Reset (Staryu): each time it acts, one stat drop on it is undone.
- Mimed Barrier (Mr. Mime): it casts Light Screen over its side as it arrives, and physical moves hit it at 1.15x itself.
- Clean Cut (Scyther): its critical hits ignore every stage on the target's defending stat.
- Icy Charm (Jynx): its moves hit 1.5x against a target that is infatuated or confused.
