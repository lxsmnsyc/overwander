Signature abilities for four more Hoenn families:

- Fearless Dive (Taillow): its moves hit 1.3x against a target on a higher share of its HP than it has.
- Bill Carry (Wingull): it arrives carrying a Sitrus Berry, handed to the neediest empty-handed ally or kept if there is none.
- Empath (Ralts): its Special Attack counts 1.3x while any ally is below 1/2 HP.
- Surface Walk (Surskit): it takes no damage from hazards or from weather.
