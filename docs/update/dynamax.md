# Dynamax

**Dynamax and Gigantamax** arrive. Like Mega Evolution and Z-Moves, they
happen on their own, once a side a fight, with nothing to press, so a player
and a trainer get them the same way.

## Dynamax

- **A pokemon holding a Dynamax Band** Dynamaxes the first time it drops below
  1/2 HP. Only one pokemon a side does it each fight.
- **It lasts 3 moves.** Its next 3 moves are thrown at full size, then it
  shrinks back, keeping its share of HP.
- **Its HP doubles,** current and max.
- **It stands in the middle of its team**, drawn larger with a red glow, since
  it cannot be sent away.
- **A pokemon holding a Mega Stone or a Z-Crystal never Dynamaxes.** A side's
  Mega, Z-Move and Dynamax are each once a fight.

### What it shrugs off

- It cannot flinch, and nothing sends it away: Roar, Whirlwind, Dragon Tail,
  Circle Throw, a Red Card or an Eject Button.
- Encore, Disable, Torment and Instruct do not take, and a Choice item does
  not lock it.
- OHKO moves and the weight moves (Low Kick, Grass Knot, Heat Crash, Heavy Slam)
  fail against it.
- **Behemoth Blade, Behemoth Bash and Dynamax Cannon** hit it 2x.

## Max Moves

Each damaging move goes off as the Max Move of its type, and each status move as
**Max Guard**, a guard that nothing gets through.

| Leaves behind                      | Max Moves                                                     |
| ---------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Weather                            | Flare (sun), Geyser (rain), Hailstorm (hail), Rockfall (sand) |
| Terrain                            | Lightning, Overgrowth, Mindstorm, Starfall                    |
| A stage off every foe              | Strike, Flutterby, Phantasm, Wyrmwind, Darkness               |
| A stage onto the user's whole side | Knuckle, Steelspike, Ooze, Quake, Airstream                   |

A Max Move hits harder the stronger the move it replaced, and goes through any
guard but Max Guard, breaking it.

## Gigantamax

- **A pokemon with the Gigantamax Factor** throws its species' **G-Max Move**
  in place of that type's Max Move when it Dynamaxes. There are 33 of them, each
  with its own effect: G-Max Wildfire leaves the field burning, G-Max Gold Rush
  scatters coins, G-Max Finale heals the whole side, and so on.
- **Max Mushrooms** give the factor to a pokemon whose species can Gigantamax.
  They are dug up in the prized band.
- **The factor stays** through trades and evolution, and shows on the catch
  sheet.

The Dynamax Band is dug up beside the Mega Stones, and turns up in dust clouds.

## Not here yet

- **Gigantamax shapes** wait for the collection to draw them. A Gigantamax
  pokemon is drawn as its own shape, larger.
- **Coalossal's G-Max Volcalith and Copperajah's G-Max Steelsurge** are ready
  for the day their lines are drawn.
- **Max Raids** come next.
