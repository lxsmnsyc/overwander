Built parties give each member a job, and the top trainers bring a Mega:

- A built party fields a special core, a physical core, a healer, a protector, a redirector and a field control.
- The healer is the frailest member able to heal, cure or raise the team, and the redirector is the bulkiest.
- Ace trainers, gym leaders, executives, the Elite Four, champions and legends may give one member a Mega Stone, a core first.
