# Translation terms

The words a batch translates once and every later batch reuses. See [translations.md](translations.md) for the procedure. Japanese is written in kana, Spanish as Spain uses it.

## Money and counts

- `ui_gold` writes money as おかね {amount} in Japanese and {amount} de dinero in Spanish, after the verdict's `verdict_gold`.
- Where only a number and a short unit fit (the price chip on an auction square, `auction_price_chip`), Spanish writes {amount} ₽, the games' money sign. Money amounts pass through `formatNumber` so they group by locale (25.000 in Spanish).
- A Japanese count of pokemon takes ひき (`{count}ひき`), a count of moves つ, of times かい, of waiting things けん, of items, boxes and candies こ, of steps ほ, of species しゅるい.
- Japanese quotes a line in 「」, Spanish in «».

## Lists inside a sentence

- `catch_and`, `catch_or` and `dex_fossil_and` are the joiners an evolution's conditions, its times of day and a fossil's halves are listed with. English keeps its words; Japanese lists conditions with 、 after しんかの じょうけん:, Spanish with y and o.
- English puts an article before an item or species named in a sentence (`withArticle`); the other languages name it bare. `articled()` in `catch-dialog/describe.tsx` and `game/items/use-item.ts` decides by locale.

## Language names

The picker names each language in itself: English, 日本語, Español. 日本語 is written in kanji, as every language list writes it, though the rest of the Japanese text is kana.

## Stats, stages and Zeniths

- The stats and stages use the games' words, in `text/<locale>/stats.yaml` and `stages.yaml`: Attack, Defense, Sp. Attack, Sp. Defense, Speed read こうげき, ぼうぎょ, とくこう, とくぼう, すばやさ and Ataque, Defensa, Ataque Esp., Defensa Esp., Velocidad. HP is PS in Spanish. Evasion and Accuracy (stages only) read かいひ, めいちゅう and Evasión, Precisión.
- A Zenith is named from a template in `text/<locale>/zeniths.yaml`, the way the games name a Mega: ゼニス{holder} in Japanese (no gap, as メガ is written) and Cenit-{holder} in Spanish (as Mega-). The holder is the pokemon's name in that locale.

## Signature ability names

The names of the signature abilities (`text/<locale>/abilities/signature/`) are coined, since the games name none of them. A name reads like an official ability name of its language: short kana in Japanese, title case in the style of the Spanish games.
