# The desert and the Relic Castle

**Ten more families** from the fifth generation: the crocodiles, cacti and
crabs of Unova's desert, the gang and the coffins of the ruins under it, and
the rubbish Castelia leaves behind. **Two mainline abilities that never worked
now do.**

Everything from before is still here.

## The desert

**Sandile, Krokorok and Krookodile** cross the desert and the badlands by day.
Sandile becomes Krokorok at 29 and Krookodile at 40.

**Darumaka and Darmanitan** walk the same country. Darumaka becomes Darmanitan
at 35, and **Darmanitan sits down into its Zen shape below half its HP** and
stands back up above it, which turns what it hits with into what it thinks
with and makes it far harder to shift. The shape is its own dex entry.

**Maractus** stands in both, alone.

**Dwebble and Crustle** are written but not out in the world yet, since nobody
has drawn a Crustle.

## The ruins and the city's edge

**Scraggy and Scrafty** work the badlands and the steppe after dark. Scraggy
becomes Scrafty at 39.

**Sigilyph** patrols the desert and the badlands by day.

**Yamask and Cofagrigus** haunt the same places at night. Yamask becomes
Cofagrigus at 34.

**Trubbish and Garbodor** turn up through the badlands and the bog after dark.
Trubbish becomes Garbodor at 36.

## The three elemental monkeys

**Pansage and Simisage, Pansear and Simisear, and Panpour and Simipour**, each
evolving on a stone: a Leaf Stone, a Fire Stone and a Water Stone.

**None of the three is out in the world yet.** They are staged as a set, and
nobody has drawn a Simisear or a Simipour.

## Ten signature abilities

Each family brings one of its own, and the monkeys share a shape.

- **Leaf Crown, Ember Tuft and Geyser Tail**, for the three monkeys. The first
  time a blow takes it under 1/2 HP it spends its tuft on the whole enemy side,
  once a battle: the Pansage line seeds them, the Pansear line burns them and
  the Panpour line traps them in a whirlpool.
- **Death Roll**, for the Sandile family. Its contact moves hit 1.25x against a
  target it has already landed one on.
- **Glancing Blow**, for the Darumaka family. A move of its that misses still
  deals 1/4 of what it would have.
- **Dry Spell**, for Maractus. While no weather is up, its moves hit 1.3x and
  it heals 1/16 of its HP each time it acts.
- **Slab**, for the Dwebble family. The rock it carries takes the first 1/4 of
  its HP worth of damage each fight.
- **Gang Up**, for the Scraggy family. Its moves hit 10% harder for each
  teammate still standing with it, up to 1.3x.
- **Ward Circle**, for Sigilyph. Nothing can lay hazards on its side while it
  stands, and it sweeps any away as it arrives.
- **Death Mask**, for the Yamask family. Whoever finishes one of its teammates
  loses 2 stages of its best stat.
- **Litterbug**, for the Trubbish family. It casts Toxic Spikes onto the enemy
  side as it arrives.

## Two abilities that now work

- **Mummy.** Whoever lands a contact move on a Yamask loses one of their own
  abilities and catches Mummy in its place. The line had no working ability at
  all before this.
- **Perish Body.** Whoever reaches into a Cofagrigus is marked to faint in 3
  turns, and the coffin goes with them.

## What else they can be born with

**Each end of a line reaches a fourth.** A Krookodile can be born with Rough
Skin, a Maractus with Rattled, a Crustle with Solid Rock, a Scrafty with Guts,
a Sigilyph with Compound Eyes, and a Cofagrigus with Cursed Body, Pressure or
Perish Body. A Simisage reaches Sap Sipper or Harvest, a Simisear Flash Fire or
Moxie, and a Simipour Water Absorb or Analytic.

## A fix along the way

- **A Grass type shrugs off Leech Seed however the seed reaches it**, not only
  when the move itself put it there.

## Where to look first

- **Desert and badlands by day**, for Sandile, Darumaka, Maractus and Sigilyph.
- **The same ground at night**, for Yamask.
- **Badlands and steppe after dark**, for Scraggy, with Trubbish in the bog
  beside them.
