Signature abilities for the Regi trio and the Eon pair:

- Stone Seal (Regirock): for 8 seconds it deals and takes 0.5x, then deals 1.25x and gains 2 stages of Defense.
- Frost Seal (Regice): the same seal, waking into 2 stages of Special Defense.
- Iron Seal (Registeel): the same seal, waking into 2 stages of Attack.
- Eon Shield (Latias): its allies take 0.8x damage while it stands, itself excluded.
- Eon Lance (Latios): its moves deal 1.25x and count Reflect and Light Screen for nothing.
