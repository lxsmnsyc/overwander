Signature abilities for four more Kanto families:

- Relentless (Spearow): each move it lands on the same target as the last hits 10% harder, up to 1.4x.
- Constrict (Ekans): a contact move it lands corners the target and makes its next cast 20% longer.
- Chain Lightning (Pikachu): an Electric move it lands arcs to one other standing enemy for 1/3 of the damage.
- Curl Up (Sandshrew): each hit it takes rolls it 10% tighter for more Defense, up to 5, and the next physical move it lands spends the roll on power.
