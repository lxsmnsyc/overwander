# Log: i18n-people-npcs

- Extraction first, in its own commit (213900fe7). Under `src/overworld/npcs` 17 files changed; 122 messages `npc_*` went into `src/i18n/messages/en.json`, all in `shop.tsx`, `shared.ts`, `lessons.ts` and the `interact` script of each role (breeder, channeler, daycare-lady, dojo-master, fossil-maniac, fossil-scientist, groomer, hyper-trainer, kurt, move-reminder, move-tutor, nurse-joy, trader). Chef, geologist and vendor share `shop.tsx`; rocket-grunt and trainer have no script. Numbers and names are parameters (`{fee}`, `{steps}`, `{ability}`, `{from}`/`{to}`), a composed line is one message (`npc_trader_line`, `npc_groomer_done`), and the two lines with bold markup (`npc_sum_gold`, `npc_kurt_sum`) are split into the part before the bold total and the total itself. The unit on the counter chip is a message too (`npc_unit_gold`, `npc_heart_scales` as a plural, `npc_unit_apricorns`).
- Reused rather than added: `ui_gold` for every "{n} gold", `sayFriendship` (catch-summary) for the groomer's mood word, the shared `ui_level_name` pattern was not usable because the trader's English order differs.
- English output stayed identical in the extraction commit. A second, separate commit (d58739c2b) passes prices, fees and egg steps through `formatNumber`, as the Hyper Trainer already did, so a number of 1,000 or more now groups ("12,500", "12.500" in Spanish). Under 1,000 nothing changes. This is the only English change.
- `Lesson.counted` in `lessons.ts` became a function of the count (`"{count} forgotten"`, `"{count} to learn"`): a string appended after a number cannot be reordered for Japanese.
- Translation: messages by role, in the voice of each person (Daycare Lady "dear" is あなた / cariño; the Dojo Master is gruff, Joy polite, Kurt an old craftsman, the Channeler formal). Japanese is kana only, with 。 and fullwidth ！？; Spanish is Spain's, with Nv. for Lv., "Mochila" for the Bag, "Oscuro" for a Shadow pokemon, "Escama Corazón" for the Heart Scale. Terms reused from the list: きとうし / Médium, どうじょうの しはん / Maestro del Dojo, かせきの けんきゅういん / Científica de Fósiles, ガンテツ / Kurt, わざおもいだし, わざおしえ, じかんわく / franja for "this while", あいぼう / compañero for the buddy, わく / hueco for slots, こたいち for the value the Hyper Trainer raises.
- Fossil Scientist in Spanish keeps the term list's feminine (la Científica), so no Spanish line of hers says "he" or "she"; the English "nothing he can open" is "nada que se pueda abrir".
- Counts: messages 122 (en, ja, es), npcs.yaml 18 people (name, description, quote for all; `spent` for the 7 who serve once a window: Breeder, Daycare Lady, Groomer, Fossil Maniac, Channeler, Trader, Hyper Trainer), vendor-stalls 6, decorations 14.
- Collision check (script folding katakana onto hiragana, dropping spaces and accents): every NPC, stall and decoration name against every official ability, move, item and species name of its locale. No exact match. Substring hits are plain scenery nouns inside long official names (こおり in こおりのいし, Roca in Cabeza Roca) and Puesto de Poké Balls around the item Poké Ball; none is a name.
- Names reread for a second reading or meaning, as their own pass:
  - ja: トレーダー (Trader) was written first and renamed こうかんや: トレーダー is one kana from トレーナー and reads as the finance word. まつ (Pine) became マツ, since まつ also means "to wait". はな (Flower) became はなばな, since はな also reads as the nose. Kept after a look: トリマー (Groomer, the pet-groomer word), あきんど (Vendor, only 商人), コック (the official Cook class, also a tap word but official), きとうし, ちしつがくしゃ, ハイパートレーナー, やたい, こおり (Ice, also 郡 but a scenery noun beside it), き (Tree, the term list's word in ミツの き).
  - es: Comerciante (Trader) was written first and renamed Intercambiador: Comerciante names the vendor, chef and geologist as well. Cañas (Reed) became Juncos: cañas are beers in Spain. Kept: Peluquero, Vendedor, Cocinero, Geólogo, Superentrenador, Peñasco, Tocón, Puesto de ... for the stalls.
- Tests: `test/data/locale-people-places.test.ts` gains three checks (every person, stall and scenery line written in ja and es with the fields English has; descriptions end on 。 or .; no two people share a name in a locale). The existing parity test covers the new YAML; `test/i18n/messages.test.ts` covers the 122 messages without a change. `test/data/official-names.test.ts` (no kanji in ja text) passes.
- Lint: the first run of the new test failed `no-unsafe-type-assertion`; the checks now read the YAML through valibot schemas (03c677642).

Trader in es is Comerciante, chosen by the user, so the Intercambiador rename is undone. Tree scenery in ja is じゅもく. Groomer in es stays Peluquero. わざおもいだし is official and stays.
- Japanese fixes: Groomer quote names the pokemon as the subject; Kurt's quote says つんだ (picked, harvested) instead of えらんだ; the stray space in the groomer's refusal is gone; the breeder's refusal no longer says the player bred with him (この じかんわくに もう かけあわせたか).
- Spanish fixes (Spain): "Sin cargo" is "Gratis" for Nurse Joy and Kurt, and Kurt's "Trae acá" is "Dámelos"; the Trader's "tipo" (read as an elemental type) is "rareza" in the description, quote and empty line; "uno del que no puedes separarte"; Fossil Scientist is Científico (the English lines say "he"); the Hyper Trainer's "valor" is "IV", as the term list has it.

- Money: es `npc_price_note` read "{price} ₽" beside "de dinero" everywhere else; it follows `ui_gold` now ("{price} de dinero"). ja already writes おかね {price} like `ui_gold`.
- The trader's `{band}` stays English in ja and es until `i18n-places` writes `spawn-rarities.yaml`; noted under stubs.
- Kept: npc_daycare_done says cariño, which the English line lacks, because it matches her voice.
