The summary after a raid is laid out team first. Each party is a full-size row of six, drawn as the fight left it with the fainted greyed and each one's HP where it ended. Beside it, a plate says where the team placed, who fielded it, how much it dealt and its share of the damage, with your own row in green. The boss stands last with what it dealt back, and the best single pokemon is named under the list.
