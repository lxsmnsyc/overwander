Signature abilities for four more Kanto families:

- Gallop (Ponyta): its Speed rises 10% each time it acts, up to 1.5x, and any hit it takes clears it.
- Delayed Reaction (Slowpoke): it feels half of each hit at once and the other half 4 seconds later.
- Repulsion Field (Magnemite): special moves hit at 0.9x for everybody on the field, its own included.
- Leek Duelist (Farfetch'd): critical stage 2 higher and criticals 1.25x harder, and everything hits it 1.25x in return.
