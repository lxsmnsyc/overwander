Signature abilities for the first four Johto families:

- Petal Bed (Chikorita): its allies heal 1/16 of their HP each time they act.
- Ignition (Cyndaquil): it casts Will-O-Wisp at an enemy as it arrives on the field.
- Gator Grip (Totodile): a contact move it lands casts Bind on that target.
- Sentry (Sentret): nobody on its side can be hit by a critical hit while it stands.
