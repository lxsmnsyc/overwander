Signature abilities for four more Hoenn families:

- Mycelium (Shroomish): anything carrying poison, sleep, paralysis, a burn or a freeze takes 1.2x from every blow while it stands.
- Wide Swing (Slakoth): its physical moves reach every enemy on the field, not only the one it aimed at.
- Vanishing Act (Nincada): for 1 second after it lands a move, single-target moves aimed at it miss.
- Echo Chamber (Whismur): a sound move it lands echoes 2 seconds later for 1/4 of the damage it dealt.
