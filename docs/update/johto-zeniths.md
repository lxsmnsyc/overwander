# Johto's Zeniths

**40 new Zeniths**, one for every Johto pokemon that can have one, from
Typhlosion to Celebi. Each is reached by holding its own **Zenith Shard**, which
is dug up where its line lives. A Zenith fights 100 above its pokemon's base
stat total and wears an ability its line can never be born with; no two of
Johto's share one.

## What to look at first

- **Zenith Shuckle** wears Simple, so one Shell Smash is four stages each way.
- **Zenith Sudowoodo** finally is a tree: Rock and Grass, and Illusion hides it as the last of its team until it is hit.
- **Zenith Magcargo** wears Steam Engine: douse it in Fire or Water and its Speed climbs six stages.
- **Zenith Delibird** brings the snow with it.

Unown is left without a Zenith, alongside Ditto.
