Signature abilities for the last three Johto families, finishing the region. The tower duo share one signature told either side of a fall:

- Silver Aegis (Lugia): the first blow that would finish a teammate leaves it on 1 HP instead. Once per battle.
- Rainbow Rekindling (Ho-Oh): the first teammate to fall gets back up on 1/3 of its HP. Once per battle.
- Timeline Split (Celebi): the first time it drops below 1/2 HP, every stat drop on it is undone and every status cleared.
