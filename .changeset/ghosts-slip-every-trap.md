- A Ghost type can always be swapped out, whatever an opponent has trapped it with: binding moves, Mean Look, Spider Web, Block, Thousand Waves, Fairy Lock, Arena Trap, Shadow Tag, Magnet Pull, Latch On, Tentacle Grasp, Chase Down and Root Hold.
- What a pokemon does to itself still holds a Ghost in place, so Ingrain roots it like anything else.
