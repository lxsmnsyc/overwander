Added the Exp Charm and the Oval Charm, two key items the buddy carries. The Exp Charm pays one more candy of the caught pokemon's family on every catch. The Oval Charm halves the steps an egg needs when it is picked up, and does not stack with a Flame Body buddy. Both turn up in the rarest band of the ground's item pool. The Mark Charm is gone.
