Signature abilities for four more Kanto families:

- Second Head (Doduo): every third move it lands strikes again at once for 50% power.
- Sleek Hide (Seel): contact moves hit it at 0.75x and everything else at 1.1x.
- Corrosive Ooze (Grimer): whoever lands a contact move on it has their held item destroyed.
- Spike Shell (Shellder): contact moves hit it at 0.5x, and whoever lands one takes 1/8 of their own HP.
