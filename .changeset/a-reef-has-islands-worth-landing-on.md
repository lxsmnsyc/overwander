A coral reef's islands have life on them: Krabby, Corphish, Wingull, Shellos and Slowpoke by day, with Kingler, Crawdaunt, Pelipper, Gastrodon, Slowbro, Shuckle and Slowking behind them, and the crabs, slugs and Shuckle alone after dark. The Unowns were the only thing standing there before, which made an alphabet easier to meet than it should be.
