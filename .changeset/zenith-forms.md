- New: Zenith forms. A final-stage pokemon with no Mega and no other held-item shape can reach its Zenith by holding its own Zenith Shard: 100 more base stat points, an ability worn on top of its own, and sometimes a new type.
- One pokemon on a team reaches its Zenith in a fight, separately from the team's Mega, and a Zenith floats its shard above it in battle.
- Each shard is found only where its line lives, in the prized band.
- First Zenith: Zenith Arcanine, Fire/Dragon, wearing Strong Jaw. Its shard is buried in Growlithe's grasslands, savannas, steppes and volcanoes.
- A pokemon holding a Zenith Shard can't use a Z-Move, Terastallize or Dynamax, the same as one holding a Mega Stone.
