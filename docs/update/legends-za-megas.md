# The Legends: Z-A Megas

**49 new Megas** from Pokemon Legends: Z-A and its Mega Dimension, each opened by
a new **Mega Stone**. They follow the rules every Mega does: a held stone, one
Mega a team, put on as the holder takes the field.

## The Megas

Raichu (X and Y), Clefable, Victreebel, Starmie, Dragonite, Meganium,
Feraligatr, Skarmory, Chimecho, Absol Z, Staraptor, Garchomp Z, Lucario Z,
Froslass, Heatran, Darkrai, Emboar, Excadrill, Scolipede, Scrafty, Eelektross,
Chandelure, Golurk, Chesnaught, Delphox, Greninja, Pyroar, Floette, Meowstic,
Malamar, Barbaracle, Dragalge, Hawlucha, Zygarde, Crabominable, Golisopod,
Drampa, Magearna, Zeraora, Falinks, Scovillain, Glimmora, Tatsugiri and
Baxcalibur.

- A stone opens its Mega only for the form it names. Only Eternal Floette holds a
  Floettite, and an Alolan Raichu holding a Raichunite stays as it is.
- One stone serves a species' forms: Meowsticite for either Meowstic, Magearnite
  for both Magearna and Tatsugirinite for all three Tatsugiri.
- **Mega Zygarde** comes from the Complete Forme. A Zygarde holding a Zygardite
  Mega Evolves the moment Power Construct makes it Complete.

## New abilities

- **Mega Sol (Meganium):** its own moves act as if the sun were out.
- **Dragonize (Feraligatr):** its Normal moves become Dragon and hit 1.2x.
- **Piercing Drill (Excadrill):** its contact moves go through Protect and
  similar guards for a quarter of the damage.
- **Eelevate (Eelektross):** it floats like Levitate, and a knockout raises its
  highest stat.
- **Spicy Spray (Scovillain):** a move that damages it burns the attacker.
- **Aura Guard (Lucario Z):** contact moves deal it half damage.
- **Fire Mane (Pyroar):** its Fire moves hit 1.5x.
