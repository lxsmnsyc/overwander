Moves and abilities are now written as YAML on the same pipeline as the species: a move's battle numbers, its cast clips and its words each in their own folder, an ability's name and line as text, and each region's signature grants in a file of its own. The species names moved into blocks beside the rest of their data. Nothing about any move or ability changes, and the editor schemas cover the new files.
