# Twist Mountain and the Swords of Justice

**Seven more families** from the fifth generation, the region's last roads and
Twist Mountain, and the **four Swords of Justice**: Cobalion, Terrakion,
Virizion and Keldeo, each with a lair or a way to call one out.

Everything from before is still here.

## The Swords of Justice

- **Cobalion** keeps the **Guidance Chamber** in the mountains.
- **Terrakion** keeps the **Trial Chamber** in the mountains and the badlands.
- **Virizion** keeps the **Rumination Field** in the temperate forests and the
  woodlands.
- **Keldeo** is a mythical, so no lair stages it. It waits in the bogs, and a
  **Colt's Petal** calls one out to be fought.
- **A Keldeo that knows Secret Sword fights in its Resolute form.**

Each sword guards its teammates in its own way while it stands.

| Sword     | Signature   | What it does for its teammates                   |
| --------- | ----------- | ------------------------------------------------ |
| Cobalion  | Iron Vigil  | Physical moves land on them at 0.8x              |
| Terrakion | Stone Vigil | Special moves land on them at 0.8x               |
| Virizion  | Leaf Vigil  | Indirect damage lands at 0.8x, and no poison     |
| Keldeo    | Tide Vigil  | They never flinch, and refuse enemy stat drops   |

Beside Justified, each reaches three abilities of its own: Sturdy, Clear Body
and Inner Focus for Cobalion, Sand Rush, Rock Head and Moxie for Terrakion,
Chlorophyll, Leaf Guard and Serene Grace for Virizion, and Swift Swim, Analytic
and Steadfast for Keldeo.

## Route 12 and the last roads

**Durant** works the badlands and the mountain by day, and **Heatmor** comes
over the same badlands and the volcano after dark.

**Larvesta and Volcarona** stand in the desert by day, and on no other ground.
Larvesta becomes Volcarona at 59. **The moth is a legendary here**, and the grub
is as rare as a baby.

**The Relic Castle is a legendary lair** in the desert, with Volcarona in it. A
raid in the desert can stage it, the way one on the mountain can stage Mt.
Ember.

## Twist Mountain and the moor below it

**Cubchoo and Beartic** cross the tundra and the glacier by day, evolving at 37.

**Cryogonal** drifts over the glacier and the alpine tundra after dark.

**Tynamo, Eelektrik and Eelektross** hold the mountain and the bog at the same
hours. Tynamo becomes Eelektrik at 39, and **an Eelektrik takes a Thunder
Stone**. A Tynamo knows four moves and never learns a fifth until it is
something else, which is how the games have it.

**Mienfoo and Mienshao** train on the mountain and in the montane forest by
day, evolving at 50.

## Seven more signature abilities

- **Anteater**, for Heatmor. Its moves hit 1.5x against a Bug or Steel target.
- **Ant Guard**, for Durant. Fire moves hit it at 0.5x.
- **Ember Halo**, for the Larvesta family. Every enemy loses 1/16 of its HP each
  time it acts while it stands.
- **Frost Fangs**, for the Cubchoo family. Its contact moves hit 1.25x, with a
  20% chance to freeze.
- **Crystal Chain**, for Cryogonal. A freeze on an enemy lasts 2x as long while
  it stands.
- **Latch On**, for the Tynamo family. Whatever its contact moves land on
  cannot flee and loses 1/16 of its HP each time it acts, one at a time.
- **Sleeve Guard**, for the Mienfoo family. Contact moves land on it at 0.75x.

**Heatmor and Durant are a pair.** Heatmor into Durant comes out at 0.75x, so
the ant wins the exchange it was built to lose.

## What else they can be born with

A Heatmor can be born with Sheer Force, a Durant with Compound Eyes, a Volcarona
with Drought or Magic Guard, a Cryogonal with Ice Body, Snow Warning or Clear
Body, an Eelektross with Volt Absorb, Strong Jaw or Water Absorb, and a
Mienshao with Iron Fist. Beartic needs nothing invented, since Rattled walks up
from the Cubchoo below it.

## A fix along the way

  Missingno.

## Where to look first

- **Mountain by day**, for Durant and Mienfoo, and a raid there for Cobalion or
  Terrakion.
- **Temperate forest and woodland**, for a Rumination Field and Virizion.
- **Bog after dark**, for Tynamo, and with a Colt's Petal, for Keldeo.
- **Desert by day**, for Larvesta and the Relic Castle.
- **Tundra and glacier**, for Cubchoo by day and Cryogonal at night.
