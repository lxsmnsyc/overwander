Boxes: make up to 32 named, coloured boxes and file your catches into them. A box keeps each pokemon in its own square and allows gaps, so a living dex can hold a place for what is missing, and it can be laid out by dex number or closed up in one press. Deleting a box sends its pokemon back to Default, and a pokemon that changes hands leaves its old owner's box.
