The rest of the game takes the Field Kit look: the overworld dialogs, the shell and the social screens.

- Stops, the Factory, portals, gym seats, honey trees and nests open with a band across the top: the place or its keeper on a stage, and what they say beside it. Their costs sit in one strip, and a note by the buttons says what pressing them does.
- A Safari encounter announces the wild pokemon on the dialog's title. Its level, held item, flight and ability sit on a glass plate over the field.
- The world map has a rail on the left: Ground or Sky, where you are, and the key.
- Talking to someone shows them in the band at the top. Questions are cards, the shop switches between Buy and Sell, and teaching a move shows the moves as the catch sheet's move cards.
- Settings, quests and news have rails on the left. Quests show how much of each pane is claimed, and news pages its releases.
- The profile opens as one sheet with a rail of its panes, including friends, requests, trades, bids and lots for sale.
- Auctions have a rail to show everything, only items or only pokemon. Listing a lot is one sheet that asks once more before it goes up. Bids and lots say where they stand in one word: Winning, Outbid, Won, Sold, Unsold.
- Trades, raids, duels, friends, gifts and notifications follow the same rows and cards. Notices and settled trades say how long ago they happened.
- On a phone, buttons stack with the main one on top, grids stay six across, and pressing a lot or a gift opens its card across the foot of the screen.
