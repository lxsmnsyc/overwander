# The Tapus, the light trio and the Ultra Beasts

**Alola's legends arrive.** The four **Tapus** guard their ruins, **Cosmog**
grows into **Solgaleo or Lunala**, **Necrozma** folds either of them into
itself, and **the Ultra Beasts** come through the wormholes. Each family carries
**a signature ability of its own**.

Everything from before is still here.

## The four Tapus

| Tapu      | Lair               | Biome                                    |
| --------- | ------------------ | ---------------------------------------- |
| Tapu Koko | Ruins of Conflict  | The tropical seasonal forest             |
| Tapu Lele | Ruins of Life      | The tropical rainforest                  |
| Tapu Bulu | Ruins of Abundance | The desert                               |
| Tapu Fini | Ruins of Hope      | The rocky coast, on land or in the water |

- **Each is met rarely, at any hour, in its own biome**, and raided at its lair.
- **Electric, Psychic and Grassy Surge** lay their terrain as the pokemon takes
  the field, as Misty Surge already does.

**Each Tapu's signature raises one stat of its whole team by 1.25x** while its
own terrain is on the field:

- **Storm Blessing**, Tapu Koko's. Speed, under Electric Terrain.
- **Mind Blessing**, Tapu Lele's. Special Attack, under Psychic Terrain.
- **Wild Blessing**, Tapu Bulu's. Attack, under Grassy Terrain.
- **Mist Blessing**, Tapu Fini's. Special Defense, under Misty Terrain.

## The light trio

| Pokemon         | Where                                           | Evolves                                             |
| --------------- | ----------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| Cosmog, Cosmoem | The grassland, rarely, by the Lake of the Sunne | At 43, then at 53: Solgaleo by day, Lunala by night |
| Solgaleo        | The Altar of the Sunne, in the badlands         |                                                     |
| Lunala          | The Altar of the Moone, in the badlands         |                                                     |
| Necrozma        | Ten Carat Hill, on the mountain                 |                                                     |

- **The N-Solarizer folds a Solgaleo into a Necrozma** as Dusk Mane Necrozma,
  and **the N-Lunarizer folds a Lunala into one** as Dawn Wings Necrozma.
  Either item takes the pair apart again, and is never spent.
- **Dusk Mane and Dawn Wings are drawn as Necrozma** until their own art exists.
- **Ultra Burst.** A Dusk Mane or Dawn Wings Necrozma holding Ultranecrozium Z
  takes the field as Ultra Necrozma, with Neuroforce.
- **The fusion dialog names both pokemon**, the one being folded in and the one
  it goes into, rather than always a dragon and a Kyurem.

Their signatures mirror each other:

- **Zenith**, the Cosmog line's. Its damaging moves hit harder the fuller its
  HP, from 1x near 0 HP up to 1.3x at full.
- **Nadir**, Necrozma's. The reverse, from 1x at full HP up to 1.3x near 0.

| Type    | Signatures                                                            |

| Psychic | Closed Mind (Poipole and Naganadel)                                   |

## Abilities now built

- **Full Metal Body, Shadow Shield and Prism Armor** work as Clear Body,
  Multiscale and Filter do, and Mold Breaker cannot ignore them.
- **Neuroforce**: its super-effective moves hit 1.25x.
- **Beast Boost**: each knockout raises its highest stat 1 stage.

## Other abilities

- **Every Tapu** can be born with Telepathy. **Tapu Koko** also takes Lightning
  Rod or Static, **Tapu Lele** Magic Guard or Synchronize, **Tapu Bulu** Grass
  Pelt or Sap Sipper, and **Tapu Fini** Water Absorb or Regenerator.
- **Solgaleo** takes Heavy Metal, **Lunala** Infiltrator, and **Necrozma**
  Tinted Lens, Mirror Armor or Levitate.
- **Nihilego** takes Levitate, Poison Touch or Merciless, **Buzzwole** Iron
  Fist, Guts or Moxie, and **Pheromosa** Speed Boost, Queenly Majesty or Tinted
  Lens.
- **Xurkitree** takes Lightning Rod, Volt Absorb or Static, **Celesteela** Heavy
  Metal, Sturdy or Weak Armor, and **Kartana** Sharpness, Super Luck or Sniper.
- **Guzzlord** takes Gluttony, Thick Fat or Ripen, **Naganadel** Corrosion,
  Poison Point or Infiltrator, **Stakataka** Sturdy, Solid Rock or Stamina, and
  **Blacephalon** Magic Guard, Aftermath or Flash Fire.
- **All of them learn their moves** by level, machine and tutor, as they do in
  Ultra Sun and Ultra Moon.
