Overwander design system

Field Kit

The world is the screen; the interface is the gear a trainer carries. Every panel is an object with weight: it comes out of the button that opened it, lands with a small bounce, and goes back where it came from. Colour only ever means something.

The world first

Chrome hugs the edges. Nothing sits over the middle of the board unless the player asked for it.

Things have weight

Hard shadows, a 2px keyline, buttons that squash and land. A press is felt before it is read.

Motion has an origin

A panel grows out of what opened it and returns to it. Exits are faster than entrances.

Colour is a word

Blue frames, green offers, red cannot be undone, gold is worth something, violet is channelled.

Pixels stay pixels

Sprites are never smoothed or scaled by fractions. Only sprite moments use stepped timing.
