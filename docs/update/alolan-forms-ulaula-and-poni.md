# The Alolan forms, Ula'ula and Poni

**Every Alolan form of a Kanto line**, from Alolan Rattata to Alolan Marowak,
then the families of **Ula'ula's roads**, and of **Poni and the sea**, with
Type: Null and Silvally. Each new family carries **a signature ability of its
own**.

Everything from before is still here.

## The Alolan forms

| Form                            | Where                                                    | When              | Evolves                          |
| ------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------- | -------------------------------- |
| Alolan Rattata, Raticate        | The tropical seasonal forest and the tropical rainforest | Evening and night | At 20, at night                  |
| Alolan Raichu                   | The beach and the tropical seasonal forest               | Any hour          | From Pikachu, by Thunder Stone   |
| Alolan Sandshrew, Sandslash     | The alpine tundra, the glacier and the tundra            | Any hour          | By Ice Stone                     |
| Alolan Vulpix, Ninetales        | The alpine tundra, the glacier and the tundra            | Any hour          | By Ice Stone                     |
| Alolan Diglett, Dugtrio         | The volcano and the badlands                             | Any hour          | At 26                            |
| Alolan Meowth, Persian          | The tropical seasonal forest and the tropical rainforest | Any hour          | By friendship                    |
| Alolan Geodude, Graveler, Golem | The steppe and the shrubland                             | Any hour          | At 25, then by trade             |
| Alolan Grimer, Muk              | The beach and the mangrove                               | Any hour          | At 38                            |
| Alolan Exeggutor                | The beach                                                | Any hour          | From Exeggcute, by Leaf Stone    |
| Alolan Marowak                  | The volcano                                              | Evening and night | From Cubone, by evening or night |

- **A Pikachu given a Thunder Stone can become either Raichu**, and an Exeggcute
  given a Leaf Stone either Exeggutor.
- **A Cubone's Marowak depends on the hour**: the Kanto one by morning or day,
  the Alolan one by evening or night.
- **Each Alolan form shares its Kanto family's candy.**

**A line whose every stage is Alolan has a signature of its own:**

- **Rich Diet**, Alolan Raticate's. Its Attack rises 1 stage for each Berry it
  eats.
- **Frostforged**, Alolan Sandslash's. It takes Fire and Fighting moves at
  0.75x.
- **Aurora Crown**, Alolan Ninetales's. While hail or snow falls, it casts
  Aurora Veil as it arrives.
- **Wire Snare**, Alolan Dugtrio's. After each entrance, it binds the first to
  land a contact move on it.
- **Taunting Gaze**, Alolan Persian's. It casts Taunt at an enemy as it arrives.
- **Magnet Float**, Alolan Golem's. It casts Magnet Rise on itself as it
  arrives.
- **Crystal Toxin**, Alolan Muk's. The poison it puts on an enemy cannot be
  cured.

Alolan Raichu, Alolan Exeggutor and Alolan Marowak keep their family's
signature.

## Ula'ula's roads

| Family     | Where                                        | When              |
| ---------- | -------------------------------------------- | ----------------- |
| Komala     | The tropical seasonal forest and the savanna | Any hour          |
| Turtonator | The volcano and the badlands                 | Any hour          |
| Mimikyu    | The woodland and the bog                     | Evening and night |
| Drampa     | The mountain and the alpine tundra           | Any hour          |

None of them evolves. Their signatures:

- **Nap Time**, Komala's. Every 3rd time it acts, it heals 1/4 of its HP.
- **Grudge Shroud**, Mimikyu's. The first time each enemy lands a move on it, it
  casts Spite at them.
- **Turtonator and Drampa share a pair.** **Blast Shell** makes Turtonator's
  next Fire move hit 1.5x after a physical move lands on it or a teammate.
  **Elder's Ire** makes Drampa's next Dragon move hit 1.5x after a special move
  lands on it or a teammate.

## Poni and the sea

| Family                      | Where                              | When     | Evolves           |
| --------------------------- | ---------------------------------- | -------- | ----------------- |
| Type: Null, Silvally        | The beach, rarely                  | Any hour | By friendship     |
| Bruxish                     | The coral reef and the kelp forest | Any hour | Never             |
| Dhelmise                    | The ocean and the kelp forest      | Any hour | Never             |
| Jangmo-o, Hakamo-o, Kommo-o | The badlands and the mountain      | Any hour | At 35, then at 45 |

- **Silvally has a lair of its own**, Aether Paradise, on the beach, where it
  can be raided.
- **RKS System makes a Silvally the type of the Memory it holds.** A Silvally
  born with another ability stays Normal.
- **The seventeen Memories can be dug up** in the prized band.

Their signatures:

- **Memory Echo**, Silvally's. Its super-effective moves hit 1.2x.
- **Psychic Gnash**, Bruxish's. Its biting moves confuse 20% of the time.
- **Ghost Ship**, Dhelmise's. Its Ghost and Grass moves hit 1.3x in rain.
- **War Clangor**, Kommo-o's. Each sound move it lands raises its Defense 1
  stage, up to 3.

## Abilities now built

- **Surge Surfer**: its Speed is doubled on Electric Terrain.
- **Tangling Hair**: whoever lands a contact move on it loses 1 stage of Speed.
- **Ripen**: Berries it eats heal 2x as much and raise 2x as many stages.
- **Galvanize**: its Normal moves are Electric moves, and hit 1.2x.
- **Power of Alchemy**: it takes a fainted teammate's ability in place of its
  own, as Receiver does.
- **Disguise**: the first move that hits Mimikyu costs it 1/8 of its HP instead,
  and leaves it busted for the rest of the fight.
- **Dazzling**: enemy moves that cut the queue are turned away, as Queenly
  Majesty does.

## Other abilities

- **Alolan Raticate** takes Ripen, **Alolan Sandslash** Iron Barbs or Ice Body,
  **Alolan Ninetales** Ice Body or Refrigerate, **Alolan Dugtrio** Steelworker,
  and **Alolan Raichu** Levitate.
- **Alolan Golem** takes Heavy Metal, **Alolan Muk** Pickpocket, and **Alolan
  Exeggutor** Leaf Guard.
- **Komala** takes Oblivious, Unaware or Sticky Hold, **Turtonator** Flame
  Body, Rough Skin or Magma Armor, **Mimikyu** Infiltrator, Rattled or Perish
  Body, and **Drampa** Fluffy.
- **Silvally** takes Adaptability or Download, **Bruxish** Swift Swim,
  **Dhelmise** Heavy Metal, Cursed Body or Shadow Tag, and **Kommo-o**
  Justified.
- **All of them learn their moves** by level, machine, tutor and egg, as they
  do in Sun, Moon, Ultra Sun and Ultra Moon.

## Fixes along the way

- **Receiver and Power of Alchemy no longer take** Shields Down, Comatose,
  Disguise or RKS System.
- **Soak no longer changes the type** of a pokemon with RKS System.
