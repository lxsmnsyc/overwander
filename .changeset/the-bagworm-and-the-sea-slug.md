Two more Sinnoh families, and the first ones met in more than one shape:

- A Burmy is met wearing the place it was met in, plant in the forests, sandy on the beaches and deserts, trash in the badlands and the high ground, and it keeps that cloak through evolution: three Burmy and three Wormadam, each cloak its own types and its own hidden ability. A male leaves the case behind as a Mothim. Its signature Patchwork raises Defense and Special Defense a stage for the first hit of each type it takes, three times a fight.
- Shellos comes in two shells, and which one the world hands over is decided by the side of the meridian a player is standing on rather than by the shore. Its signature Two Seas pays the west shell 1.25x on Water moves and the east shell the same on Ground moves.
