Signature abilities for four more Kanto families:

- Fading Presence (Gastly): moves aimed at it are 15% less accurate, and whatever lands hits it 1.3x.
- Living Tunnel (Onix): its allies take 0.8x from Rock and Ground moves while it stands, and it takes those at 1.2x.
- Dream Siphon (Drowzee): while any enemy is asleep its Special Attack is 1.25x and it heals 1/16 of its HP each time it acts.
- Heavy Pincer (Krabby): its physical moves hit 1.45x while it is at or above 1/2 HP.
