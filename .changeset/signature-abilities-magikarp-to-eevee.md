Signature abilities for four more Kanto families:

- Late Bloomer (Magikarp): its moves hit 5% harder for every 10 seconds it has been in the fight, up to 1.5x.
- Safe Passage (Lapras): it casts Safeguard over its party as it arrives, and its teammates cannot be stopped from fleeing.
- Formless (Ditto): critical hits land on it as ordinary hits, and its stages cannot be lowered.
- Latent Potential (Eevee): whichever of its five battle stats is lowest counts 1.3x.
