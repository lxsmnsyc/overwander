Signature abilities for four more Hoenn families:

- Soul Harvest (Duskull): it heals 1/4 of its HP whenever anything on the field faints, either side.
- Fruit Crop (Tropius): every 8 seconds it grows a Sitrus Berry, if its hands are empty.
- Ringing Head (Chimecho): enemy cast and channel times run 25% longer while it stands.
- Doom Mark (Absol): a move it lands marks that target, and the next blow anybody lands on them within 4 seconds hits 1.3x.
