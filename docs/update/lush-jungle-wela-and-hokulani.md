# The Lush Jungle, Wela Volcano and Mount Hokulani

**Twelve more Alola families**: the ones the Lush Jungle turns up, the ones on
Route 8 and around Wela Volcano Park, and the ones on Hano Beach and Mount
Hokulani. Each family carries **a signature ability of its own**.

Everything from before is still here.

## The Lush Jungle

| Family              | Where                                                                   | When            | Evolves |
| ------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------- | ------- |
| Comfey              | The tropical rainforest, the tropical seasonal forest and the grassland | Morning and day | Never   |
| Morelull, Shiinotic | Not in the wild yet                                                     |                 | At 24   |
| Oranguru            | Not in the wild yet                                                     |                 | Never   |
| Passimian           | Not in the wild yet                                                     |                 | Never   |

- **Morelull and Oranguru wait on art** for Shiinotic and Oranguru. Passimian
  waits on Oranguru, its counterpart.

Their signatures:

- **Drowsy Glow**, Shiinotic's. 20% of its hits cast Yawn on the target.
- **Lei Gift**, Comfey's. As it arrives, it gives its most hurt teammate a lei,
  which heals them 1/16 of their HP each time they act.
- **Sage's Call**, Oranguru's. Each time it acts, its strongest teammate casts
  their next move 25% faster.
- **Rush Pass**, Passimian's. Its moves hit 1.3x whenever they are cast faster
  than they would be on their own.

## Route 8 and Wela Volcano Park

| Family             | Where                                                        | When     | Evolves            |
| ------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------ | -------- | ------------------ |
| Mareanie, Toxapex  | The water of the coral reef, the rocky coast and the beach   | Any hour | At 38              |
| Salandit, Salazzle | The volcano and the badlands                                 | Any hour | At 33, female only |
| Stufful, Bewear    | The grassland, the tropical seasonal forest and the woodland | Any hour | At 27              |
| Wimpod, Golisopod  | The beach and the rocky coast                                | Any hour | At 30              |

Their signatures:

- **Toxic Dome**, Toxapex's. The first hit after each entrance lands at 0.5x,
  and it casts Toxic at the attacker.
- **Fume Flare**, Salazzle's. Its Fire moves poison 20% of the time.
- **Fond Crush**, Bewear's. Its moves hit 1.3x against an infatuated target.
- **Opening Slash**, Golisopod's. The first move it lands after each entrance
  hits 1.5x.

## Hano Beach and Mount Hokulani

| Family               | Where                              | When     | Evolves |
| -------------------- | ---------------------------------- | -------- | ------- |
| Sandygast, Palossand | The beach and the desert           | Any hour | At 42   |
| Pyukumuku            | The beach and the rocky coast      | Any hour | Never   |
| Minior               | The mountain and the alpine tundra | Any hour | Never   |
| Togedemaru           | The mountain and the grassland     | Any hour | Never   |

- **Minior is met in its shell**, and each one has its own core colour.

Their signatures:

- **Castle Drain**, Palossand's. It takes 1/8 of the HP of whoever lands a
  contact move on it, and heals that much.
- **Tossed Back**, Pyukumuku's. Once a battle, the first blow that would knock
  it out leaves it on 1 HP and sends it off the field for its strongest
  teammate.
- **Starfall**, Minior's. Its first move after each entrance is cast 50%
  faster.
- **Charged Spines**, Togedemaru's. After a contact move lands on it, its next
  Electric move that lands hits 1.5x.

## Abilities now built

- **Receiver**: it takes a fainted teammate's ability in place of its own.
- **Corrosion**: it can poison Poison and Steel types.
- **Fluffy**: it takes contact moves at 0.5x and Fire moves at 2x.
- **Emergency Exit**: below 1/2 HP it leaves the field for its strongest
  teammate, as Wimp Out does.
- **Water Compaction**: each Water move that lands on it raises its Defense 2
  stages.
- **Sand Spit**: a damaging move landing on it casts Sandstorm.
- **Innards Out**: whoever knocks it out with a move takes as much damage as it
  had HP left.
- **Shields Down**: above 1/2 HP a Minior stays in its shell, where no major
  status gets through. At or below 1/2 HP it cracks open to its core.

## Other abilities

- **Shiinotic** takes Dry Skin, **Comfey** Aroma Veil, **Oranguru** Forewarn,
  and **Passimian** Guts or Pickup.
- **Toxapex** takes Poison Point, **Salazzle** Flame Body or Poison Touch, and
  **Golisopod** Battle Armor, Sharpness or Swift Swim.
- **Palossand** takes Sand Spit or Cursed Body, **Pyukumuku** Regenerator or
  Liquid Ooze, **Minior** Weak Armor, Magic Guard or Sturdy, and
  **Togedemaru** Static.
- **All of them learn their moves** by level, machine, tutor and egg, as they
  do in Sun, Moon, Ultra Sun and Ultra Moon.
