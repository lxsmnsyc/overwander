Signature abilities for the next four Johto families:

- False Wood (Sudowoodo): it counts as a Grass type for working out what hurts it, until the first hit lands on it.
- Updraft (Hoppip): it cannot be trapped, its Speed cannot be lowered, and it always gets away.
- Tailthrow (Aipom): it casts Fling as it arrives on the field, throwing its held item at an enemy.
- Sunlit Charge (Sunkern): its channelled moves hit 1.3x and cannot be interrupted.
