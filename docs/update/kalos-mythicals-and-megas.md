# Kalos's mythicals and Mega Evolution

**Kalos is written in full**: its three mythicals, **Diancie, Hoopa and
Volcanion**, and its last two lines, Litleo and Spritzee. **Mega Evolution** is
here too, with all 48 Megas of the sixth generation and the Primals of Kyogre
and Groudon.

Everything from before is still here.

## Kalos's three mythicals

| Mythical  | Home          | Lair           | Relic         |
| --------- | ------------- | -------------- | ------------- |
| Diancie   | The mountains | Diamond Domain | Heart Diamond |
| Hoopa     | The desert    | Dahara Ruins   | Sealed Ring   |
| Volcanion | The volcanoes | Nebel Plateau  | Steam Valve   |

- **No mythical is staged in the world.** Spending its relic is the only way to
  fight one, as with every mythical before them.
- **The relics are in the rarest band** of the overworld item pool and nowhere
  else. Each is spent when its raid starts.
- **The Prison Bottle is a held item.** A Hoopa holding one fights unbound, with
  160 Attack and 170 Special Attack. The bottle is in the prized band, beside the
  orbs and the Gracidea.

## Litleo and Spritzee

- **Litleo becomes Pyroar at 35.**
- **Spritzee becomes Aromatisse** when it is traded holding a Sachet.
- **Neither is in the wild yet.** Nobody has drawn a male Pyroar, and Aromatisse
  has only two of its animations.

## Mega Evolution

**A pokemon holding its own Mega Stone takes its Mega shape as it takes the
field.** There are 48 Megas, from Mega Venusaur to Mega Diancie. Rayquaza needs
no stone: it Mega Evolves if it knows Dragon Ascent.

- **Only one pokemon on a team Mega Evolves in a fight.** The highest level goes
  first. A tie goes to the bigger Mega, then to the earlier party slot.
- **A Mega keeps its catch's ability and wears the Mega's own on top.** Nine
  Megas whose line can already have that ability wear a different one. The
  [player's guide](../mechanics/battles.md#mega-evolution) lists them.
- **The stone glows over a Mega's head** for the rest of the fight. A Mega with
  no art of its own yet is drawn as its ordinary self, so the stone is how you
  tell.
- **The 47 Mega Stones are buried like the plates**, and dust clouds kick them
  up. No shop stocks them.
- **The demo raid's parties carry stones**, so you can see a Mega before you own
  one.

### Primal Reversion

- **A Kyogre holding the Blue Orb becomes Primal Kyogre**, and a Groudon holding
  the Red Orb becomes Primal Groudon.
- **It is not a Mega Evolution.** Every holder changes, the way an Origin Forme
  does, and none of them uses up the team's one Mega.
- **An orb floats over a Primal** the way a stone does over a Mega.

## A signature for each of them

The mythicals:

- **Regalia**, Diancie's. Its whole team gains 1 stage of Defense every 10
  seconds, up to 3. It is Carbink's Crystal Growth, handed to the court.
- **Ringback**, Hoopa's. 20% of the single-target moves aimed at it go back to
  whoever threw them.
- **Boiler**, Volcanion's. Its Fire moves hit 1.15x in rain, and its Water moves
  1.15x in sunlight.

The last two lines:

- **Pride Call**, Litleo's. It casts Noble Roar at an enemy as it arrives.
- **Calming Scent**, Spritzee's. The enemy side loses 1 stage of Speed whenever
  it uses up its own held item. It is Swirlix's Sugar Rush, read from the other
  side.

## Abilities now built

- **Steam Engine**: a Fire or Water move landing on it raises its Speed 6
  stages.
- **Aerilate**: its Normal moves are Flying moves instead, and hit 1.2x.
- **Parental Bond**: a move it casts at one target lands twice. The second hit
  is 0.25x.
- **Primordial Sea**: heavy rain while it stands. Water moves hit 1.5x and Fire
  moves fail.
- **Desolate Land**: harsh sunlight while it stands. Fire moves hit 1.5x and
  Water moves fail.
- **Delta Stream**: strong winds while it stands. Moves super effective on a
  Flying type hit it at 1x.
- **None of these three skies can be replaced** by any other weather while its
  holder stands.

## Other abilities

- **Diancie** takes Solid Rock and Serene Grace.
- **Hoopa** takes Levitate, Prankster and Trace.
- **Volcanion** takes Flash Fire, Steam Engine and Pressure.
- **Pyroar** takes Intimidate, and **Aromatisse** takes Natural Cure and Misty
  Surge.
