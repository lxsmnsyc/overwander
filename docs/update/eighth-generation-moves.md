# The eighth generation's moves

**89 new moves** from Sword and Shield and from Legends: Arceus, taught to every
pokemon already in the game. Each has a picture, and every legendary and
mythical signature has one of its own.

Galar's and Hisui's pokemon arrive family by family.

## 89 new moves

65 from Galar and 24 from Hisui, at the numbers Scarlet and Violet settled them
on. There is no Dynamax yet, so the Max Moves are not among them.

- **Most ride what was already there.** Flip Turn swaps out like U-turn, Wicked
  Blow always lands a critical hit, and Meteor Beam winds up like Skull Bash.
- **Grassy Glide, Rising Voltage, Expanding Force, Misty Explosion and Terrain
  Pulse** read the terrain under the user.
- **Behemoth Blade, Behemoth Bash, Eternabeam, Dynamax Cannon, Glacial Lance,
  Astral Barrage and the rest** are here for the pokemon they belong to.

### Moves with rules of their own

- **Jaw Lock and Octolock** hold a pokemon on the field.
- **Body Press** hits with the user's Defense.
- **Bolt Beak and Fishious Rend** hit 2x against a target that has not begun a
  cast.
- **Tar Shot** makes Fire moves hit the target 2x.
- **Stone Axe and Ceaseless Edge** leave hazards behind them.
- **Court Change** swaps hazards, screens, Mist, Safeguard and Tailwind between
  the sides, each with the time it had left.
- **Power Shift** swaps both pairs of attacking and defending stats.
- **Expanding Force** reaches everything opposite on Psychic Terrain.
- **Aura Wheel** is Morpeko's alone, and turns Dark while it is Hangry.
- **Life Dew, Jungle Healing and Lunar Blessing** mend the whole team.

## Moves look like themselves

- **Every legendary and mythical signature is drawn as its own.** That covers
  Behemoth Blade and Behemoth Bash, Eternabeam and Dynamax Cannon, Glacial
  Lance, Astral Barrage, Thunder Cage, Dragon Energy, the Galarian birds'
  three, Urshifu's two, Jungle Healing, Mystical Power and each of the four
  storms.
- **Dragon Darts has a picture of its own.**
- **The rest look like the earlier move they take after.** Flip Turn looks
  like U-turn, and Life Dew like Recover.

## Everything already here learns them

**Kanto, Johto, Hoenn, Sinnoh, Unova, Kalos and Alola** learn the new moves by
level, machine, tutor and egg, the way Sword and Shield, Brilliant Diamond and
Shining Pearl, and Legends: Arceus teach them.
