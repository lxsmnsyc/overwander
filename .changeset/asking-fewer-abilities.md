Asking whether a pokemon has an ability is cheaper, which a raid of forty-nine felt:

- A unit that does not carry the ability answers outright instead of putting the question to the whole field. Nothing may grant an ability through that query, only take one away, so the answer is the same.
- Effects that ask whether a holder is standing now read the ability's own holder list rather than sweeping every unit, the way Cloud Nine already did. Whether that holder is still up, and whether something is suppressing what it carries, are still asked of each one.
