'overwander': patch
---

The AI no longer casts a stat-stage move that would change nothing: a Swords Dance at +6, a Growl on foes already at -6, a Screech into Mist. It looks for a target where the move still works instead. Moves that do something besides (Minimize, Defog, Memento, Swagger and a few others) are still weighed on the rest of what they do.
