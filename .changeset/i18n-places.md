Biomes, skies and their lines, times of day, landmarks, lairs, phenomena, spawn rarities and lair titles now read in Japanese and Spanish, and so do town names: each locale has its own word lists, so a town is the same town in every language and reads like a place of its own (Japanese `…ぐんの かいがらやまタウン`, Spanish `Pueblo Concha del Risco, Quejigal`).
