# Kalos's Zeniths

**26 new Zeniths**, one for every Kalos pokemon that can have one, from
Diggersby to Volcanion. Each is reached by holding its own **Zenith Shard**,
which is dug up where its line lives. A Zenith fights 100 above its pokemon's
base stat total, none of it in HP, and wears an ability its line can never be
born with. No two of Kalos's share one.

## What to look at first

- **Zenith Aegislash** keeps Stance Change. Drawing the blade turns it into
  its Blade Zenith, with the same bonus moved over to Attack and Special
  Attack, and Sharpness makes Sacred Sword and Night Slash cut deeper.
- **Zenith Heliolisk** brings the sun, which a Solar Power or Dry Skin catch
  makes the most of.
- **Zenith Goodra** turns Water and brings its own rain.
- **Zenith Vivillon** fights in whichever pattern it was born with, and
  **Zenith Florges** in whichever colour, each under one shard. Super Gourgeist
  has a Zenith of its own on its own stats.
- **Zygarde has no Zenith.** Power Construct can take any Zygarde to the
  Complete Forme, and the Complete Forme has a Mega.
