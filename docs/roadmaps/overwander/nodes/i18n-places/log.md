# Log: i18n-places

- Files: `text/{ja,es}/{biomes,weather,times-of-day,landmarks,lairs,phenomena,spawn-rarities,lair-titles,town-names}.yaml`, every key English has. Counts: biomes 30, skies 26 (name and line), times of day 4, landmarks 25, lairs 97, phenomena 4, spawn rarities 8, lair titles 2, town words 23 biomes x 8 heads, 40 tails, 12 titles, 12 marks, 64 counties.
- Method: WikiDex through its API (`api.php?action=query&prop=extracts`, English or Spanish titles, redirects on) for place and term names; PokeAPI's `location_names.csv` (the commit `official-names` pins) for the places it has. Bulbapedia and the Japanese wikis refuse the fetch tool (403), so most Japanese names are from memory unless a WikiDex page printed them.
- Memory is not reliable: Mt. Ember was written `もえつきやま` from memory and WikiDex prints `ともしびやま`. Every Japanese name listed under "To confirm" in the outcome was not read anywhere.
- Spain forms where WikiDex gives both: Cueva Retorno, Montaña Dura, Columnas Lanza, Santuario Abundancia (the Latin American forms are Cueva Regreso, Montaña Sólida, Pilares Lanza, Santuario de la Abundancia).
- Skies: es uses the games' weather words (Despejado, Nublado, Lluvia, Diluvio, Tormenta de arena, Granizo, Niebla, Nieve, Ventisca, Ceniza volcánica, Lluvia de meteoros). ja uses the games' はれ, くもり, あめ, おおあめ, すなあらし, あられ, きり, ゆき, ふぶき, かざんばい; WikiDex printed no Japanese for them, so these are from memory.
- Collision check (script folding katakana onto hiragana, dropping spaces and accents) of every biome, landmark, sky, phenomenon, rarity and lair name against every ability, move, item and species name of its locale: the sky names `ふぶき` (Blizzard), es Ventisca, Niebla, Neblina and Llovizna equal a move or an ability, as English's Blizzard and Haze do; kept, they are the games' words. Renamed because they were new clashes: ja Thunderstorm `かみなり` (Thunder) became `らいう`; ja Meteor Shower `りゅうせいぐん` (Draco Meteor) became `ながれぼしの あめ`.
- Second-reading pass: ja `ぬまち` (Swamp) and `しつげん` (Bog) are different words for different ground; `かなた` (Beyond) reads as "the far side"; `ガマ` (Croak, Swamp) is both cattail and toad, kept as the toad; `しんきろう` is the Desert head and the Fata Morgana sky, a mirage in both. ja `はつしも` replaced `しもばしら` (Frost): after the mark `かみ` or `しも` it read as `かみしも` (the garment). es `Oscuro` in `Día oscuro` equals the Shadow term; es `Alijo de objetos` and `Tablón de subastas` have no other meaning.
- Spawn rarities: the Trader says "I will take any of yours that is {band}" and ja writes `{band}の ポケモン`. ja bands are all nouns (ふつう, ややレア, レア, ごくレア, ちょうレア, きちょう, でんせつ, まぼろし) because an adjective cannot take の; es bands are masculine singular and lower case (común, poco común, raro, escaso, esquivo, preciado, legendario, singular) because they follow "que sea". Side effect: the dex badge reads "Mañana · raro" in lower case.
- Town words: heads translate the English head at the same place (Shell is かいがら and Concha, Tide is しお and Marea). Tails are place nouns in ja (やま, みなと, もり) and phrases in es (del Risco, de la Atalaya) because Spanish place names do not weld suffixes. The county words are kana (ja, plus ぐん in the joiner) and comarca names (es), not transliterations of the English ones, except invented stems (Kelven, Orren, Pello, Umber) kept as sounds. Distinct words inside every list and no head shared between biomes in either locale (test).
- Joiners: ja `{county}ぐんの {mark}{head}{tail}{title}`, with タウン and シティ welded on like マサラタウン and any other title after a space. es `{title} {mark} {head} {tail}, {county}`; the mark agrees with the title (`agreeSpanish`: Ciudad Nueva, Pueblo Nuevo, Molinos Nuevos), Gran, Norte, Puerto and the rest stay as they are. English output is unchanged (`town-names.test.ts` untouched, passes).
- Spanish tails that matched a county were renamed to avoid "del Robledal, Robledal": county Oakenwold is Quejigal, Glenmarch Marca del Cauce, Cragmarch Marca Peñascosa; tails moor and hollow are de la Landa and de la Vaguada.
- Tests: six checks for the places files and four for towns in `test/data/locale-people-places.test.ts`; the joiner test was updated for the new joiners.
- Terms: "Places, weather and towns" section in `docs/strategy/translations-terms.md`.

## Second pass on towns (maintainer's decisions)

- ja: the town is one katakana word, head and tail welded, title welded on, county after it in full-width brackets (`ナミハタケシティ（コガネザカイ）`); the `ぐんの` prefix is gone. Rule for titles: タウン and シティ are the games' loanwords, and every other title is the native word in katakana, welded too (ムラ, サト, チク...), so every name is one word in one script and none needs a space. Rule for marks: loanwords (ニュー, オールド...), because the native ones made words by accident (コ + ナミ read as Konami; シモ is a slang word and became シタ before the loanwords replaced it).
- Scan of every head + tail (+ title, + mark) against every ja species, move, ability and item name, and against a list of rude words: collisions and fixes: マツ + バ = マツバ (Morty), the tail ba became ニワ; キリ + サク = キリサク (the move Slash), tail サク became カキネ; ヤシキ + チク read キチク, tail became ニワ; イタダキ + チク read キチク, tail became テッペン; ハク + ソウ contained クソ, Hoar became シラジモ; ハゲワシ became コンドル; ヒガシ + ネツ contained シネ, Fever became ハツネツ; エニシダ + サカイ contained ダサ, March became ザカイ; ガレバ + カゲ contained バカ, shade became ヒカゲ; タケ + ツカ contained ケツ, cairn became ケルン and barrow コフン; ツジ (Crossing) became ジュウジロ. Thin and Cloud (Alpine Tundra) became キハク and ウンカイ (ウスイ is a surname, クモ is a spider).
- es: the head words that read as bare nouns became adjectives that take the title's gender and number (Rompiente Bravío, Deriva Varado, Incendio Ardiente, Polvo Polvoriento, Tormenta Tormentoso, Niebla Neblinoso, Nevada Nevado, and so on); animals, plants, materials and place nouns stay (Garza, Roble, Granito, Hueso, Cuenca). Position, length and order of every list are unchanged. Two more: Pluma became Emplumado and Caballo Caballar; the spire tail became del Chapitel because Needle's Aguja repeated it.
