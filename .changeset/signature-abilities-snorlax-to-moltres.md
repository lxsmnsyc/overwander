Signature abilities for four more Kanto families:

- Full Belly (Snorlax): it heals 1/16 of its HP every time it acts, and its cast times are 25% longer.
- Absolute Calm (Articuno): it cannot be made to flinch, confused or infatuated, and statuses on it run out in half the time.
- Lightning Reflexes (Zapdos): its cast times are 25% shorter, and it cannot be paralysed or made to flinch.
- Ashfall (Moltres): when it faints, it casts Will-O-Wisp at every standing enemy.
