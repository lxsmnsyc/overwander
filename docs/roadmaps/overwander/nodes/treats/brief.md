# Pokéblocks and Poffins

## Goal

Cooking two to four berries into a treat whose flavours are their sum. Recommended purpose: friendship and training, more when the flavour matches the nature's liked flavour. Treats are one item family with a flavour profile, so bag, market and auctions handle them.
