# Paldea's legends and Paradox pokemon

**Ting-Lu and Chi-Yu**, **Koraidon and Miraidon**, **sixteen Paradox pokemon**
and **Gimmighoul's line**, each with a signature ability.

## Gimmighoul and Gholdengo

**Gimmighoul** wanders the woodland and grows into **Gholdengo** for 999
Gimmighoul candies.
It is drawn on the move until the collection draws its chest.

- **Coin Scatter:** each damaging move it lands scatters coins the way Pay Day
  does, within Pay Day's own limit.

## The Treasures of Ruin

**Ting-Lu** waits at the Groundblight Shrine in the badlands, and **Chi-Yu** at
the Firescourge Shrine on the volcano. Wo-Chien and Chien-Pao wait for their
art.

Each move one lands stops the target raising one stat for 6 seconds, refreshed
on every hit:

- **Cracked Vessel (Ting-Lu):** Special Attack.
- **Scattered Beads (Chi-Yu):** Special Defense.

## Koraidon and Miraidon

Both are found at **Zero Lab** on the mountain: Koraidon by day, Miraidon by
night, in its Low Power mode.

- **Sun Sprint (Koraidon):** its moves wind up 20% faster while the sun shines.
- **Drive Mode (Miraidon):** its moves wind up 20% faster on Electric Terrain.

## The Paradox pokemon

Each is a prized find with a signature of its own. **Booster Energy** wakes
Protosynthesis or Quark Drive without sun or Electric Terrain.

- **Great Tusk:** the stat drops its own moves give it are undone by its next
  contact move.
- **Scream Tail:** its sound moves wind up 30% faster.
- **Flutter Mane:** a single-target move at it has a 20% chance to go to one of
  the attacker's own teammates instead.
- **Slither Wing:** casts Quiver Dance the first time it falls below 1/3 HP.
- **Sandy Shocks:** its Electric moves hit Ground types.
- **Roaring Moon:** every enemy's next move winds up 30% slower when it takes
  the field.
- **Walking Wake:** its Water moves are not weakened by the sun, and wind up 20%
  faster in it.
- **Gouging Fire:** heals 1/8 of its HP each time its protect move blocks one.
- **Raging Bolt:** its Electric moves have a 15% chance to lay Electric Terrain.
- **Iron Treads:** its Ground moves lay Stealth Rock, at most once every 15
  seconds.
- **Iron Bundle:** its Ice moves heal a teammate 1/4 of their HP instead of
  hurting them.
- **Iron Hands:** a target of its punches cannot protect itself for 4 seconds.
- **Iron Moth:** its Fire moves poison 20% of the time.
- **Iron Thorns:** an Electric move landing on it whips up a sandstorm, at most
  once every 15 seconds.
- **Iron Valiant:** winds up 40% faster for a second after it is hit.
- **Iron Leaves:** its slicing moves hit 1.3x on any terrain.

Brute Bonnet, Iron Jugulis, Iron Boulder and Iron Crown wait for their art.
