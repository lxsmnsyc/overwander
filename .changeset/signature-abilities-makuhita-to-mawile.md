Signature abilities for five more Hoenn families:

- Shove (Makuhita): a contact move it lands on an enemy winding a move up flinches them, costing them that cast.
- Magnetize (Nosepass): enemy moves aimed at one of its teammates are pulled onto it instead.
- Kitten Pace (Skitty): its Speed counts 1.3x while it is at full HP.
- Shadow Tax (Sableye): a move it lands removes one raised stage from the target.
- Jaw Claim (Mawile): a move it lands moves one raised stage from the target onto itself.
