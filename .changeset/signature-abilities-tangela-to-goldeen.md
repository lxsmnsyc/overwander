Signature abilities for four more Kanto families:

- Endless Growth (Tangela): each time it acts it heals 1/16 of its HP and its Defense rises 5%, up to 1.5x.
- Mother's Shield (Kangaskhan): enemy moves aimed at an ally below 1/2 HP are aimed at her instead.
- Whirl Current (Horsea): enemy cast times are 20% longer while rain is falling.
- Upstream (Goldeen): its moves hit 1.35x against any target with more HP than its own.
