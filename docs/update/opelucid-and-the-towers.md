# Opelucid and the towers

**Nine more families** from the fifth generation: the lamps and the golems
around Celestial Tower, the dragons of Dragonspiral Tower and the tunnels under
it, and the three that hold the last two roads out of Opelucid. **A wild
pokemon can now be met at a higher level than before.**

Everything from before is still here.

## Celestial Tower and the road to it

All three of these families keep to the evening and the night.

**Litwick, Lampent and Chandelure** drift over the bog and the woodland. Litwick
becomes Lampent at 41, and **a Lampent becomes a Chandelure on a Dusk Stone**,
the first Unova line to evolve on a stone rather than a level.

**Elgyem and Beheeyem** stand out in the desert and the cold desert, evolving at
42.

**Golett and Golurk** cross the badlands and the steppe, evolving at 43.
Neither has a gender.

## Dragonspiral Tower

**Axew, Fraxure and Haxorus** work the mountain and the montane forest by day,
at 38 and again at 48.

**Druddigon** keeps to the mountain and the badlands by day, and turns up in
every cave.

**Deino, Zweilous and Hydreigon** cross the badlands and the volcano after dark.
Deino becomes Zweilous at 50 and Hydreigon at 64.

## The last two roads

**Pawniard and Bisharp** hold the badlands and the shrubland after dark,
evolving at 52.

**Rufflet and Braviary** ride the mountain and the steppe by day, and **Vullaby
and Mandibuzz** the badlands and the desert at the same hours. Both evolve at
54.

**Every Rufflet and Braviary is male, and every Vullaby and Mandibuzz is
female**, the way the games have them.

## Wild pokemon reach higher levels

- **A fully grown wild pokemon** can be met up to **level 80**, up from 60.
  Hydreigon does not exist below 64, which the old ceiling had no room for.
- **The end of a two-stage line** can be met up to **level 70**, up from 50.
  Bisharp, Braviary and Mandibuzz all arrive above the old ceiling.

## Nine signature abilities

- **Hexlight**, for the Litwick family. Its moves hit 1.4x against a target that
  already carries a status.
- **Swap Field**, for the Elgyem family. It casts Wonder Room as it arrives.
- **Broken Seal**, for the Golett family. The first time it drops below 1/2 HP
  it gains 2 stages of Attack and loses 1 of Defense, once a battle.
- **Scoring**, for the Axew family. Every physical move it lands takes 1 stage
  of Defense off the target.
- **Sunwarmed**, for Druddigon. Its moves take 0.75x as long to cast while harsh
  sunlight is up.
- **Three Heads**, for the Deino family. Every move it lands also bites one
  other enemy for 1/3 of the damage.
- **Honed**, for the Pawniard family. It gains 1 stage of Attack every time an
  enemy raises a stat of its own.
- **Warcry**, for the Rufflet family. It gains 2 stages of Attack every time one
  of its teammates faints.
- **Bonewear**, for the Vullaby family. It gains 1 stage of each defence every
  time an enemy faints.

## What else they can be born with

A Chandelure can be born with Illuminate, a Beheeyem with Forewarn, a Golurk
with Stamina, a Haxorus with Hyper Cutter, a Druddigon with Intimidate, a
Hydreigon with Berserk or Pressure, and a Mandibuzz with Guts. Braviary needs
nothing invented, since Hustle walks up from the Rufflet below it.

## A fix along the way

- **Agatha fields the Ghost type** and her named Golbat and Arbok, rather than
  drawing on the whole Amorphous egg group. That widener dated from when Kanto
  had one fully grown Ghost, and it had come to reach Gardevoir, Gallade and
  Castform.

## Where to look first

- **Bog and woodland after dark**, for Litwick.
- **Desert and cold desert at night**, for Elgyem, with Golett in the badlands.
- **Mountain and montane forest by day**, for Axew, and any cave for Druddigon.
- **Badlands and volcano after dark**, for Deino.
- **Mountain and steppe by day**, for Rufflet, with Vullaby over the desert.
