Signature abilities for the next four Johto families:

- Magma Trail (Slugma): an enemy it has landed a move on loses 1/16 of its HP each time it acts, while it stands.
- Icebreaker (Swinub): a move it lands tears Reflect and Light Screen off the target's side.
- Coral Bloom (Corsola): whenever it is healed, the ally lowest on HP is healed the same amount.
- Standoff (Remoraid): nothing it uses counts as contact, so it never sets off what answers a touch.
