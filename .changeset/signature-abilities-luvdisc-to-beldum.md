Signature abilities for three more Hoenn families:

- Shared Heart (Luvdisc): whenever a teammate is healed, it heals half of that amount as well.
- Skull Charge (Bagon): its contact moves deal 1.4x, and it takes 1/8 of the damage they deal back.
- Hive Mind (Beldum): its moves hit 10% harder for each teammate standing with it, up to 3 of them.
