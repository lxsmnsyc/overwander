Signature abilities for the first four Hoenn families. The three starters share one signature, each growing through a fight in the stat its line is built on:

- Sap Surge (Treecko): it gains a stage of Speed each time it acts, up to 3 of its own.
- Ember Surge (Torchic): it gains a stage of Attack each time it lands a move, up to 3 of its own.
- Silt Surge (Mudkip): it gains a stage of Special Defense each time it takes a hit, up to 3 of its own.
- Pack Hunt (Poochyena): its moves hit 1.2x against anything a teammate has already damaged.
