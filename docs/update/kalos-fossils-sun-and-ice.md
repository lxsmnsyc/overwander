# Kalos's fossils, sun and ice

**Fifteen more pokemon from Kalos**: two fossils brought back from the rock,
the squids, lizards and wrestler of the shores and hills, and the stumps,
pumpkins and icebergs of the woods and the north. Each family carries **a
signature ability of its own**.

Everything from before is still here.

## Two fossils

- **The Jaw Fossil brings back Tyrunt, and the Sail Fossil brings back
  Amaura**, at level 20 like every other fossil.
- **Both rocks are dug out of the ground or bought from the Fossil Maniac.**
  Neither line is met in the world any other way.
- **Tyrunt becomes Tyrantrum at 39 by day**, and **Amaura becomes Aurorus at 39
  at night**.

- **Two of Pumpkaboo's four sizes are here**, the average one and the super
  one. The small and large sizes wait until their grown shapes are drawn.
- **All fifteen learn their moves** by level, machine, tutor and egg, as they
  do in X, Y, Omega Ruby and Alpha Sapphire.

## A signature for each of them

The two fossils both work against the clock:

- **Jaw Snap**, Tyrantrum's. A move it lands cancels whatever the target was
  casting or channelling, once every 8 seconds per target.
- **Frostbound**, Aurorus's. A target it damages casts and channels 30% slower
  for the next 6 seconds.

The three of the shores and hills:

- **Overturn**, Malamar's. A move it lands flips every stat stage the target
  holds, once every 10 seconds per target.
- **Backfeed**, Heliolisk's. Each Electric move it lands heals its whole team
  1/16 of the damage it dealt.
- **Top Rope**, Hawlucha's. Its contact moves hit 10% harder for each 50 kg the
  target weighs, up to 1.5x.

The woods and the ice:

- **Undergrowth**, Trevenant's. A move it lands adds the Grass type to the
  target, once per target.
- **Hollowing**, Gourgeist's. A move it lands adds the Ghost type to the target,
  once per target.
- **Trevenant and Gourgeist are a pair**, since the versions keep them apart. A
  target both have hit carries both types.
- **Deadweight**, Avalugg's. Its physical moves use its Defense in place of its
  Attack while its Defense is the higher of the two. On an Avalugg it always is.

## Abilities

- **Refrigerate is built.** A Normal move thrown by an Amaura or an Aurorus
  lands as Ice and hits 1.2x.
- **Fourth abilities:**
  - Malamar takes Analytic.
  - Heliolisk takes Overcoat.
  - Hawlucha takes Sheer Force.
  - Trevenant and Gourgeist both take Cursed Body.
  - Avalugg takes Snow Warning.
