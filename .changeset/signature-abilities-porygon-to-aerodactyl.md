Signature abilities for four more Kanto families:

- Rollback (Porygon): the first time it drops below 1/2 HP, its health goes back to what it was 4 seconds earlier.
- Helix Shell (Omanyte): its Defense and Special Defense count as 1.25x against every blow it takes.
- Dome Blade (Kabuto): its blows count the target's Defense or Special Defense as 0.75x.
- Predator's Dive (Aerodactyl): its first move against each enemy hits 1.5x.
