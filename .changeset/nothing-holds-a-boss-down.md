---
'overwander': patch
---

- A raid boss is immune to Power Swap, Guard Swap and Heart Swap. A boss refuses the half of a swap that would cost it anything, so one that landed copied stages rather than trading them, in whichever direction it was cast.
- Taunt, Torment, Imprison and Encore no longer hold a boss to part of its move set, the way a Disable already did not.
- A boss is no longer staged with those three swaps, or with Healing Wish, Lunar Dance, Helping Hand and Follow Me, which are all spent on a teammate a lone boss does not have.
