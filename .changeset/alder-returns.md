Alder, Unova's champion before Iris, now turns up as a legend where a champion would be, with the six he held the league with: Accelgor, Bouffalant, Druddigon, Vanilluxe, Escavalier and Volcarona. Beating him earns the Alder Defeated mark, which unlocks his coat.

To make room for his party, three Unova lines are met in the wild. Karrablast lives in the temperate forest and the woodland by morning and day, and Shelmet in the temperate forest and the swamp by evening and night, each with its evolved form in the scarcer band. Bouffalant charges through the grassland and the steppe by morning and day.
