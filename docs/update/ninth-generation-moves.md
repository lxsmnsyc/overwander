# The ninth generation's moves

**69 new moves** from Scarlet and Violet, The Teal Mask and The Indigo Disk,
taught to every pokemon already in the game. Every signature move in every
region now has a picture of its own, not only the legendary ones.

Paldea's pokemon arrive family by family.

## 69 new moves

All of them are at the numbers Scarlet and Violet settled them on.

- **Most ride what was already there.**
  - Silk Trap and Burning Bulwark guard like King's Shield.
  - Axe Kick and Supercell Slam crash like High Jump Kick.
  - Mortal Spin clears hazards like Rapid Spin.
  - Ruination halves like Super Fang, and Comeuppance hits back like Metal Burst.
  - Thunderclap only lands on a pokemon about to attack, like Sucker Punch.
  - Double Shock spends the user's Electric type, like Burn Up spends Fire.
  - Doodle copies the target's ability onto the whole team.
- **Shed Tail and Chilly Reception swap the user out.** Shed Tail leaves its
  substitute for whoever comes in. Chilly Reception calls up snow first.

### Moves with rules of their own

- **Last Respects** hits harder for each fallen teammate.
- **Rage Fist** hits harder for each hit the user has taken, up to 350 power.
- **Glaive Rush** leaves its user taking 2x damage for 2 seconds.
- **Salt Cure** hurts the target each time it acts, more on Water and Steel
  types.
- **Syrup Bomb** slows the target each time it acts, three times.
- **Revival Blessing** brings a fallen teammate back on half its HP.
- **Fillet Away and Shed Tail** cost half the user's HP.
- **Gigaton Hammer and Blood Moon** cannot be used twice in a row.
- **Psychic Noise** stops the target healing for 4 seconds.
- **Dragon Cheer** raises the team's critical hit chance, more for Dragon types.
- **Collision Course and Electro Drift** hit 1.33x harder on a super-effective
  hit.
- **Order Up** reads the Tatsugiri on the team.
- **Electro Shot** fires at once in rain.
- **Upper Hand** only lands on a target winding up a fast move.

### Weather and dances

- **Snow raises Ice types' Defense 1.5x.** Snowscape and Chilly Reception call
  it up.
- **Dancer copies Fiery Dance, Victory Dance and Aqua Step.**

### Waiting on later releases

- **Tera Blast and Tera Starstorm** hit as plain moves until Terastallization
  arrives.
- **Raging Bull and Ivy Cudgel** change type with Paldean Tauros's breed and
  Ogerpon's mask, once those forms are in the game.

## Every signature has its own picture

- **Each Paldea signature is drawn as its own.** Koraidon charges in, Gholdengo
  rains coins, Tinkaton's hammer comes down from the sky, and Dondozo flings its
  Tatsugiri. Each Starmobile's Torque rolls in as its own wheel.
- **Older signatures that borrowed a picture now have their own too.** That is
  75 moves, from Sketch and Volt Tackle to King's Shield, Pyro Ball and Dire
  Claw.
- **The moves many pokemon learn look like the earlier move they take after.**

## Everything already here learns them

**Kanto, Johto, Hoenn, Sinnoh, Unova, Kalos, Alola, Galar and Hisui** learn the
new moves by level, machine, tutor and egg, the way Scarlet and Violet teach
them.

- **Nothing learned by level is taken away.**
- **Egg moves go on the pokemon that hatches**, not on its evolutions.

## Fixes

- **Population Bomb and Triple Axel** no longer claim each strike rolls its own
  accuracy. A multi-hit move rolls once.
