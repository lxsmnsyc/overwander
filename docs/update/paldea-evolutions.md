# Paldea's new evolutions and regional lines

**Seven older lines grow a new evolution**, Paldea's regional Wooper and Tauros
arrive, Bloodmoon Ursaluna rises, and Poltchageist brings the other half of the
tea pair. One in a hundred of three lines is a rarer form of its own.

## New evolutions

Each keeps its family's signature ability.

- **Annihilape:** Primeape evolves after landing Rage Fist 20 times, counted
  across fights.
- **Farigiraf:** Girafarig evolves once it knows Twin Beam.
- **Dudunsparce:** Dunsparce evolves once it knows Hyper Drill.
- **Kingambit:** Bisharp evolves at level 60 holding a **Leader's Crest**.
- **Dipplin and Hydrapple:** Applin evolves with a **Syrupy Apple**, and Dipplin
  once it knows Dragon Cheer, at any level.
- **Archaludon:** Duraludon evolves with a **Metal Alloy**.

## Paldea's regional lines

- **Paldean Wooper and Clodsire** live in the swamp. **Bog Down:** an enemy
  that lands a Ground move on it cannot switch out for 6 seconds.
- **Paldean Tauros** comes in three breeds: Combat on the grassland, Blaze on
  the savanna and Aqua by the water. **Raging:** while its Attack is raised, its
  moves wind up 20% faster.
- **Bloodmoon Ursaluna** prowls the taiga at night. It keeps the Teddiursa
  family's Sweet Paw.

## The tea pair

**Poltchageist and Sinistcha** haunt the forest at night, evolving with an
**Unremarkable Teacup**. **Tea Service:** its healing moves aimed at a teammate
wind up 40% faster. It is the support half of the pair Sinistea's Last Pour
began.

## One in a hundred

Each of these is decided by the pokemon itself and never changes, the way
Maushold's family of three is. A wild one is met in its rare form just as often.

- **Antique Sinistea** grows into an **antique Polteageist** with the new
  **Chipped Pot**. Every other Sinistea is a fake and needs the Cracked Pot.
- **Artisan Poltchageist** grows into a **masterpiece Sinistcha** with the new
  **Masterpiece Teacup**. Every other Poltchageist needs the Unremarkable
  Teacup.
- **Three-Segment Dudunsparce** is what one Dunsparce in a hundred grows into.

The rare forms are drawn as their usual form until the collection draws them.
