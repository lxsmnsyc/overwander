The catch sheet stands its pokemon still, and the line under it in squares.

A pokemon is whatever size it was drawn at, and the pictures of what one turns
into were drawn at twice their own sheets. A branching line came out at a
different height on every row, with the condition beside each one starting in a
different place, which read as three unrelated drawings rather than a list of
what this pokemon could become. Each stands in a square of its own now, fitted
to it, so the rows line up whatever is in them.

Both pictures on the sheet also stand the same way: the pokemon at the top idles
facing down and left instead of walking on the spot, and what it turns into
faces the same way. The row is a sum with the picture above it, and two pokemon
facing different ways is two drawings that happen to share a page.
