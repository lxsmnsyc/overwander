# Kanto

**Kanto** is the first release of Overwander: a world to walk, the original 151
pokemon living in it, and everything you do with them.

Every major release is named after a region. This one is named for the region
its pokemon come from.

## The world

Pick a direction and keep walking. Wherever you stop, the place is already
there, finished and full.

- 25 kinds of country, from coral reef and savanna to volcano and glacier.
- No loading screens, and no edge you are herded away from.
- Two players in the same field at the same time see the same things.
- Nothing waits for you: the pokemon, the berries, the raids and the people at
  the crossroads all turn over on their own schedules.

## Pokemon

All 151, and each one is already itself before you walk up to it.

- Its level, nature, gender, ability, size and moves are settled. No rerolls.
- **Shinies**, **hidden abilities**, runts and record-breakers.
- **Shadow** pokemon, harder to raise until you put them right.
- A **featured family** every so often, the same one for the whole world, with
  far more of them about, far better shiny luck and far more candy.

## Catching

No wild battles. Walk up to a pokemon and it is you, it, and whatever is in your
bag.

- Throw a ball, feed a berry to settle it, or back away.
- Miss and it may bolt for good.
- Fourteen kinds of ball, each good somewhere different: first throw, tenth
  throw, in the dark, in the water.

## Battles

Both sides fight at once, in real time. Nobody waits for a turn.

- Wind up, swing, recover. A heavy move leaves you open while it gathers.
- Quick pokemon get several hits in before a slow one lands.
- Your pokemon choose their own moves, and never waste one on a target it
  cannot touch.
- Conditions and weather carry out of the fight, so a party is something to look
  after.

## Raids

A lair holds a legendary for a few hours, and anybody may join.

- Bring up to six pokemon and up to twenty players, or just stand there and
  watch.
- The boss hits your whole party at once, and stands still at the start while
  you set up.
- Beat it once and the lair closes until it restocks. Everyone in the lobby is
  paid the same.
- **Mythicals** appear only when somebody spends a relic to call one out.
- Bring a friend into a lobby, to fight or just to watch.
- **Team Rocket grunts** block a path with three shadowed pokemon and pay out
  when you beat them.

## The league, and other trainers

The road is full of people who want a fight.

- **Duelling trainers** stand at their own cells: an Ace, or an expert in one
  type, with a purse on the win.
- **Gym leaders** hold the region's eight badges, one badge each.
- The **Elite Four** want every badge before they will see you, and the
  **Champion** wants all four of their marks.
- **Gym seats** are held by other players. Beat the party standing there and you
  take a share of their gold and the cell itself, then sit down on it yourself.
- **Duels** are two players who both chose to fight, in a private lobby, with
  whoever they invite watching. Nothing is staked and nothing is lost.

## Quests, badges and titles

- A **quest board** asks for things you were going to do anyway and pays for
  them: catches, hatches, walks, raids, trades.
- **Three dailies and a weekly hunt** on top of it, the same for everybody in
  the world, counted from the moment the day first sees you.
- **Achievements** climb four tiers on lifetime counters, per deed, per type,
  and per class of trainer beaten.
- Wear one **title** over your name: League Challenger, Master Collector,
  whatever you have earned.

## Raising

Nothing levels up by turning up to a fight. You raise it.

- **Candy** is shared across a family, so catching Caterpies raises your
  Butterfree.
- You choose which stats a pokemon trains. **Wings** raise that ceiling.
- Walking together makes a pokemon fond of you, and some evolve on nothing else.
- Evolve by level, stone, friendship or trade. An **Everstone** stops all of it.

## Eggs

An egg is a pokemon you have not met yet, decided the moment you picked it up.

- Find them in **nests**, or ask the **breeder** for one.
- They hatch on steps, from a few hundred to several thousand.
- A pokemon with **Flame Body** at your side halves the walk.
- What comes out inherits from its parents, so a good pair is worth keeping.

## People

Someone different is standing at the crossroads every few hours, and you cannot
tell who until you get there.

- The **vendor** sells balls and medicine.
- The **breeder** makes an egg from two of your pokemon.
- The **daycare lady** takes half the remaining walk off an egg.
- **Nurse Joy** patches up the party.
- The **groomer** makes a pokemon fond of you far faster.
- The **Move Reminder** gives back something it forgot.
- The **Fossil Maniac** sells fossils and the **Fossil Scientist** revives them,
  which is the only way to meet an Omanyte, a Kabuto or an Aerodactyl.
- A **Team Rocket grunt**, who is not here to help.

Each helps you once, then it is a walk to another crossroads.

## Items

Hardly anything is for sale. You dig it up.

- **Item caches** hold whole stashes, and **berry patches** fruit all day.
- Odd things happening out in the open leave gems, plates and wings behind.
- A buddy with **Pickup** finds things while you walk.
- Gear keeps working: Leftovers, plates, weather rocks, and a **Utility Belt**
  that lets a pokemon carry two things.
- Berries fire once, exactly when needed. No medicine works once a fight starts.

## Trading

Two ways a pokemon changes hands.

- **The auction house**, open to everybody: list a lot and it leaves your bag
  until the auction ends, bid and the gold leaves your purse at once and comes
  back the moment somebody outbids you. One lot each, per day, and only the
  genuinely scarce may be listed.
- **A trade with a friend**: your pokemon for theirs, with gold either way if
  you like. What you offer is held while they think about it, and a traded
  pokemon can evolve the way only a traded one can.

## Friends and gifts

- Add a friend by their **friend code**, so somebody has to hand it to you
  first.
- Requests can be taken back. Blocking quietly ends the lot.
- **Gifts** are set aside for you: a pokemon, a stack for your bag, or a meeting
  where you still throw the ball.
- Four are waiting on day one: Bulbasaur, Charmander, Squirtle, and twenty Poke
  Balls. Take all three starters if you like.

## Where the art comes from

Every pokemon on screen is animated from **SpriteCollab**, the Pokémon Mystery
Dungeon sprite collection, whose artists are credited per sprite in that
project's own files. The overworld people are the **Pokengine** community's fan
sheets.

Everything in the interface is **pokesprite**: the item icons in the bag, the
eighteen type badges, and the three marks a move's category is shown by.

[Credits](../credits.md) names all of it, sheet by sheet, with the terms each
comes under.

## A few promises

- Nothing goes off when you walk over it. You click what you want.
- Everyone sees the same world at the same moment.
- Morning, evening and night are read off your clock, and they change what you
  meet.
- Nothing timed can be rushed by changing your device.
- A battle plays back exactly as it happened.
