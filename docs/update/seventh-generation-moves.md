# The seventh generation's moves

**68 new moves** from the seventh generation, taught to every pokemon already in
the game, **Z-Moves**, **Psychic Terrain** and **Aurora Veil**. The **Reveal
Glass** brings the Therian Formes, and every held form now shows its item.

Alola's pokemon arrive family by family.

## 68 new moves

From Shore Up to Double Iron Bash, with the Let's Go partner moves included.

- **Liquidation, High Horsepower, Throat Chop, Psychic Fangs, Stomping Tantrum,
  Pollen Puff and Leafage** fill out what a pokemon can throw.
- **Sunsteel Strike, Moongeist Beam, Photon Geyser, Spectral Thief, Multi-Attack
  and Double Iron Bash** are here for the pokemon they belong to.
- **Zippy Zap to Veevee Volley** are the Let's Go partner moves.

### Terrain and screens

- **Psychic Terrain** holds for **10 seconds**. Grounded pokemon are safe from
  the other side's moves that wind up faster than an ordinary move, and their
  Psychic moves hit 1.3x.
- **Aurora Veil** only goes up in hail or snow. It cuts both physical and
  special damage against the side by 1/3. It does not stack with Reflect or
  Light Screen, and Brick Break, Psychic Fangs and Defog take it down.
- **Glitzy Glow and Baddy Bad** put up Light Screen and Reflect over the user's
  side as they land.
- **Psychic Terrain washes the floor pink.**

### Guards, traps and timing

- **Baneful Bunker** guards the user and poisons whatever touches it.
- **First Impression** works once a trip onto the field. Throwing it spends
  Fake Out's and Mat Block's chance too.
- **Shell Trap** only goes off when a physical move hits the user during its
  wind-up, and then goes off at once. **Beak Blast** burns anything that
  touches the user during its wind-up.
- **Spotlight** draws every single-target move aimed at the target's side to
  the target.
- **Instruct** makes the target throw its last move again at once.
- **Laser Focus** makes the user's next move within 4 seconds a critical hit.
- **Throat Chop** stops the target using sound moves for 4 seconds.
- **Pollen Puff** heals a teammate instead of hitting it.

### Moves that read the fight

- **Sunsteel Strike, Moongeist Beam and Photon Geyser** ignore the target's
  abilities. Photon Geyser lands as a physical move when the user's Attack is
  higher.
- **Stomping Tantrum** hits 2x after the user's last move failed.
- **Core Enforcer** takes an ability off each target that is not winding up a
  move.
- **The 17 Memories** are held items that set Multi-Attack's type.

## Z-Moves

**A pokemon holding a Z-Crystal turns a matching move into its Z-Move** as the
move goes off. There is nothing to press, so a player and the AI get it the
same way.

- **One Z-Move a side a fight.** The first one a side throws spends it for every
  pokemon on that side.
- **The 18 type crystals**, such as Firium Z, turn any damaging move of their
  type into that type's Z-Move. Its power comes from the move it replaces: 100
  for 55 power or less, rising to 200 for 140 or more.
- **The 17 signature crystals**, such as Decidium Z, each turn one line's own
  move into that line's own Z-Move.
- **A status move of a type crystal's type still goes off as itself**, with
  the mainline Z-effect paid first: a stage raised, full HP, cleared drops, a
  higher critical hit ratio, or the other side's moves drawn to it.
- **A Mega never throws a Z-Move**, and nor does a pokemon holding its Mega
  Stone.
- **Every Z-Move has a picture of its own.** Each opens with the user gathering
  its Z-Power under a gold Z.
- **The crystals and the Memories are not found anywhere yet.** They come with
  Alola's items.

The [player's guide](../mechanics/battles.md#z-moves) has the full rules.

## Moves look like themselves

**Alola's signature moves have pictures of their own**, and every Z-Move has
one too.

## The Reveal Glass

- **A Tornadus, Thundurus or Landorus holding the Reveal Glass fights in its
  Therian Forme.** It wears Regenerator, Volt Absorb or Intimidate on top of its
  own ability.
- **The glass is in the prized band** of the item pool.
- **Every form held for a fight now floats its item over the pokemon**, the way
  a Mega floats its stone. That covers the Origin orbs, the Primal orbs, the
  Plates, the Drives, the Gracidea, the Prison Bottle and the Reveal Glass. A
  shape with no art of its own still reads as changed.

## Everything already here learns them

**Kanto, Johto, Hoenn, Sinnoh, Unova and Kalos** learn the new moves by level,
machine, tutor and egg, the way Sun, Moon, Ultra Sun and Ultra Moon teach them.

## Fixes along the way

- **Intimidate lands when it comes with a shape** put on as the pokemon enters,
  so Mega Manectric scowls as it Mega Evolves.
- **Each family has one species day a year**, on the day its number comes round
  to. Some days now feature two families.
- **Built teams carry Burn Up only as one big swing.** It burns the user's Fire
  type away and fails without it.
