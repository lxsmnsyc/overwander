Signature abilities for four more Kanto families:

- Taste Everything (Lickitung): a contact move it lands on a berry holder eats that berry, healing or curing it as the berry would.
- Smog Screen (Koffing): enemy moves are 15% less accurate while it stands.
- Corkscrew (Rhyhorn): its contact moves hit 1.15x and ignore any Defense the target has raised.
- Cushioned (Chansey): no single hit takes more than 1/6 of its HP off it.
