# Kitakami's legends, Terapagos and Pecharunt

**Munkidori, Fezandipiti, Ogerpon, Terapagos and Pecharunt** close out Paldea,
each with a signature ability.

## The Loyal Three

**Munkidori** keeps to the Wistful Fields on the grassland and **Fezandipiti**
to Oni Mountain. Okidogi waits for its art.

When a teammate faints, the next damaging move it lands is sure to badly poison,
with one more effect each:

- **Monkey's Oath (Munkidori):** that move winds up 40% faster.
- **Pheasant's Oath (Fezandipiti):** that move strikes every enemy.

## Ogerpon

**Ogerpon** hides in the Dreaded Den in the forest. It is always in one of its
four masks. The **Teal, Wellspring, Hearthflame and Cornerstone Masks** are new
items: using one on an Ogerpon puts it in that mask's shape for good, the way a
Rotom Catalog changes a Rotom.

- Each mask's ability is worn in battle: Defiant for Teal, Water Absorb for
  Wellspring, Mold Breaker for Hearthflame and Sturdy for Cornerstone.
- Its Tera type is always its mask's type, and Terastallized it takes that mask's
  Terastal shape.
- **Mask Aura:** while it wears a mask, its teammates' moves of that mask's type
  hit 1.2x.

## Terapagos

**Terapagos** sleeps in Area Zero's Underdepths, under the mountain. Tera Shift
turns it Terastal as it takes the field. One born with another ability keeps its
base shape.

- **Prismatic:** its Normal moves become whichever type hits the target hardest.

## Pecharunt

**Pecharunt** is mythical, so it is never met by chance. The **Poison Mochi**
calls it up at the Loyalty Plaza.

- **Puppet Strings:** a confused enemy's single-target moves aimed at it go to
  one of that enemy's own teammates instead.
