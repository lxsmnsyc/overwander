# Galar's routes

**35 of Galar's families**, from Grookey to Dragapult, each with a signature
ability. The **fossils revive from two halves**, and the eleven Gen 8
abilities they bring all work in battle.

## The starters

**Grookey, Scorbunny and Sobble** play for the team. Each time one lands a move
of its own type, it casts an assist move for its side, at most once every 8
seconds.

- **Drum Cue:** Helping Hand on the teammate who hits hardest.
- **Kick Cue:** After You on a teammate still winding up a move.
- **Scope Cue:** Spotlight on the enemy it hit, so the team's single-target
  moves go there.

They are met in the forest, the grassland and the bog, as rarely as the other
starters.

## The roads out of Postwick

- **Skwovet** steals the Berries an enemy would eat and eats them itself.
- **Rookidee** casts Wide Guard when an enemy winds up a move that hits its
  whole side.
- **Blipbug** has a teammate cast Detect when an enemy winds up a move at them.
- **Nickit** hands what it steals to its teammate lowest on HP with free hands.
- **Gossifleur** makes its team's healing from Leech Seed, Ingrain and Grassy
  Terrain 1.5x.
- **Wooloo** loses its fleece to the first Fire move that hits it: the burn is
  cured and it casts Agility.
- **Chewtle's** biting moves break Reflect, Light Screen and Aurora Veil.
- **Yamper** casts Charge each time its Speed rises.

## Galar Mine to Glimwood Tangle

- **Applin** shares each Berry it eats with its worst-hurt teammate. It evolves
  with a Tart Apple or a Sweet Apple.
- **Silicobra's** Ground moves hit every enemy while a sandstorm is up.
- **Cramorant** casts Stockpile with each Water move it lands.
- **Arrokuda** answers an enemy winding up a move at it with Aqua Jet.
- **Toxel** casts Charge when poison costs an enemy HP. It grows into the Amped
  or the Low Key Toxtricity by its nature.
- **Sizzlipede's** Fire moves burn up the target's Berry.
- **Clobbopus** catches its target in Octolock with its contact moves.
- **Pincurchin** strikes back with Thunder Shock at anything that touches it.
- **Sinistea** casts Healing Wish on its worst-hurt teammate as it faints. It
  moves into a Cracked Pot to evolve.
- **Hatenna and Impidimp cancel each other out.** Hatenna costs an enemy 1/8 of
  its HP for each stat it raises itself. Impidimp heals 1/8 of its HP each time
  an enemy's stat falls. Neither works while the other stands on the field.
- **Milcery** casts Decorate on any teammate it casts a status move on.
  Holding one of the seven Sweets, it evolves into the Alcremie that Sweet
  decorates.

## Circhester and Spikemuth

- **Snom** sends the added effects of moves that hit it back at whoever threw
  them: a burn, a flinch, a stat drop.
- **Eiscue** calls up hail when a physical move hits it.
- **Falinks** takes its first 5 hits at 0.5x, one trooper at a time. It is drawn
  as its Brass until the collection draws the whole troop.
- **Indeedee** casts Heal Pulse on a teammate hit super effectively. She is a
  pokemon of her own, the way Meowstic's female is.
- **Morpeko** casts Nuzzle at any enemy that eats a Berry.
- **Duraludon** takes an enemy's spread move alone, sparing its teammates.
- **Dreepy** launches a Dreepy with each Dragon move it lands, casting Dragon
  Darts at a second enemy.
- **Stonjourner** powers its team's moves up to 1.3x for 6 seconds, every 30
  seconds.

## The fossils

**Galar's fossils come in halves.** The Fossil Scientist takes a top half and a
bottom half, and spends both:

| Top             | Bottom           | Revives   |
| --------------- | ---------------- | --------- |
| Fossilized Bird | Fossilized Drake | Dracozolt |
| Fossilized Bird | Fossilized Dino  | Arctozolt |
| Fossilized Fish | Fossilized Drake | Dracovish |
| Fossilized Fish | Fossilized Dino  | Arctovish |

- **The halves are sold by the Fossil Maniac** at 12,000 gold each, and dug up
  like the other fossils.
- **Each one strikes with its tail's type.** Its head type's moves (Electric
  or Water) strike as its tail's type (Dragon or Ice) against any target the
  tail type hits harder.

## Gen 8 abilities

- **Libero** works like Protean.
- **Punk Rock** makes the holder's sound moves hit 1.3x, and sound moves hit it
  at 0.5x.
- **Cotton Down** takes 1 stage of Speed off everybody else when the holder is
  hit.
- **Ball Fetch** catches the first item somebody else throws with Fling.
- **Gulp Missile** brings Cramorant up from Surf or Dive with a catch, which it
  spits at whoever hits it next.
- **Propeller Tail and Stalwart** keep the holder's moves on whoever it aimed
  at.
- **Ice Scales** halves special damage.
- **Ice Face** takes the first physical hit and breaks Eiscue's ice. Hail or
  snow freezes it back.
- **Hunger Switch** swings Morpeko between Full Belly and Hangry each time it
  acts.
- **Power Spot** makes its teammates' moves hit 1.3x.

## Not here yet

**Rolycoly, Cufant, Mr. Rime and Zarude** wait for the collection to draw them.
Cramorant's catch is drawn as Cramorant alone for the same reason.
