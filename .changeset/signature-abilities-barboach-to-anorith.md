Signature abilities for five more Hoenn families:

- Silt Bed (Barboach): every grounded pokemon on the field, its own side included, has Speed count 0.9x.
- Dirty Fighter (Corphish): its moves hit 1.3x against a target still at full HP.
- Spin Balance (Baltoy): it cannot be flinched, cannot be forced off the field, and its stages cannot be lowered.
- Root Hold (Lileep): a move it lands stops that target fleeing for 6 seconds and counts their Speed 0.7x meanwhile.
- Claw Rush (Anorith): its moves hit 1.3x against any target slower than it.
