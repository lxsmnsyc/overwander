A built party is now composed rather than assembled: two cores and four supports, the weather settled once for all six by what the cores gain or lose under it, and no move handed to half the party. Moves, abilities, natures and gear are all chosen for the job and against each other, so nothing awakens an ability its own four moves never ask for, a Life Orb goes to the two doing the attacking, and a Choice item never locks a pokemon out of half its sheet.
