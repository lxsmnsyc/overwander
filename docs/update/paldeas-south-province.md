# Paldea's South Province

**Paldea's three starters** arrive, each with a signature ability. More of the
South Province follows family by family.

## The starters

**Sprigatito, Fuecoco and Quaxly** are performers. One of each one's blows
reaches past the pokemon it was thrown at.

- **Petal Scatter:** a Grass critical hit Sprigatito's line lands also deals
  1/4 of its damage to every other enemy.
- **Spreading Flame:** when a Fire move from Fuecoco's line burns a target,
  the other enemy lowest on HP is burned too.
- **Wake:** a Water move from Quaxly's line slows its target and every other
  enemy by one stage, at most once every 8 seconds.

Each final stage reaches four abilities:

- **Meowscarada:** Overgrow, Protean, Prankster and Magician.
- **Skeledirge:** Blaze, Unaware, Punk Rock and Flash Fire.
- **Quaquaval:** Torrent, Moxie, Dancer and Quick Feet.

They are met as rarely as the other starters: Sprigatito in the grassland in the
morning and day, Fuecoco on the savanna by day and evening, and Quaxly in the
swamp from morning to evening.
