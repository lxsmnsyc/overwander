Signature abilities for five more Hoenn families:

- Ore Hunger (Aron): Steel and Rock moves deal it no damage and heal it 1/4 of what they would have.
- Mind Over Body (Meditite): it takes 0.5x damage while it is casting or channelling a move.
- Jolt Start (Electrike): its first move of a battle goes off a step ahead of everything and hits 1.5x.
- Cheer On (Plusle): each time it acts, the ally lowest on HP gains a stage in its best stat, up to 3 times a battle.
- Jeer At (Minun): each time it acts, the enemy highest on HP loses a stage in its best stat, up to 3 times a battle.
