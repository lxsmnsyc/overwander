# From Route 5 to Driftveil

**Eleven more families** from the fifth generation: the tricksters and thinkers
along Unova's fifth road, the three that live in Chargestone Cave, and the four
around Driftveil and its Cold Storage. **Two mainline abilities that never
worked now do.**

Everything from before is still here.

## Route 5

**Zorua and Zoroark** cross the temperate forest and the woodland after dark.
Zorua becomes Zoroark at 30.

**Minccino and Cinccino** run the grassland and the shrubland by day. A Minccino
takes a **Shiny Stone**, so it never needs a level.

**Solosis, Duosion and Reuniclus** drift over the same two countries by day, and
**Gothita, Gothorita and Gothitelle** stand there at night. Both lines evolve at
32 and again at 41.

## Chargestone Cave

**Joltik and Galvantula, Ferroseed and Ferrothorn, and Klink, Klang and
Klinklang** are met underground, and on the mountain above the cave. The gears
are out by day and the other two after dark.

Joltik becomes Galvantula at 36, Ferroseed becomes Ferrothorn at 40, and Klink
becomes Klang at 38 and Klinklang at 49.

## Driftveil and the Cold Storage

**Basculin** holds the bog and the swamp at every hour. It comes in **two
schools that will not share a river**: the red stripe throws itself about and
the blue one takes the landing. That is the whole difference between them, and
each is its own dex entry.

**Ducklett and Swanna** fly over the same water by day, evolving at 35.

**Vanillite, Vanillish and Vanilluxe** cross the tundra and the glacier, at 35
and again at 47.

**Alomomola** swims the ocean and the coral reef at any hour.

## Two abilities that now work

- **Illusion.** A Zorua takes the field looking like the teammate standing
  furthest back, and the act drops the moment anything lands on it. Only the
  look changes. The line had no working ability at all before this.
- **Iron Barbs.** Whoever lands a contact move on a Ferroseed loses 1/8 of their
  HP to the spikes. That line had none either, and Rough Skin is now built from
  the same shared rule rather than its own copy of it.

## Eleven signature abilities

- **Bluff**, for the Zorua family. The first move that would hit it super
  effectively passes straight through, once a battle.
- **Clean Sweep**, for the Minccino family. It sweeps every hazard and screen
  off both sides of the field as it arrives.
- **Fixation**, for the Gothita family. Its whole team hits 1.2x against
  whichever enemy is lowest on HP.
- **Division**, for the Solosis family. It takes 1/4 of every blow aimed at a
  teammate in their place.
- **Static Feed**, for the Joltik family. It heals 1/8 of its HP whenever an
  Electric move lands on anybody, either side.
- **Thorn Curtain**, for the Ferroseed family. Its teammates take 0.85x from
  contact moves while it stands.
- **Meshing**, for the Klink family. While a teammate stands with it, its team
  takes 0.9x and its own moves hit 1.2x.
- **Blood Water**, for Basculin. Its moves hit 15% harder for each enemy already
  below 1/2 HP, up to 1.45x.
- **Swan Dance**, for the Ducklett family. Every dance move it uses raises its
  Speed 1 stage on top of what the dance does.
- **Flash Freeze**, for the Vanillite family. The first Ice move it lands each
  fight freezes the target outright.
- **Tide Pool**, for Alomomola. Every heal on its team is worth 1.3x while it
  stands, its own included.

**Two of them are a pair.** Gothita's line aims what its team throws at whoever
is failing, and Solosis' line takes a share of what is aimed at its teammates.

## What else they can be born with

**Each end of a line reaches a fourth.** A Zoroark can be born with Pickpocket,
Prankster or Trace, a Cinccino with Sturdy, a Gothitelle with Synchronize, a
Reuniclus with Telepathy, a Galvantula with Static, a Ferrothorn with Overcoat
or Sturdy, a Klinklang with Motor Drive, a Swanna with Rain Dish, an Alomomola
with Unaware, and either Basculin with Swift Swim.

## Where to look first

- **Grassland and shrubland by day**, for Minccino and Solosis, with Gothita
  there at night.
- **Temperate forest and woodland after dark**, for Zorua.
- **Any cave, or the mountain above one**, for Joltik, Ferroseed and Klink.
- **Bog and swamp**, for Basculin at any hour and Ducklett by day.
- **Tundra and glacier**, for Vanillite.
