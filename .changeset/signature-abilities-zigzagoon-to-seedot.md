Signature abilities for four more Hoenn families:

- Crooked Run (Zigzagoon): each time it acts, moves aimed at it are 0.9x as accurate, stacking 3 times, and a landed blow clears it.
- Cocoon (Wurmple): the first time it drops below 1/2 HP, it shells over for 4 seconds and both deals and takes 0.5x damage.
- Water Bloom (Lotad): it calls up Rain as it takes the field, and heals 1/16 of its HP each time it acts in Rain.
- Sun Root (Seedot): it calls up Sun as it takes the field, and its moves deal 1.3x damage in Sun.
