# The sixth generation's moves

**62 new moves** from the sixth generation, taught to every pokemon already in
the game, **terrain** under the field, and the **three starters** that came
with them.

The rest of that generation arrives family by family.

## 62 new moves

From Flying Press to Hyperspace Fury, with the Fairy moves the type had been
waiting for.

- **Moonblast, Play Rough, Draining Kiss, Dazzling Gleam and Disarming Voice**
  give Fairy a move list of its own.
- **Phantom Force, Petal Blizzard, Nuzzle, Parabolic Charge, Boomburst and
  Water Shuriken** fill out what a pokemon can throw.
- **Thousand Arrows, Precipice Blades, Origin Pulse, Dragon Ascent, Hyperspace
  Hole and Hyperspace Fury** are here for the pokemon they belong to.

### Terrain under the field

- **Electric, Grassy and Misty Terrain** each hold for **10 seconds** and reach
  only what stands on the ground.
- They cover **the whole field** in a fight between players, and **one team's
  layer** in a raid, the way weather does.
- **The ring under a side takes the terrain's colour** while it holds, so what
  the ground is doing is visible without reading anything.

### Guards, traps and webs

- **Sticky Web** is a new hazard laid under a side, and **Defog** clears it
  with the rest.
- **Spiky Shield and King's Shield** guard the user and punish whatever touches
  them. King's Shield still lets status moves through.
- **Mat Block and Crafty Shield** guard the whole team, one against damaging
  moves and one against status moves. Mat Block works once a trip onto the
  field, and throwing it spends Fake Out's chance too.
- **Fairy Lock** holds everybody on the field in place for 2 seconds. Ghost
  types walk out anyway.
- **A raid boss** is immune to Powder and Electrify, and is never staged with
  Hold Hands, Aromatic Mist, Celebrate or Happy Hour.

## Three more starters

Nine species, each with its learnset, its sprites and a candy of its own.
Chespin, Fennekin and Froakie each become their second stage at 16 and their
last at 36.

- **Chespin** lives in the temperate forest and the woodland by morning and
  day, **Fennekin** in the shrubland and the savanna by day and evening, and
  **Froakie** in the swamp and the bog in the evening and at night, on land or
  in the water.
- **Each family's signature pays out to its whole team.** Spine Bond has the
  team take physical moves at 0.85x, Ember Bond has it throw special moves at
  1.15x, and Shade Bond cuts its cast and channel times to 0.85x.
- **Two abilities arrive with them.** Bulletproof turns away every move that is
  thrown rather than swung, and Magician takes the held item off whatever it
  lands a move on.

## Moves look like themselves

**54 of the 62** carry a picture chosen for them, either their own or the one
an older move that does the same thing already uses. A terrain lights the floor
across the whole field, and Fairy moves burst into sparkles.

- **Thousand Arrows** rains arrows of earth and **Precipice Blades** drives
  stone blades up through magma.
- **Origin Pulse** fans beams down onto the target, **Dragon Ascent** dives out
  of the sky, and **Hyperspace Hole and Fury** strike out of rings.
- **Moonblast** brings a full moon down, **Play Rough** is a dust cloud of
  stars and hearts, and **Water Shuriken** throws spinning stars of water.
- **A move that does what an older one does is drawn like it.** Phantom Force
  strikes out of the dark like Shadow Force, Flying Press comes down like Sky
  Drop, and Draining Kiss drains like Giga Drain.

### Older signatures redrawn

- **Freeze Shock and Ice Burn** raise a crown of ice that shatters, **Blue
  Flare** stands up as a column of blue fire, and **Fusion Flare and Fusion
  Bolt** break open over the ground.
- **Techno Blast** fires a beam ringed with hexagons, **V-create** drives a
  great burning V down, and **Glaciate** raises ice spikes in a freezing wind.
- **Judgment** opens a wheel of light before its pillar lands, and **Seed
  Flare** gathers light before it flares.
- **Psystrike, Aeroblast and Secret Sword** each have their own picture: a
  shell of psychic shards driven in, a spiralling blast of air, and three cuts
  of a blade of light.
- **Sacred Sword** brings down three great swords, and **Spacial Rend** tears
  space open twice onto a starry dark before it shatters.

## Everything already here learns them

**Kanto, Johto, Hoenn, Sinnoh and Unova** learn the new moves by level,
machine, tutor and egg, the way X, Y, Omega Ruby and Alpha Sapphire teach them.

## Fixes along the way

- **A Ghost type can always be swapped out**, whatever an opponent has trapped
  it with, from a binding move to Mean Look, Arena Trap or Shadow Tag. What a
  pokemon does to itself, such as Ingrain, still holds it.
- **A Flying type brought down takes Ground moves at 1x.** Knocked down by
  Smack Down, held by Gravity or weighed down by an Iron Ball, it used to take
  nothing from them at all.
- **Rapid Spin clears every hazard** under its side, toxic spikes, stones and a
  sticky web included, and frees the user from a bind or a Leech Seed.
