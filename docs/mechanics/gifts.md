# Gifts

A **gift** is something the game sets aside for a player and waits with. It is
listed in the gifts dialog with a line saying why it is there, and nothing is
theirs until they take it.

Some gifts are for one player; others stand on everybody's shelf and are taken
once each. Either way a gift is decided before it is offered, so two players
taking the same open gift receive the same pokemon.

## What a gift can be

| Kind          | What claiming it does                                             |
| ------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **A pokemon** | Hands it over finished, in the ball the gift names                |
| **An item**   | Puts a stack straight into the bag                                |
| **A meeting** | Stages the pokemon and lets the player throw their own ball at it |

A gifted meeting never flees and never breaks out, so the only thing the player
decides is which ball it ends up in.

A gift may carry an expiry date, after which it is neither listed nor claimable.

## Starting out

Four gifts stand on every shelf, and each is taken once per player:

- **Bulbasaur**, **Charmander** and **Squirtle**, each at level 5.
- **20 Poke Balls** to throw at whatever comes next.

Nothing stops a player taking all three starters. Which one is a first partner is
a choice rather than a roll.

A gifted pokemon says where it came from rather than naming a place on the map,
and the trainer who gave it stands at the head of its ownership history.
