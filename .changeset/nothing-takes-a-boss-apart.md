'overwander': patch
---

A raid boss is now immune to the four moves that move abilities about. Role Play, Skill Swap, Gastro Acid and Worry Seed all fizzle against one, and the AI stops picking them there. They could take the Boss ability off it, which is what carries the raid's health pool, its stage immunities and its sweeping moves: a Worry Seed dropped a boss from 3200 HP to 160 mid-fight, and a Skill Swap handed the pool to whoever cast it. The special tier is out of their reach entirely now, so a shadow cannot be copied off a pokemon either.
