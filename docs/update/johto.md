# Johto

Johto is the second release. It adds the other hundred pokemon, the 83 moves
they brought with them, a second league, and Team Rocket's officers.

Everything Kanto had is still here.

## A hundred more pokemon

The dex runs to **251**. Johto is its own region in it, with its own quest chain
and its own medal.

- **Three new starters.** A new trainer now picks a first partner from six.
- **Nine babies**, among them Pichu, Cleffa, Igglybuff, Smoochum, Elekid, Magby,
  Togepi and Tyrogue. Nests lay them instead of the stage above.
- **Six legendaries**: Raikou, Entei, Suicune, Lugia, Ho-Oh and Celebi.
- **Eleven new evolutions of Kanto lines**, including Crobat, Espeon, Umbreon,
  Steelix, Scizor, Kingdra and Blissey.
- **25 new abilities**, among them Huge Power, Prankster, Magic Bounce, Speed
  Boost and Shadow Tag.

### Four new ways to evolve

- **Friendship.** Pichu, Cleffa and Igglybuff ask for it.
- **Time of day.** An Eevee becomes an Espeon by day and an Umbreon by night.
- **One stat against another.** A Tyrogue at level 20 becomes a Hitmonlee if its
  Attack beats its Defense, a Hitmonchan if it loses, and a Hitmontop if they
  tie.
- **A trade while holding an item**, which the trade spends. Steelix, Scizor,
  Kingdra, Politoed, Slowking and Porygon2 come this way.

## The wild is rebuilt

Spawn bands used to lump a line's stages together. They now follow the shape of
the line, and the level follows the stage.

- **Eight bands.** A first stage is common, a middle stage is rare, and a grown
  one is elusive.
- Each stage band is **half the one above it**, so a walk turns up the whole of
  a line rather than the bottom of it over and over.
- **A level comes from the stage.** A Charmander is met at 5 to 16, a Charmeleon
  at 16 to 36, a Charizard at 36 to 60. A species that never evolves is met at
  10 to 50, and a baby at 5 to 10.
- A catch pays **1 to 8 candy**.
- **Every day of the year features a family.** No day is blank now.
- **18 species** that named a home biome but appeared in no pool are found there
  at last, among them Lanturn, Quagsire, Ampharos, Ursaring and Piloswine.

**Item stashes are drawn from the ground they are buried in.** Evolution stones,
weather rocks, pearls, shells, mushrooms, star pieces, rare bones and pretty
wings each belong to the landscapes that suit them. Balls, medicine and gear are
still buried everywhere.

## Unown, all twenty-eight

Every letter is its own pokemon with its own sprite sheet.

- The letters stand in the **prized band of every biome** at equal odds, so
  which one you meet is the roll and not the ground.
- Each carries **Levitate** and three hidden abilities. One of them is the
  ability the letter stands for.
- The dex keeps **one row** for a pokemon with forms. Press it to open the whole
  alphabet.

## 83 new moves

Every Gold, Silver and Crystal move, merged into all 251 learnsets. **Dark and
Steel** arrive as attacking types.

- **Guards**: Protect, Detect and Endure.
- **Holds**: Mean Look, Spider Web, Encore and Spite.
- **The ghost moves**: Curse, Nightmare, Destiny Bond and Perish Song.
- **Party moves**: Belly Drum, Pain Split, Psych Up, Heal Bell, Safeguard and
  Baton Pass.
- **Spikes**, laid on the ground and swept away by Rapid Spin.
- **Pursuit** catches a pokemon on its way out at double power. Switching is no
  shelter from it.
- **Sketch** keeps what it drew, out of a raid or a fight against the world.
- **Moves that work out their own power**: Flail, Reversal, Return, Frustration,
  Present, Magnitude, Beat Up and Hidden Power. A pokemon now carries its
  **friendship** into battle, which is what Return is worth in its hands.

Each move is drawn rather than borrowed from an atlas. A ghost closes in on its
target, hearts rise off it, spikes settle along the ground, and a move that
bites or cuts is drawn from the flag it already carries.

## The whole party is on the field

Several moves now reach the caster's own side, which matters here because
teammates stand beside each other rather than on a bench.

- **Earthquake, Surf, Explosion and Self-Destruct** catch teammates.
- **Present** can be handed to a teammate, and the AI hands it to whoever is
  hurt.
- **Psych Up** copies the teammate who has built the most, and **Pain Split**
  shares with the healthiest one.
- A single-target attack can be aimed at a teammate that **absorbs its type**.
  The AI feeds Volt Absorb, Water Absorb, Flash Fire and the rest when the
  payout is worth a cast.
- **Transform, Sketch, Mimic and Encore** can all be aimed at a teammate.

## A second league

**Sixteen gyms**, eight badges in each region, and two champions.

- **Johto's eight leaders**, Falkner to Clair, keep gyms in the countries their
  type belongs to.
- **Will, Koga and Karen** take seats beside Kanto's Elite Four. **Bruno keeps a
  seat in each league**, which is two fights and two marks.
- **Lance** stands as Johto's champion. A champion seat rolls between him and
  Red's old chair, asks for its own league's Elite Four, and pays its own
  league's title.
- Every leader, elite and champion fields five of their own type and a
  **signature sixth**.
- **Giovanni keeps Kanto's eighth gym**, which is a Ground gym now.

## The Legend tier

A rank above the champions. It asks nothing of a challenger: no badges, no
league.

- A **legend** turns up in a champion's seat one window in sixty-four, and under
  the four rarest skies for certain.
- Their named six stand at **level 100** with three abilities, three held items
  and perfect stats each.
- Beating one pays **250,000 to 500,000 gold**, their mark, and an item off the
  rare band. One time in twenty-one the item is off the **special band**, which
  no other fight reaches.
- **Red** is the first legend, with his Mt. Silver party. **Blue** takes the
  Kanto champion's seat.

## Team Rocket's officers

- **Executives** Archer, Ariana, Proton and Petrel bar a Rocket cell one window
  in eight. They field six rare pokemon at the Elite Four's level, and any of
  the six can be taken off them.
- **A grunt fields six** now rather than three, and hands over one it was not
  fighting with.
- **Giovanni** fights at a champion's level.
- Rocket cells are marked in their own **crimson**.

## Kurt and the apricorns

- **Apricorn trees** stand in the world. Each bears one colour, on the same
  clock a berry patch fruits on.
- **Kurt** joins the wandering people. He carves for free, as often as you have
  fruit.
- **Seven balls** nobody sells. The **Fast Ball** answers anything quick, the
  **Heavy Ball** climbs to 4x by weight, the **Lure Ball** answers whatever a
  ripple startled out, and the **Friend Ball** hands over a pokemon that already
  likes you. The **Moon**, **Level** and **Love Balls** fill out the set.

## The beasts and the towers

- A lair can hold **more than one resident**, and the raid rolls which one is
  home. The **Burned Tower** holds all three beasts.
- **Four new lairs**: the Burned Tower, the Whirl Islands, the Bell Tower and
  the Ilex Forest shrine.
- The **GS Ball** is hidden in the special band of the item pool. Spending it
  calls Celebi to the shrine.
- **Celebi** also walks the temperate forest at the mythical rate, the way Mew
  walks the rainforest.

## Purses and parties

- **Every fight pays a purse of its own rung.** A roadside trainer or a grunt
  pays 5,000 to 15,000, a gym leader 20,000 to 50,000, an executive 40,000 to
  90,000, an elite 50,000 to 110,000, Giovanni 120,000 to 250,000, and a
  champion 150,000 to 300,000.
- **Raid purses climb with them**: 35,000 for a shadow raid, 80,000 for a
  legendary and 200,000 for a mythical.
- Elites, executives and champions **leave an item** behind as well.
- **Experts field pokemon raised for the fight.** A gym leader's six carry 10 in
  every value and 50 training points a stat. The Elite Four and the executives
  have perfect HP and Speed. A champion and Giovanni add the stats their species
  leans on.
- A gym leader's six each **hold an item** chosen for that species. Elites and
  executives carry a **second ability** as well, which catching your own never
  gives you. Champions carry two of each.
- What Team Rocket leaves behind **keeps how it was raised**.

## More trainers on the road

- **A trainer class belongs to a region.** A Kanto Swimmer and a Johto Swimmer
  are the same trade in two places, drawn differently, each fielding what their
  own region grows.
- **Johto brings ten classes of its own**, among them the Sage, the Skier, the
  Firebreather, the Medium and the Boarder.
- **Thirteen more classes** stand at duelling cells besides. A class can field a
  pair of types rather than one.
- **Every type the game grows** now has somebody on the road fielding it.

## Coats, marks and titles

- **Every expert is a sprite you can wear** once you hold their badge, mark or
  title.
- **Every Team Rocket rank leaves a mark**, from the rank and file to the
  executives to Giovanni. Each is worth the coat it was met in.
- A Kanto leader's **Heart Gold look** unlocks with that gym's badge and Johto's
  crown.
- Filling a region's dex is worth that **region's professor** to wear.

## Where the art comes from

Every pokemon ships as a folder out of **SpriteCollab**: one description, and a
drawing for each coat over it.

- **Kanto and Johto are both redrawn** from the current collection, with female
  coats for the 22 species that have them.
- Moves can ask for **thirteen more clips** than they could. A Karate Chop
  chops, a Gust flaps, an Earthquake rumbles and a Sing sings.
- The credits screen names **every artist**, grouped by what they drew. The
  landmark, decoration and tree artists are credited for the first time.

## Fixes along the way

- **The first frame carries less.** Moves, abilities, items and the battle
  engine are fetched when something asks for them.
- Pokemon draw on **older Safari and Firefox**, which used to see every one of
  them as Missingno.
- A **raid boss** is never staged with a move it cannot use, such as a heal it
  cannot receive.
- A pokemon casting a move over its own side **no longer turns its back** on the
  fight.
- **Shadows follow the sun** through a turn of the camera, and stop disappearing
  at two bearings of it.
- The menu keypad is laid out in themed rows, and **Inventory is called Bag**.
- **Seven Kanto spawns** filed in the wrong band are put right.
- The dashboard stops waiting forever on an account that is not there.
