Kalos trainers are on the road. 41 classes join the duelling stops, drawn in their XY sheets and fielding Kalos pokemon. 35 are Kalos's own takes on trades already met, from the Ace Trainer to the Hex Maniac, the Garçon and the Roller Skater. Six are trades nobody had before, each with its own achievement line and titles: the Sky Trainer, the Fairy Tale Girl, the Furisode Girl, the Butler, the Tourist and the Rising Star. The Kalos Ace travels everywhere the other Aces do. Stops in most biomes now roll from a longer list, so who stands at a given stop changes.
