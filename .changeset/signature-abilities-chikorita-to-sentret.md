Signature abilities for the first four Johto families:

- Sapmark (Chikorita): anything it lands a move on loses 1/16 of its HP each time it acts, and Chikorita drinks the same.
- Embermark (Cyndaquil): anything it lands a move on loses 1/16 of its HP each time it acts, or 1/8 while it is burned.
- Jawmark (Totodile): the one thing it has its jaws in loses 1/8 of its HP each time it acts, only ever one at a time.
- Sentry (Sentret): nobody on its side can be hit by a critical hit while it stands.
