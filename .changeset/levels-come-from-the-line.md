'overwander': patch
---

A wild pokemon's level is read from its own evolution line rather than from its rarity band: a Charmander is met at 5 to 16, a Charmeleon at 16 to 36, and a Charizard at 36 to 60. A stage whose next step is a stone, a trade or an evolution a later generation holds starts at 30, a species that never evolves is met at 10 to 50, and a baby or an unown at 5 to 10.

Changes world generation.
