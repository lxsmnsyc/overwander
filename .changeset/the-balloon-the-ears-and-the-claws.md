Three more Sinnoh families: Drifloon, Buneary and Glameow. A Drifloon is out at dusk and its Carry Off blows a target away one attack in five, as Whirlwind would; Buneary evolves by friendship rather than by level, and its Springheel raises Speed a stage for each of the first three attacks it lands; a Glameow's Velvet Claws hit 1.25x with contact moves against anything that has raised a stat that fight.

Two mainline abilities arrived with them: Flare Boost, which is 1.5x Special Attack while burned, and Klutz, which leaves whatever its holder is carrying doing nothing at all.
