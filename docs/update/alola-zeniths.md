# Alola's Zeniths

**60 new Zeniths**: one for every Alola pokemon that can have one, from
Decidueye to Melmetal, and one for each Alolan final of a Kanto line, from
Alolan Raticate to Alolan Marowak. Each is reached by holding its own **Zenith
Shard**, which is dug up where its line lives. A Zenith fights 100 above its
pokemon's base stat total, none of it in HP, and wears an ability its line can
never be born with. No two of Alola's share one.

## What to look at first

- **Alolan finals have their own Zeniths.** An Alolan Ninetales holds the
  Alolan Ninetales Zenith Shard and fights Ice/Fairy with Ice Scales. The Kanto
  shard does nothing for it. Alolan Raichu gets one too, since the Megas belong
  to Kanto Raichu.
- **Shape-shifters keep their Zenith.** Wishiwashi schools into its School
  Zenith, Minior's shell cracks to its core's Zenith, and Mimikyu's Busted Form
  stays a Zenith.
- **Kept and born shapes share one shard.** Dusk Mane and Dawn Wings Necrozma,
  each Oricorio style and each Lycanroc form have a Zenith of their own. Ultra
  Necrozma has none, because it needs the Ultranecrozium Z held where the shard
  would go.
- **Zenith Shiinotic** puts foes to sleep with Spore, and Bad Dreams then hurts
  them while they sleep.
- **Zenith Pheromosa** wears Magic Guard, so a missed High Jump Kick costs it
  nothing.
