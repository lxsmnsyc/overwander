Signature abilities for four more Hoenn families:

- Feeding Frenzy (Carvanha): it gains a stage of Attack whenever any enemy faints, up to 3 of them.
- Spout (Wailmer): its Water moves reach every enemy on the field, not only the one it aimed at.
- Magma Vent (Numel): the first time it drops below 1/2 HP, every enemy loses 1/8 of their HP, once per battle.
- Body Heat (Torkoal): its Defense and Special Defense count 1.3x while the Sun is up.
