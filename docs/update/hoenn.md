# Hoenn

Hoenn is the third release. It adds the last 135 pokemon of the region, the 102
moves they brought, a third league, the Battle Frontier, and two more syndicates
fighting over the map.

Everything Kanto and Johto had is still here.

## 135 more pokemon

The dex runs to **386**. Hoenn is its own region in it, with its own quest chain
and its own medal.

- **Three new starters.** A new trainer now picks a first partner from nine,
  across three regions.
- **Eight legendaries**: Regirock, Regice, Registeel, Latias, Latios, Kyogre,
  Groudon and Rayquaza.
- **Two mythicals**: Jirachi and Deoxys.
- **Two babies**, Azurill and Wynaut, which the nests lay instead of the stage
  above.
- **Two fossils**, the Root and the Claw, holding the Lileep and Anorith lines.
  Neither line is met in the wild.
- **17 new abilities**, among them Wonder Guard, Pure Power, Poison Heal,
  Truant, Air Lock, Simple, Normalize and Forecast.
- **The Hoenn Pokedex** asks for 27 caught, then 68, then 133, and pays a Master
  Ball, the **Hoenn Dex Medal** and **Professor Birch** to wear.

### New ways to evolve

- **What a pokemon was born as.** A Wurmple spins a Silcoon if it is male and a
  Cascoon if it is female. The catch sheet shows only the branch that pokemon
  fits, since gender is not something you can work towards.
- **A trade that forks.** A Clamperl becomes a Huntail or a Gorebyss depending
  on what it was holding when it changed hands.
- **A Prism Scale, or fondness at level 40.** Either turns a Feebas into a
  Milotic, and a species reachable by two roads never spends an item the other
  road did not ask for.
- **A Nincada at level 20 leaves a Shedinja behind**, which costs the Poke Ball
  you have to be carrying. It is met nowhere else.

### A form can be worn, or chosen

- A **Castform** takes the shape of the sky it is standing under: Fire in sun,
  Water in rain, Ice in hail. A worn shape is never spawned or caught, so
  **meeting the pokemon fills the whole set in**: seeing one Castform fills all
  four of its dex squares.
- A **Deoxys** is rearranged instead. Using a **Meteorite** on one offers every
  shape but the one it is standing in, so you pick the arrangement rather than
  rolling for it, and the rock is spent doing it. Any shape walks back to any
  other, and each fills its own dex square once it has been stood in.
- A **second arrangement costs a second rock**. Meteorites turn up in the prized
  band and nobody stocks them. Which shape a Deoxys is in never changes the band
  it is read at.

## The Battle Frontier

**Seven houses**, the rank above a champion. Each takes only somebody holding
the crown of the region it stands in, and each is the first fight in the game
whose **rules** differ rather than its roster. Three a side, level 100, and a
purse of 200,000 to 400,000.

| House          | Brain   | The house rule                                           |
| -------------- | ------- | -------------------------------------------------------- |
| Battle Tower   | Anabel  | Nothing. The fight the other six are read against        |
| Battle Pyramid | Brandon | Nobody holds an item, and yours leave theirs at the door |
| Battle Arena   | Greta   | Judged after 10 turns on the share each side has left    |
| Battle Pike    | Lucy    | Your three walk through a curtain and arrive afflicted   |
| Battle Factory | Noland  | The house lends both sides out of one crate of 6         |
| Battle Palace  | Spenser | Every pokemon picks its own moves and targets by nature  |
| Battle Dome    | Tucker  | He names nobody until your three are frozen              |

- Beating a house hangs its **silver symbol** on the shelf. Beating it without
  losing a pokemon hangs the **gold** one.
- **A Brain keeps a second team** for anybody holding their silver, so the gold
  symbol is a second fight rather than the same one scored twice.
- A battle records the house rule it was fought under, so **a replay is the
  fight that happened**, curtain roll and rented hand included.

## A third league

**Twenty-four gyms**, eight badges in each region, and three champions.

- **Hoenn's leaders**, Roxanne to Juan, keep gyms in the countries their type
  belongs to. Mossdeep is one gym kept by two people, and whichever of **Tate or
  Liza** a chunk seats, the Mind Badge is what it pays.
- **Sidney, Phoebe, Glacia and Drake** take the region's Elite Four seats, each
  fielding their type and nothing else.
- **Wallace** takes the champion's seat and pays the **Hoenn Champion** title.
- **Steven** is a legend, met in a champion's seat one window in sixty-four the
  way Red is, asking for nothing.
- Every leader is drawn in their **Ruby and Sapphire coat**, and Roxanne,
  Flannery, Tate and Liza carry an **Omega Ruby** one as well.

## Experts build their parties

From the Elite Four upwards, a trainer no longer fights with the last four moves
it levelled into.

- Its six are **composed**: two cores and four supports, the weather settled
  once for all of them, and no move handed to half the party.
- It carries **the four moves its species is best with**, and moves, abilities,
  natures and gear are chosen against each other. Nothing wakes an ability its
  own moves never ask for.
- Its gear is **priced rather than ordered**: a Life Orb goes to something that
  hits hard enough to pay the recoil, Leftovers to a wall, a Choice item to a
  pokemon built around one blow. Nobody carries two of a kind.

## Three teams, one landmark

- **Team Aqua** keeps the crime landmark on the water, **Team Magma** on the
  volcanoes and the dry country, and **Team Rocket** everywhere else.
- Each has its own boss, executives, uniform and marks: **Archie** and
  **Maxie**, with Matt and Shelly, Tabitha and Courtney under them.
- Everybody is named team and rank first, as in **Team Aqua Leader Archie**.
- **A grunt fields two out of each of the biome's three bands** and leaves any
  one of its six behind, rather than the half it was not fighting with.
- A pokemon taken off any of them is recorded as **taken from a syndicate**, so
  the box search term is `met:syndicate`.

## Catching is a throw you can read

- **A ball rocks up to three times** before it holds, and how far it got says
  how close the throw came. The odds are unchanged: three independent shakes
  multiply back to the old chance.
- **A critical throw holds on one shake.** How often depends on how many species
  you have caught.
- The **Catching Charm** turns up in the rarest band of found items and puts
  half again on every throw.
- **Twenty more buddy abilities have a say in a safari.** Trace shows a wild
  pokemon's ability before the first ball. Magnet Pull holds a Steel type still.
  Arena Trap and Shadow Tag halve the chance one bolts. Super Luck doubles how
  often a critical throw comes along, and Sniper gives the shake behind one two
  chances. A buddy that absorbs a type makes a wild pokemon of that type half
  again as easy to catch.

## Speed buys back the wait

Speed now shortens cooldowns. **Every 512 points halves what is left of a
move's wait**, closing on 95% off, read with stages, abilities and held items
in. Everything that moved Speed did nothing before this.

## A nature you can change

**21 mints**: one for every nature that moves a stat, and a **Serious Mint** for
a pokemon that should move none. A mint is spent on one pokemon and changes its
nature for good, so a pokemon raised for years is no longer stuck with the roll
it was met on.

- The **wandering chef** sells every one of them, and the ground hides them in
  the prized band.
- **A vendor counter lays out 12 kinds** now rather than 6, or its whole shelf
  where it carries fewer.

## 102 new moves

Every Ruby, Sapphire and Emerald move, merged into all 386 learnsets. The Kanto
and Johto species learn what gen 3 teaches them on top of everything they
already knew.

- **Holds**: Taunt, Torment and Imprison keep a pokemon out of part of its move
  set, and Yawn puts one to sleep 4 seconds after it hears it.
- **Winds up**: Focus Punch is lost if its user is hit while charging, and
  Revenge hits twice as hard for the same thing.
- **Stockpile** fills its store over 3 steps, a charge and a pair of stat stages
  each, and Spit Up and Swallow are worth what it put away.
- **Theft**: Trick swaps held items, Knock Off knocks one away, Recycle picks up
  what was spent, and Role Play and Skill Swap move abilities about.
- **Cover**: Follow Me takes what an ally was about to take, Magic Coat turns a
  status move back on its caster, and Snatch takes a self-cast move for itself.
- **Later**: Wish heals 4 seconds on and can be left with a party member, and
  Doom Desire lands like a Future Sight.
- **The ground the fight is on.** A battle now carries its biome, and Nature
  Power, Secret Power and Camouflage each read it. A swamp, a glacier and a
  forest throw something different.
- **Uproar** is a rampage that keeps the field awake for as long as it runs, and
  **Fake Out** is one surprise per trip onto the field.

Each move is drawn rather than borrowed from an atlas. Eruptions and spouts pour
down, Ingrain and Frenzy Plant put roots up through the floor, Dive goes under
and Bounce goes up. **A sound move is heard rather than seen**, read off the
move's own flag. Taunt, Torment, Imprison, Ingrain, Yawn, Follow Me, Magic Coat,
Snatch, Grudge, Uproar and Helping Hand all leave a mark on the pokemon carrying
them.

## The road is busier

- **Trainers are met out on the open seas**, and only the ones who could be
  there: the swimmers, fishermen, sailors and tubers a country puts on its
  water.
- **Hoenn brings 24 classes of its own**, among them the Ninja Boy, the Tuber,
  the Ruin Maniac, the Kindler and the Aroma Lady, covering every type the
  region grows.
- **Nine new lairs**: the Desert Ruins, the Island Cave, the Ancient Tomb,
  Southern Island, the Marine Cave, the Terra Cave, the Sky Pillar, Birth Island
  and Forina.
- The **Aurora Ticket** and the **Wish Tag** join the special band, and each
  calls its mythical out wherever the player is standing.
- **Nothing fruits on the lava.** Berry patches and apricorn trees only stand
  where they could grow.

## Fixes along the way

- **A critical hit is even money two stages up and certain three**, in place of
  the doubling that never reached a certainty.
- **A blow a substitute ate, or an immunity refused, carries no secondary
  effect**, and the type chart no longer scales a status move.
- **A move that deals no damage is no longer stopped by the type chart**, so a
  Ghost hears a Growl and a Normal type sees a Confuse Ray. Thunder Wave still
  fails against a Ground type.
- **Teeter Dance confuses everything else on the field**, and Soft-Boiled and
  Milk Drink can be aimed at a party member and heal a share of that pokemon's
  health.
- **Ice Ball rolls 5 times in one cast**, and Flatter, Tickle, Feather Dance,
  Memento, Metal Sound and Fake Tears may be aimed at an ally.
- **A raid boss is never staged with Wish, Ingrain, Slack Off or Swallow**, or
  with Role Play, Skill Swap, Memento, Grudge or Endeavor.
- **Taking a unit off a team, or a team out of an alliance, removes it** rather
  than putting it back.
- **The credits keep the drawings under each artist's name**, and names that
  differ only in case or by a bracketed tag are read as one person.
- **A stop dialog says what a challenger fields once.** The team size and level
  band no longer sit above a stake line that already gives both, and a Team
  Rocket grunt's stake line names its level band the way every other
  challenger's does.
