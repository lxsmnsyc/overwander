# Johto

**Johto** is the second release: the other hundred pokemon, the moves they
brought with them, a second league to beat, and Team Rocket back in business.

Kanto is still there. Everything below is on top of it.

## A hundred more pokemon

The dex runs to 251, and Johto is a region the game pages, walks and awards
apart.

- **Three new starters** on the shelf beside Kanto's. Pick from six on your
  first day.
- **Nine babies**: Pichu, Cleffa, Igglybuff, Smoochum, Elekid, Magby, Togepi and
  Tyrogue among them, laid by the nests that used to lay the stage above.
- **Legendaries**: Raikou, Entei, Suicune, Lugia, Ho-Oh and Celebi.
- **Eleven Kanto lines grow again**: Crobat, Espeon, Umbreon, Steelix, Scizor,
  Kingdra, Blissey and the rest.
- **25 new abilities**, from Huge Power and Prankster to Magic Bounce, Speed
  Boost and Shadow Tag.
- Every one of them walks the countries it belongs in, so the wild is different
  wherever you already were.

New ways to evolve, too. A pokemon can grow on **friendship**, on the **time of
day** (Espeon by day, Umbreon by night), or on **its own stats**: a Tyrogue at
level 20 becomes a Hitmonlee, a Hitmonchan or a Hitmontop depending on which
half of it is stronger.

## Unown, all twenty-eight

Every letter is its own pokemon, with its own sheet, standing in the prized band
of every biome at equal odds.

- Which letter you meet is the roll, not the ground you walked.
- Each carries **Levitate** plus three hidden abilities, one of them the
  letter's own.
- The dex keeps one row for a pokemon with forms. Press it and the whole
  alphabet opens.

## 83 new moves

Every Gold, Silver and Crystal move, merged into all 251 learnsets, with **Dark
and Steel** arriving as attacking types.

- **Guards**: Protect, Detect and Endure.
- **Holds**: Mean Look, Spider Web, Encore and Spite.
- **The ghost pair**: Curse, Nightmare, Destiny Bond and Perish Song.
- **Party moves**: Belly Drum, Pain Split, Psych Up, Heal Bell, Safeguard and
  Baton Pass.
- **Spikes**, laid on the ground and swept away by Rapid Spin.
- **Pursuit** catches a pokemon on its way out, at double power. Switching is no
  shelter.
- **Sketch** keeps what it drew, out of a raid or a fight with the world.
- Moves that work out their own power: Flail, Reversal, Return, Frustration,
  Present, Magnitude, Beat Up and Hidden Power. A pokemon carries its
  **friendship** into battle now, which is what Return is worth in its hands.

Each one is drawn rather than borrowed: a ghost closes in, hearts rise off a
target, spikes settle along the floor, and anything that bites or cuts takes its
picture from what it is.

## A second league

Sixteen gyms, eight badges each region, and two champions.

- **Johto's eight leaders**, Falkner to Clair, keeping gyms in the countries
  their type belongs to.
- **Will, Koga and Karen** take seats beside Kanto's Elite Four, and **Bruno
  keeps one in each league**: two seats, two fights, two marks.
- **Lance** stands as Johto's champion. A champion seat rolls between him and
  Red's old chair, asks for its own league's Elite Four, and pays its own
  league's title.
- Every leader, elite and champion fields five of their own type and a
  **signature sixth**.
- **Giovanni keeps Kanto's eighth gym**, which is a Ground gym now rather than
  the one gym about nothing.

## The Legend tier

Above the champions, a fight that asks nothing of you and gives up nothing
easily.

- A **legend** turns up in a champion's seat one window in sixty-four, and under
  the four rarest skies for certain. No badges asked, no league.
- Their named six stand at **level 100**, each with three abilities and three
  held items.
- Beating one pays **250,000 to 500,000**, their mark, and an item off the rare
  band. One time in twenty-one it is off the special band, which is the only
  fight in the game that reaches it.
- **Red** is the first, with his Mt. Silver party. **Blue** takes the Kanto
  champion's seat.

## Team Rocket, promoted

- **Executives**: Archer, Ariana, Proton and Petrel bar a Rocket cell one window
  in eight, fielding six rare pokemon at the Elite Four's level, and any of the
  six can be taken off them.
- **Grunts field six** now rather than three, and hand over one they were not
  even fighting with.
- **Giovanni** fights at a champion's level.
- Rocket cells are called out in their own crimson.

## Kurt, and the apricorns

- **Apricorn trees** stand in the world, each bearing one colour, on a berry
  patch's clock.
- **Kurt** joins the wanderers in his Heart Gold coat, and carves free, as often
  as you have fruit.
- **Seven balls** nobody else sells: the **Fast Ball** for anything quick, the
  **Heavy Ball** climbing to 4x by weight, the **Moon Ball**, the **Lure Ball**
  for whatever a ripple startled out, the **Level** and **Love Balls** thrown
  from behind your buddy, and the **Friend Ball**, which hands a pokemon over
  already fond of you.

## The beasts and the towers

- A lair can hold **more than one resident**, and the raid rolls who is home.
  The **Burned Tower** holds all three beasts.
- Four new lairs: the Burned Tower, the **Whirl Islands**, the **Bell Tower**,
  and the **Ilex Forest shrine**.
- The **GS Ball** is hidden in the special band of the world, and calls Celebi
  out at the shrine.

## Worth more, and better armed

- **Every fight pays its own rung.** A roadside trainer or a grunt pays 5,000 to
  15,000, a gym leader 20,000 to 50,000, an executive 40,000 to 90,000, an elite
  50,000 to 110,000, Giovanni 120,000 to 250,000, a champion 150,000 to 300,000.
- Raids climb with them: 35,000 for a shadow raid, 80,000 for a legendary,
  200,000 for a mythical.
- **Experts come field-trained.** A gym leader's six each hold an item chosen
  for that species. Elites and executives carry a **second ability** besides,
  which is the one thing catching your own never gives you, and champions carry
  two of each.
- What Team Rocket leaves behind **keeps how it was raised**.
- Elites, executives and champions leave an item as well.

## A busier road

- **Trainer classes belong to a region.** A Kanto Swimmer and a Johto Swimmer
  are the same trade twice, drawn differently, each fielding what their own
  region grows.
- **Johto brings ten classes of its own**, from the Sage and the Skier to the
  Firebreather, the Medium and the Boarder.
- **Thirteen more** stand at duelling cells besides, and a class may field a
  pair of types rather than one.
- Every type the game grows now has somebody on the road fielding it.

## Coats, marks and titles

- **Every expert is a sprite you can wear**, once you hold their badge, mark or
  title.
- **Every Team Rocket rank leaves a mark**, from the rank and file to each
  executive to Giovanni himself, each worth the coat it was met in.
- A Kanto leader's **Heart Gold look** unlocks with that badge and Johto's
  crown: the same gym, years later.
- **Johto has a dex chain and a medal of its own**, and filling a region's dex
  is worth that region's professor to wear.

## Where the art comes from

Every pokemon ships as a folder out of **SpriteCollab**: one description, and a
drawing per coat over it.

- Kanto and Johto both redrawn from the current collection, with **female
  coats** for the 22 that have them, and shinies throughout.
- Moves reach for thirteen more clips than they could. A Karate Chop chops, a
  Gust flaps, an Earthquake rumbles, a Sing sings.
- The credits screen now names **everybody**: every artist, grouped by what they
  drew, along with the landmark, decoration and tree artists for the first time.

## Fixes along the way

- The first frame carries less: moves, abilities, items and the battle engine
  are fetched when something asks for them.
- Pokemon draw on **older Safari and Firefox**, which used to see every one of
  them as Missingno.
- The menu keypad is laid out in themed rows, and Inventory is called **Bag**.
- Seven Kanto spawns filed in the wrong rarity band are put right.
- The dashboard stops waiting forever on an account that is not there.
