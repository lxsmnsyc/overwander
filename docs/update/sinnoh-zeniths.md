# Sinnoh's Zeniths

**54 new Zeniths**, one for every Sinnoh pokemon that can have one, from
Torterra to Manaphy. Each is reached by holding its own **Zenith Shard**, which
is dug up where its line lives. A Zenith fights 100 above its pokemon's base
stat total and wears an ability its line can never be born with; no two of
Sinnoh's share one.

## What to look at first

- **Zenith Rotom** has a shape for each of its five appliances. Whichever one
  holds the Rotom Zenith Shard fights as that appliance's Zenith, and each lays
  Electric Terrain as it comes in.
- **Zenith Wormadam** fights in whichever cloak it was born in: Plant, Sandy or
  Trash, each with its own spread, and Fur Coat over all three.
- **Zenith Cherrim** brings its own sun, so it opens into its Sunshine Zenith
  the moment it lands.
- **Zenith Regigigas** still starts slow, but Multiscale halves the first hit
  while it wakes up.
- **Zenith Togekiss** wears Pixilate, so Extreme Speed is a Fairy move that
  goes first.
