# Unova's Zeniths

**69 new Zeniths**, one for every Unova pokemon that can have one, from
Victini to Meloetta. Each is reached by holding its own **Zenith Shard**, which
is dug up where its line lives. A Zenith fights 100 above its pokemon's base
stat total, none of it in HP, and wears an ability its line can never be born
with; no two of Unova's share one.

## What to look at first

- **Zenith Kyurem** has a shape for Black and for White Kyurem. Whichever
  dragon it holds, it keeps the Kyurem Zenith Shard and Ice Scales.
- **Zenith Darmanitan** is Fire/Fighting until it drops below half. Then Zen
  Mode turns it into its Zen Mode Zenith, Fire/Psychic, and Berserk raises its
  Special Attack as it changes.
- **Zenith Meloetta** dances from Aria to Pirouette and stays a Zenith, and
  Scrappy lets Hyper Voice and Relic Song hit Ghosts.
- **Zenith Sawsbuck** fights in whichever season it was born in, and **Zenith
  Basculin** in whichever stripe, both under one shard.
- **The Simisage, Simisear and Simipour Zeniths** bring their own Grassy
  Terrain, sun and rain.
