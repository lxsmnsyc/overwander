# Pinwheel Forest

**Nine more families** from the fifth generation: everything Unova's great
forest holds, from the tailors in the canopy to the toads in the mud, with a
signature ability for each of them.

Everything from before is still here.

## The outer woods

**Sewaddle, Swadloon and Leavanny** are the tailors of the temperate forest and
the woodland, out in the morning and by day. Sewaddle becomes Swadloon at 20,
and **a Swadloon becomes a Leavanny once it is fond enough of its trainer**.

**Venipede, Whirlipede and Scolipede** work the same two woods after dark.
Venipede becomes Whirlipede at 22 and Scolipede at 30.

**Cottonee and Whimsicott** blow across the grassland and the shrubland, and
**Petilil and Lilligant** root in the temperate forest and the grassland, both
in the morning and by day. **Each takes a Sun Stone**, so neither ever needs a
level.

## The inner woods

**Timburr, Gurdurr and Conkeldurr** carry their beams through the same forest
and woodland by day. Timburr becomes Gurdurr at 25, and **a Gurdurr becomes a
Conkeldurr on being traded**.

**Tympole, Palpitoad and Seismitoad** croak through the bog and the swamp after
dark. Tympole becomes Palpitoad at 25 and Seismitoad at 36.

**Audino** stands alone in the forest and the woodland by day.

**Throh and Sawk** are the two halves of one dojo. **Both are written but
neither is out in the world yet**, since nobody has drawn a Throh, and a pair
waits for both its halves.

## Nine signature abilities

- **Tailor**, for the Sewaddle family. It dresses its worst hurt teammate as it
  arrives, and that one takes 0.8x for the rest of the fight.
- **Hurry Venom**, for the Venipede family. Poison it puts on an enemy costs
  them 1.5x as much each time they act.
- **Spore Drift**, for the Cottonee family. Its powder moves cannot miss, and
  nothing is immune to them.
- **Pollen Waltz**, for the Petilil family. Every stage one of its dances gives
  it is given to its teammates as well.
- **Load Bearing**, for the Timburr family. At or above 1/2 HP it takes 0.6x
  from everything. Below that it takes the lot and deals 1.4x.
- **Ripple Out**, for the Tympole family. A move it lands also deals 1/4 of
  that damage to every other enemy.
- **Ward**, for Audino. While it stands, the first blow that would finish a
  teammate leaves them on 1 HP instead, once per teammate.
- **Red Belt** and **Blue Belt**, for Throh and Sawk. Physical moves hit the
  Throh's team at 0.85x, and the Sawk's team throws them at 1.15x. Both cover
  the holder too.

**Two pairs answer each other.** Cottonee carries powder to anything at all and
Petilil shares its dances with its own side, and the dojo splits the same way:
one belt covers a team against physical moves, the other arms it.

## What else they can be born with

The ordinary pools are the mainline's: Swarm and Chlorophyll on the tailors,
Poison Point and Swarm on the centipedes, Prankster and Infiltrator on Cottonee,
Chlorophyll and Own Tempo on Petilil, Guts and Sheer Force on Timburr's line,
Swift Swim and Hydration on the toads, Healer and Regenerator on Audino, and
Inner Focus across the dojo. Overcoat, Speed Boost, Chlorophyll, Leaf Guard,
Iron Fist, Water Absorb, Klutz and Mold Breaker are the hidden ones.

**Each end of a line reaches a fourth.** A Scolipede can be born with Rough
Skin, a Whimsicott with Magic Bounce, a Lilligant with Serene Grace, a
Conkeldurr with Rock Head, an Audino with Friend Guard, a Throh with Stamina
and a Sawk with Scrappy.

## A fix along the way

- **A built expert no longer carries a move its pokemon can never make good
  on**, such as a Dream Eater on something with no way to put anybody to sleep.

## Where to look first

- **Temperate forest and woodland by day**, for Sewaddle, Timburr and Audino.
- **The same woods after dark**, for Venipede.
- **Bog and swamp at night**, for Tympole.
- **Grassland and shrubland in the morning**, for Cottonee, with Petilil in the
  forest beside it.
