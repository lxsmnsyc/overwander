Signature abilities for four more Kanto families:

- Full Belly (Snorlax): it heals 1/16 of its HP every time it acts, and its cast times are 25% longer.
- Frostwing (Articuno): every enemy loses a stage of Speed as it arrives on the field.
- Stormwing (Zapdos): every enemy loses a stage of Special Defense as it arrives on the field.
- Emberwing (Moltres): every enemy loses a stage of Defense as it arrives on the field.
