# Kalos's last families and its legends

**Fifteen more pokemon from Kalos**: the slug dragons of the swamps and the bat
dragons of the night, the last of Kalos's families, and **Xerneas, Yveltal and
Zygarde**. Each family carries **a signature ability of its own**.

Everything from before is still here.

## Twelve pokemon in the wild

| Family                 | Where                                         | When               | Evolves                      |
| ---------------------- | --------------------------------------------- | ------------------ | ---------------------------- |
| Goomy, Sliggoo, Goodra | The bogs and swamps, on land and in the water | Any hour           | At 40, then at 50            |
| Noibat, Noivern        | The woodland and the tropical rainforest      | Evening and night  | At 48                        |
| Pancham, Pangoro       | The tropical seasonal forest and the woodland | Morning to evening | At 32                        |
| Swirlix, Slurpuff      | The grassland and shrubland                   | By day             | Traded holding Whipped Dream |
| Sylveon                | The grassland and woodland                    | Any hour           | From Eevee, see below        |
| Dedenne                | The grassland, the woodland and the caves     | Any hour           | Never                        |
| Carbink                | The mountains, the badlands and the caves     | Any hour           | Never                        |

- **An Eevee kept close that knows Baby-Doll Eyes becomes a Sylveon.** It
  stands beside the other eeveelutions.
- **Sliggoo and Pancham evolve on level alone.** The mainline also asks for rain
  and for a Dark type in the party, which nothing here can measure yet.
- **Litleo and Spritzee wait.** Nobody has drawn a male Pyroar, and Aromatisse
  has only two of its animations.
- **All of them learn their moves** by level, machine, tutor and egg, as they do
  in X, Y, Omega Ruby and Alpha Sapphire.

## Kalos's three legendaries

| Legendary | Where                             | Lair          |
| --------- | --------------------------------- | ------------- |
| Xerneas   | The temperate forest and woodland | Winding Woods |
| Yveltal   | The mountains and the tundra      | Frost Cavern  |
| Zygarde   | The mountains and the badlands    | Terminus Cave |

- **Each is at home where its own story happens.** Kalos catches its mascots in
  a laboratory under a town, which a world made of open country has no place
  for.
- **Zygarde carries three shapes.** It is met as the 50% shape. The 10% shape is
  its own entry. Power Construct turns it into the Complete shape at 1/2 HP.
- **The Zygarde Cube is dug out of the ground.** Used on a Zygarde, it puts it
  into its other shape, either way round. It is spent on each
  change.

## A signature for each of them

The slug and the bat:

- **Seepage**, Goodra's. It holds back 40% of every direct blow it takes and
  pays it out over the next 4 seconds, so a heal can land before the damage
  does.
- **Echolocation**, Noivern's. It hears whoever lands a move on it, and its
  next damaging move at that enemy is a critical hit.

The last families:

- **Begrudge**, Pangoro's. It gains 1 stage of Attack every time an enemy is
  healed.
- **Sugar Rush**, Slurpuff's. Its whole team gains 1 stage of Speed whenever it
  uses up its own held item.
- **Quick Whiskers**, Dedenne's. It casts a step ahead. This shortens cast time,
  not cooldown.
- **Crystal Growth**, Carbink's. It gains 1 stage of Defense and 1 of Special
  Defense every 10 seconds, up to 3 of each.

The legendaries each work on stat stages in a different way:

- **Quickening**, Xerneas's. Every stat rise on its team is 1 stage bigger.
- **Withering**, Yveltal's. Every stat drop it lands is 1 stage deeper.
- **Even Keel**, Zygarde's. Every stat stage on the field counts at 0.5x, its
  own included.

## Abilities now built

- **Gooey**: whatever makes contact with a Goomy, Sliggoo or Goodra loses 1
  stage of Speed.
- **Sweet Veil**: nothing on a Swirlix or Slurpuff's team can be put to sleep.
- **Pixilate**: a Sylveon's Normal moves go out as Fairy moves at 1.2x.
- **Fairy Aura and Dark Aura**: every move of that type on the field hits 1.33x,
  whoever throws it.
- **Aura Break**: while it is on the field, each aura gives 0.75x instead.
- **Power Construct, Triage and Earth Eater.**

## Other abilities

- **Goodra** takes Water Absorb, and **Noivern** takes Soundproof.
- **Pangoro** takes Guts, and **Slurpuff** takes Friend Guard and Thick Fat.
- **Dedenne** takes Minus, and **Carbink** takes Levitate and Solid Rock.
- **Xerneas** takes Healer, Multiscale and Triage.
- **Yveltal** takes Moxie, Merciless and Shadow Tag.
- **Zygarde** is born with Aura Break, and takes Power Construct, Earth Eater
  and Sturdy in its hidden band.
