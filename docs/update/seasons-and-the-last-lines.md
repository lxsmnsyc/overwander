# Seasons and the last of Unova's lines

**The world now has seasons**, and Deerling and Sawsbuck wear them. Emolga
glides into the forest, and **the fourteen Unova lines the region was still
missing** are written into the dex.

Everything from before is still here.

## Seasons

The world is in one of four seasons at a time, and **the season changes every
month**.

| Month                          | Season |
| ------------------------------ | ------ |
| January, May, September        | Spring |
| February, June, October        | Summer |
| March, July, November          | Autumn |
| April, August, December        | Winter |

**Everybody is in the same season at once**, wherever they are standing.

## Deerling and Sawsbuck

- **They stand in the grassland and the temperate forest** at any hour.
  Deerling becomes Sawsbuck at 34.
- **Each comes in four coats**, and the month decides which one is met. The
  coat a catch was met in stays with it.
- **Turning is their own.** Their Normal moves take their coat's type and hit
  1.2x: Grass in spring, Fire in summer, Ground in autumn and Ice in winter. A
  move that was already that type gains nothing.
- **Sawsbuck can also be born with Fur Coat**, which is new: physical moves land
  on its holder at half damage.

## Emolga

- **Emolga stands in the temperate forest and the woodland** at any hour.
- **Glidewake is its own.** Each move it lands gives it 1 Evasion stage, up to
  2, and any hit on it takes the whole lot back at once.
- **It can also be born** with Motor Drive, Wind Rider or Cheek Pouch.

## The last of Unova's lines

Fourteen species in eight families, all in the dex and in the data.

- **Tirtouga and Carracosta**, the fossil turtle, and **Archen and Archeops**,
  the first bird.
- **Karrablast and Escavalier, and Shelmet and Accelgor.** The two lines trade
  with each other: hand a Karrablast over while a Shelmet comes the other way,
  and one takes the shell the other loses.
- **Foongus and Amoonguss**, the mushrooms that grew into the shape of a dropped
  ball.
- **Frillish and Jellicent**, the jellyfish that drift under ships.
- **Stunfisk**, flat in the mud, and **Bouffalant**, which settles everything
  head first.

**None of the fourteen is out in the world yet.** No biome stages any of them,
and the two Unova fossils are held back with them.

## Eight signature abilities

- **Deep Hold**, for the Tirtouga family. The first hit that would take it under
  1/2 HP leaves it there instead, and raises its Defense 2 stages.
- **Featherstone**, for the Archen family. The first time it falls under 1/2 HP
  it heals 1/4 of its HP, once a battle. That is the mark Defeatist reads.
- **Shell Thief**, for the Karrablast family. The first physical move it lands
  on each enemy takes 1 Defense stage off them and puts it on itself.
- **Barehide**, for the Shelmet family. It refuses every Defense drop from
  anybody else, and gains 1 stage of Special Defense instead.
- **Sporeburst**, for the Foongus family. Fainting casts Stun Spore at every
  enemy on the field.
- **Still Water**, for the Frillish family. While it stands, every enemy takes
  1.2x as long to cast. Cooldowns are untouched.
- **Shockmud**, for Stunfisk. Its Ground moves hit 1.2x and paralyse 20% of the
  time.
- **Herd Bond**, for Bouffalant. Its Attack rises 10% for each other pokemon on
  its team still standing.

**Karrablast and Shelmet are a pair.** One takes a guard off whatever it
strikes, and the other refuses to give one up.

## What else they can be born with

A Carracosta can be born with Battle Armor, an Archeops with Rock Head, Sheer
Force or Big Pecks, an Amoonguss with Rain Dish or Overcoat, a Jellicent with
Liquid Ooze, a Stunfisk with Lightning Rod and a Bouffalant with Rock Head.

## Where to look first

- **Temperate forest and woodland**, for Emolga.
