Signature abilities for the next four Johto families:

- Resonance (Yanma): its sound moves reach every enemy on the field, not only the one it aimed at.
- Contagious Yawn (Wooper): it casts Yawn at an enemy as it arrives on the field.
- Magpie (Murkrow): any held item taken off somebody else on the field goes into its empty hands.
- Shared Misery (Misdreavus): the first time it drops below 1/3 of its HP it casts Pain Split at the healthiest enemy.
